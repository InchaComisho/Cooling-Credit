# Episode 57: The Night of Four Locations

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 57 begins the “four-location operation,” stepping into the core of the shared cooling line arc.  
The temperature of the medical delivery container, the waiting station ventilation, Venue Mist V-3A, and the old waiting room’s underground wiring room. Yuto and the others split up across their respective locations and try to protect the line on the brink of failure.

---

## Main Text

The warning alarm in the night delivery waiting station would not stop.

Beep.  
Beep.  
Beep.

“Shared cooling line load: 87%”  
“Temporary storage space: 7.8°C”  
“Venue Mist V-3A: 24%”  
“Medical delivery container: temperature rising”

Under the red emergency lights, Yuto looked at his companions.

Haru.  
Nagi.  
Sōma.  
Katase.

Everyone was tired.  
But no one intended to back down here.

“We move in four locations.”

Yuto said.

“Here, at the waiting station and temporary storage space.  
The venue’s V-3A.  
The old waiting room’s underground wiring room.  
Unless we watch all of them at the same time, we can’t protect the line.”

“Who goes where?”

Katase asked.

Yuto had already organized it in his head.

“Katase-san and Sōma-san, please stay here.  
Your role is to protect the medical delivery container and the safety of the delivery workers.”

“Me and Sōma?”

Sōma asked.

“Yes.  
Sōma-san can speak to the people on-site.  
Katase-san can judge cargo priorities and responsibility.”

Sōma hesitated for only a moment.

Then he immediately nodded.

“Got it.  
I’ll watch the red container.  
I won’t let people or carts get too close.”

“Haru, go to the venue.  
I want you to confirm on-site whether V-3A is really operating.”

“By myself?”

“The venue is closed.  
Before touching any equipment, we need someone who can first check the safety of the surroundings.”

“Understood.”

“Nagi-san and I will go to the old waiting room’s underground wiring room.”

Nagi adjusted her grip on the light.

“To check the master unit.  
But we look for a way to switch it, not stop it.”

“I don’t know what that ‘switching’ means.”

Katase said.

“By the time we understand it, it may be too late.”

“That’s why we hurry.”

Yuto answered.

“But we won’t break it by rushing.”

At that moment, a new display appeared on his phone.

“Cooperative work confirmed”  
“Four-location coordination bonus: active”

“Limited challenge: final emergency condition  
Progress: 72%”

And beneath the blue gauge, a number appeared.

“Additional estimated reward upon success: 0.150 CC”

Yuto looked at the screen for only an instant.

0.150 CC.

The largest additional reward he had seen so far.

It would push him beyond a cumulative 1.0 CC.  
He could see what lay beyond it.

But he could also see the red container display.

“Medical delivery container: 8.6°C”

“There’s no time. Let’s go!”

◇

Haru headed to the venue.

The nighttime flea market venue looked like a different place from daytime.

The stalls had been cleared away.  
The tents were folded.  
Only streetlights fell across the empty parking lot.

At its center, mist was coming out.

Shhhhh…

Into a place with no one there.

Cold mist vanished into the air.

“…It’s running for nothing.”

Haru adjusted his reflective vest.

He checked the surroundings.

No people.  
No cars.  
Only the nozzles at the center of the venue were spraying at a steady rhythm.

A display appeared on his terminal.

“V-3A: 24%”  
“External control conflict: continuing”

“On-site confirmation.  
V-3A is operating. No people present.”

Haru reported over the line.

“Understood.”

Yuto’s voice returned.

“Is there a manual control box near the nozzles?”

“There is.  
But it’s sealed.  
I won’t touch it for now.”

“Please.  
We’ll decide after seeing the master unit’s information.”

“Got it.”

Haru stood beneath the mist.

It was cool.

But no one was there.

Beyond that cold air, an old sign at the venue swayed.

“You can rest here.”

Right now, no one was resting there.

“Cooling a place with no people,  
while the place with people is in danger… That’s the worst.”

Haru muttered quietly.

◇

The old waiting room.

Yuto and Nagi stood in the back of the machine room.

Beside the blue toolbox.  
The old control panel.  
And a seam in the floor that had not been visible until the day before.

“Old maintenance space  
Underground wiring room”

Nagi shone the light on it.

“Here.”

There was a small metal handle in the floor.

Yuto pulled it.

Heavy.

“It won’t open.”

“There’s a lock on the side.”

Nagi pointed to the piping along the wall.

An old label.

“Maintenance door lock  
Administrator key”

“Another key.”

Yuto said.

However, the blue toolbox they had found earlier had contained three keys.

Waiting Room M-1.  
Venue Mist V-3.  
Delivery Relay D-2.

None of them fit.

“There’s no administrator key.”

Nagi said.

“Then what do we do?”

Yuto looked at the control panel.

Administrator ID 00.

The elderly man from daytime.  
The person who built the shared cooling line.

That voice knew something.

“Can you hear me?”

Yuto spoke toward the control panel speaker.

“We need to enter the underground wiring room.  
We need to check the master unit.”

Noise.

Bzz…

No reply.

“Please.  
There’s a medical delivery container at the waiting station.  
The venue mist is running even though no one is there.”

Several seconds.

Then a low voice came through the speaker.

“…The key was given to you from the beginning.”

“What?”

“Look at the bottom of the toolbox.”

Nagi pulled the blue toolbox closer.

She took out the tools once more.

An old wrench.  
Gloves.  
An inspection sheet.  
The ring of keys.

Beneath them, there was another thin iron plate.

“A false bottom.”

Nagi lifted the iron plate.

Inside was a small black key.

“Admin M-0”

“M-0…”

Yuto picked up the key.

“It was here from the beginning.”

“I was watching to see whether the one who used it  
was merely someone in a hurry,  
or someone who listened to people.”

The voice from the speaker.

“Watching… Where are you?”

There was no answer.

Only a faint coughing sound came from beyond the speaker.

Yuto grew uneasy.

“Aren’t you at the hospital…”

“Look at the master unit now.”

The voice grew a little stronger.

“Do not stop it.  
Return its use to the proper places.”

Yuto inserted the black key.

Click.

The floor lock disengaged.

Creak…

The floor hatch opened.

A narrow staircase leading downward.

Cold air blew up from below.

“Can you go?”

Nagi asked.

“Let’s go.”

Yuto and Nagi descended underground.

◇

The underground wiring room was larger than expected.

Cables covered an entire wall.  
Old terminal boards.  
Thick pipes.  
Blinking lamps.

At the center stood a black box-like device.

“Shared Cooling Line  
Central Control Master Unit”

Yuto caught his breath.

“This is the master unit…”

The master unit’s screen displayed three bases.

“Old Waiting Room M-1”  
“Venue Mist V-3”  
“Delivery Relay D-2”

And one more.

“Unregistered terminal: external connection active”

“Unregistered terminal.”

Nagi said.

“Is this the external operator?”

Connection history was listed below the screen.

“20:12 V-3A output change request”  
“20:21 waiting station lighting system access”  
“20:26 emergency ventilation lock request”  
“20:34 V-3A output increase request”

“It really was intentional.”

Yuto clenched his fist.

“Someone is raising the load in order, putting the waiting station in danger.”

“But from here, we may be able to disconnect the unregistered terminal.”

Nagi looked at the operation panel.

Two options appeared on the screen.

“Force disconnect unregistered terminal”  
“Isolate unregistered terminal and redistribute control route”

“If we force disconnect it, will it be over?”

“I don’t know.  
If the external connection suddenly cuts off, V-3A may keep its last instruction.”

“What about isolating it and redistributing the route?”

“It removes the unregistered terminal while returning control of each base to the master unit side.  
But that may expose loads that had been hidden until now.”

Yuto remembered the anonymous voice.

“Do not stop the master unit.  
‘Switch it.’”

“We isolate it and redistribute.”

Yuto said.

“Minimize cooling at the unmanned venue,  
and return priority to the waiting station where people are, and to the medical container.”

“For that, we need information from each site.”

“I’ll connect communications.”

Yuto called out to everyone.

“Haru, watch the venue’s V-3A and tell us whether the output drops.”

“Understood.”

“Katase-san, the temperature in temporary storage.  
Sōma-san, the medical container reading.”

“Got it.”

“It’s 8.9 degrees now!  
If it goes over nine, it’s dangerous!”

Sōma’s voice was tense.

“Nagi-san, operate it.”

“Wait.  
The master unit redistribution requires us to set each base’s ‘priority.’”

Four sliders appeared on the screen.

“Human occupancy”  
“Medical and refrigerated transport”  
“Venue equipment protection”  
“Unregistered terminal isolation”

The priorities had to total 100%.

“How do we split it?”

Nagi asked.

There was no time.

Yuto pictured the delivery workers in the waiting station.  
The red medical container.  
The mist in the unmanned venue.  
And the unregistered terminal whose identity was still unknown.

“Medical and refrigerated transport first.”

“What percentage?”

“Forty-five.”

“Human occupancy?”

“Thirty-five.”

“Venue equipment protection?”

“The minimum. Five.”

“The remaining fifteen goes to unregistered terminal isolation.”

Nagi operated the screen.

“Medical and refrigerated transport: 45%”  
“Human occupancy: 35%”  
“Venue equipment protection: 5%”  
“Unregistered terminal isolation: 15%”

“I’m executing it.”

Yuto closed his eyes once.

“Please.”

Nagi pressed the button.

“Redistribute control route?”

“Execute.”

The device in the underground wiring room groaned loudly.

Gooooooon…

The lamps changed from red to yellow, then from yellow to blue.

But at the same time, the cables on the ceiling began to shake violently.

Crack!

“Whoa!”

Sparks scattered.

Yuto and Nagi instinctively stepped back.

“Control route redistribution: in progress”  
“Unregistered terminal: isolation processing”  
“V-3A: output reduction request”

“Haru, how is it?”

Haru’s voice came through the connection.

“The mist is… getting weaker!  
From 24%, to 18… 14…!”

“Katase-san!”

“Temporary storage, 8.3 degrees!  
It’s starting to go down!”

“Sōma-san!”

“Medical container, 8.7!  
It stopped rising!”

“Good…!”

A little strength left Yuto’s body.

But then, a final warning appeared on the master unit’s screen.

“Unregistered terminal: isolation failed”

“Reason: unregistered terminal is not external”

“Not external?”

Nagi moved closer to the screen.

“Connection source: inside old waiting room underground wiring room”

The room went silent.

Yuto and Nagi turned around at the same time.

At the far end of the underground wiring room.

In the shadow of the cables, there was another small door.

They had not noticed it until now.

“Old Control Seat  
No Entry”

From beyond it.

Click.

A small sound, like a keyboard being struck.

And then a familiar voice echoed through the door.

“…So you made it this far, Soma Yuto.”

Yuto held his breath.

It was the voice of the elderly man who should have been carried away by ambulance during the day.

## Afterword

In Episode 57, Yuto and the others split across four locations to pursue the root cause of the shared cooling line trouble, reaching the central control master unit in the old waiting room’s underground wiring room.

The key points this time were as follows.

- Katase and Sōma handled the waiting station and medical delivery container, Haru monitored Venue Mist V-3A, and Yuto and Nagi headed to the underground wiring room
- They discovered administrator key M-0 in the false bottom of the toolbox and succeeded in entering the underground wiring room
- The shared cooling line’s central control master unit had an “unregistered terminal” connected to it, which had been sending unauthorized controls in sequence to the venue mist, lighting, and emergency ventilation
- Yuto selected priority allocation of 45% to medical and refrigerated transport, 35% to human occupancy, 5% to venue equipment protection, and 15% to unregistered terminal isolation
- As a result, V-3A output decreased, and the temperatures of the temporary storage space and medical delivery container began to improve
- However, the unregistered terminal turned out not to be external, but inside the same underground wiring room
- From the “old control seat” at the back, they heard the voice of the elderly man who had been taken away by ambulance

Yuto and the others are beginning to succeed in redistributing the line in order to protect it.  
However, the person who has guided them through anonymous messages and audio may actually be underground beneath the old waiting room. Was he helping as an ally, or was he the one destabilizing the line? The truth is still unclear.

In the next episode, Yuto directly faces the person beyond the old control seat door.  
Why did the person who built the shared cooling line throw his own system into confusion? And what final “switch” must Yuto choose to protect both the medical delivery container and the night delivery waiting station? The answers will begin to emerge.
