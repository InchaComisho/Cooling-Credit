# Episode 49: The 18-Minute Cooling Line

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 49 is the episode with the strictest time limit in Part 4: only 18 minutes remain until the shared cooling line overloads.  
Should they stop the cool mist, or keep it running despite the danger? Yuto searches for a way to both “cool” and “protect.”

---

## Main Text

There were 18 minutes left until the shared cooling line overloaded.

At the edge of the flea market venue, the mist that had suddenly started up was spreading white through the air.

Shhhhh…

The hot air softened just a little.

“It’s cool!”

A child’s voice rose.

People who had been standing in places with little shade began gathering near the mist.

“This really helps.”

“It was so hot until just now…”

If they looked only at that scene, it was a success.

But red letters were blinking on Yuto’s phone.

“Shared cooling line: unauthorized operation detected”  
“Time until overload: 17 minutes 32 seconds”

“Should we stop it?”

Haru looked back and forth between the screen and the mist.

“But if we stop it here, people will all move back into the sun at once.”

“If we don’t stop it, everything may stop.”

Nagi said.

With a thermometer in one hand, she was checking the branch in the piping.

“Overload doesn’t mean only the waiting room’s equipment.  
It may affect every cooling function connected to the shared line.”

Sōma crossed his arms irritably.

“Then we cut the line.  
Stop only the mist that’s running right now.”

“Where?”

Haru asked.

“A valve or the control panel, right?”

“Have you forgotten what you did in the machine room?”

Sōma’s expression stiffened.

“…I know.”

Yuto stared at the cooling line map.

The old waiting room.  
The external cooling unit.  
The mist at the flea market venue.  
And an unknown connection line extending farther beyond them.

There were three branches.

One was the waiting room.  
One was the venue.  
And the last one was—gray on the map.

“Connection destination unconfirmed”

“This gray line…”

Yuto enlarged the screen with his finger.

The line extended to the opposite side of the venue, toward a cluster of old warehouses.

“Is the load high because this is operating too?”

“We don’t have time to confirm it.”

Haru answered immediately.

“Time until overload: 16 minutes 48 seconds”

Minato approached while operating her terminal.

“We are continuing to contact the management company and the warehouse company.  
However, confirming the management authority required for remote shutdown will take time.”

“We don’t have time.”

Yuto said.

“Can we lower the output only on the venue side?”

“It cannot be operated from the site.”

“Then we reduce how much is being used.”

Nagi said.

Yuto turned toward her.

“How much is being used?”

“All of the mist nozzles are operating the same way.  
But people are gathered mostly in the center.  
There’s no need to run even the edge nozzles at full power.”

“Not stop it, but throttle it?”

“I don’t know whether it can be done physically.  
But if we narrow only part of the water mist nozzle openings, the discharge amount should drop.”

“Wouldn’t it be dangerous to touch them without permission?”

Sōma said.

“It’s on the water outlet side.  
It can be checked more safely than the machine room control panel.”

“But if we block the nozzles, the pressure might rise instead.”

Haru pointed out.

Everyone fell silent.

Simply throttling it was not enough.

“…Wait.”

Yuto remembered the words of the old man from the old waiting room the day before.

“After night shifts.  
People with children.  
People carrying luggage.  
On hot days, and on cold days too…”

The waiting room had once been a place where anyone could rest.

Then the cooling line probably had not been a device meant to keep cooling at full power without stopping.

“There was an old notice inside the waiting room, right?”

Yuto asked Minato.

“The notice about usage hours?”

“Yes.  
Was there any operation memo there?  
Something showing how people used it in the past.”

Minato immediately searched her terminal.

“…There are operation records attached to the old equipment diagram.”

“Can you read them?”

“Only partially.  
The paper is deteriorated, so only limited sections were restored.”

Old text appeared on Minato’s screen.

“Avoid continuous operation during high temperatures”  
“Basic operation: 15 minutes running, 5 minutes resting”  
“When the waiting room is in use, switch external mist to low output”

Yuto held his breath.

“This is it.”

“Alternating operation…”

Nagi said.

“They didn’t run the waiting room’s indoor cooling and the outdoor mist at maximum output at the same time.  
That was the old operation method.”

Haru looked at the map.

“But the waiting room still can’t be officially used.”

“We don’t have to open the inside right now.  
We create shade in front of the door and make it a short-term waiting place.  
During that time, we rest the mist for five minutes.”

“If we stop the mist, people will complain.”

Sōma said.

“That’s why we won’t just stop it.”

Yuto began running toward the center of the venue.

“We make the ‘next place to cool off’ first!”

“Hey, Yuto!”

Haru and the others followed him.

Remaining time: 14 minutes 03 seconds.

Yuto rushed over to the venue manager.

“Excuse me!  
We need to stop the mist for just five minutes!”

“What? Everyone’s using it right now.”

“I know.  
But if we keep running it at full power like this, the line itself may stop.”

“Stop?”

“Not only the venue.  
The waiting room cooling and other places too.”

The manager looked at the people gathered under the mist.

“Then what are we supposed to do?”

“We’ll make the area under the waiting room roof a five-minute rest waiting spot.  
They can’t go inside yet, but there’s shade in front of the door.  
I want to borrow simple chairs and sheets from the vendors.”

“In that short a time?”

“We’ll do it.”

Yuto answered immediately.

“There are people here who can move right now.”

Haru immediately ran toward the venue’s equipment storage area.

“I’ll bring chairs!”

“I’ll make the sign.”

Nagi received cardboard and a thick marker.

Sōma hesitated for a moment.

Then he turned toward the people near the mist.

“I’ll move the people.  
…But if they complain, help me.”

“Of course.”

Yuto said.

A five-minute rest.

It sounded short, but for users it was long.

Without an explanation, it would become dissatisfaction.  
Without an alternative place, it would simply become heat.

That was why Yuto and the others moved before stopping it.

Under the waiting room roof, they placed six folding chairs.  
They spread a blue sheet borrowed from a vendor along the wall.  
The vintage clothing shop owner brought over a small fan used for displaying products.

“Can this use an outlet?”

“With an outdoor extension cord!”

The manager pulled over a cord.

Nagi wrote on the cardboard.

“The mist will pause for 5 minutes  
You can rest here  
This adjustment protects the next cooling”

“You wrote ‘protects,’ didn’t you?”

Yuto said.

“We need to show the reason for stopping it properly.”

Nagi set down the pen.

“We’re not asking them to endure.  
We’re making it so they can use it next time too.”

Sōma raised his voice near the mist.

“Excuse me!  
This mist will be adjusted for only five minutes!  
We’ve made a rest area under the waiting room roof!”

“Aww, it’s stopping?”

A child’s voice.

“Will it start again soon?”

Someone else asked.

Sōma stumbled a little, but answered.

“It will restart.  
That’s why we’re doing this now, so everything doesn’t stop.”

His words were strangely straightforward.

Little by little, the flow of people began moving toward the waiting room.

Yuto looked at the manager’s terminal.

“Can you pause the mist?”

“With manual water shutoff, we can do it from the outside valve.  
But restarting it will also be manual.”

“Please.”

The manager nodded.

Click.

The valve turned.

The shhhhh sound slowly weakened.

The white mist disappeared.

At the same time, a number appeared on Yuto’s phone.

“Shared cooling line load: 92%”

“It went down!”

Haru shouted.

But it was still high.

“Time until overload: 8 minutes 21 seconds”

“Even after stopping it for five minutes, it’s still dangerous?”

Yuto stared at the screen.

Nagi measured the temperature of the piping.

“The venue side is dropping.  
But the gray connection destination is still operating.”

“The warehouse side?”

Haru said.

“Is someone using it?”

Sōma peered at the map.

“Wait.  
The end of the gray line… isn’t the warehouse.”

“What?”

“This position isn’t behind the warehouse.  
It’s even farther, beyond the road.”

Yuto enlarged the screen.

At the end of the line, a faint building name appeared.

“Former Night Delivery Waiting Station”

“Night delivery waiting station…”

Yuto was caught by that name.

The outskirts of town.  
Few places to rest.  
A place that, just like the old waiting room, had once been used by someone.

And immediately after that, Minato’s terminal rang.

“We have contact from the management company.”

Everyone turned toward her.

“The shared cooling line’s connection destinations include not only the old waiting room and the venue, but also the night delivery waiting station.”

“Is it still being used?”

“…Yes.”

Minato’s face stiffened slightly.

“And from tonight, a temporary large-scale delivery shift is scheduled to begin there.”

“Tonight?”

Haru said.

“Then if that place stops…”

“The delivery workers on the night shift may lose a cooling place where they can rest.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Shared cooling line load: 89%”  
“Next priority target detected”

“Night Delivery Waiting Station”

New notifications overlapped on the screen.

“Limited challenge: second condition unlocked”  
“After reconnecting the old waiting room, confirm the load at the night delivery waiting station”

Below that, a small reward estimate appeared.

“Additional estimated reward: 0.100 CC”

Yuto held his breath as he stared at the screen.

0.100 CC.

It was the largest number so far.

But the remaining time was still decreasing.

“Time until overload: 6 minutes 57 seconds”

Complete the old waiting room.  
Protect the venue’s cooling.  
And then head to tonight’s delivery waiting station.

A path far too large suddenly opened before Yuto.

## Afterword

Episode 49 depicted Yuto and the others responding to the shared cooling line overload not with the binary choice of “stop it or keep it going,” but by creating a third method: alternating operation and a temporary rest area.

The key points this time were as follows.

- They discover from old equipment operation records a past system of “15 minutes running, 5 minutes resting”
- Before stopping the mist, they create an alternative rest area under the waiting room roof
- Haru, Nagi, and Sōma each move in their own roles and function as an improvised team
- It is revealed that beyond the shared cooling line lies a new base: the night delivery waiting station

Yuto did not run the equipment simply to earn CC.  
Instead, he made the decision to “stop it so it can be used again next time.” As a result, the possibility of a much larger additional reward, 0.100 CC, opens through the night delivery waiting station.

However, the shared cooling line in front of them is still not completely safe.  
Next time, the focus will be how to reconnect the old waiting room’s cooling with only a little time remaining, and who will head to the delivery waiting station tonight.
