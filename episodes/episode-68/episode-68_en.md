# Episode 68 — Two Minutes of Priorities

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 68 begins with the two-minute choice FROST-9 presents to Yuto: restore Point D's local cooling to 30%, or keep it at 8% in order to protect the port-wide cooling reserve.  
Yuto is forced to make a wide-area decision between the immediate relief of the people in front of him and the stability that protects people he cannot see.

---

## Main Story

『Wide-Area Assignment — Emergency Choice』
『Selection Deadline: 00:01:59』

『A: Restore Point D local cooling to 30%』
『B: Prioritize port-wide reserve and maintain 8%』

North Pier Seven, Point D.

A weak mist drifted faintly toward the back of the roofed area.

Hissss...

Output was at 8%.

The white spray was barely visible.
But it was not zero.

Roof.
Sea breeze.
Water.
Cooling towels.
Ice packs.

With a collection of small measures, Point D was somehow still holding its users.

But it was hot.

A man in work clothes wrung out the towel around his neck.
An elderly woman sat on a bench with her eyes closed.
A mother pressed an ice pack to her child's forehead.

FROST-9 held the silver portable control unit.

“With this override authority, I can return Point D's mist to thirty percent.”

Yuto listened in silence.

“Rest Station No. 2 is about ten minutes from recovery. For those ten minutes, the people here could be far more comfortable than they are now.”

“But the reserve for the whole port would fall.”

“Yes.”

FROST-9 did not hesitate.

“Probably from nine percent to around six.”

Six percent.

Still not zero, as a number.

But Yuto knew what could happen.

When the shared cooling line had fallen to four percent, linked systems had become unstable.
Ventilation had nearly stopped.
Lights had gone out.
Human safety had almost been pushed outside the numbers.

“Yuto,” Haru said.

“Don't rush the answer. You have two minutes.”

“Only two minutes.”

“That's why you look.”

Haru pointed toward the port.

“Don't look only at Point D.”

Yuto opened the cooling map of North Pier Seven.

Point A — Rest Station No. 3.
Point B — Water Station for Waiting Vehicles.
Point C — Port Perimeter Shaded Bench Zone.
Point D — Residential-Edge Emergency Refuge Space.

Each point was shown in a different color.

『Point A: Yellow』
『Point B: Yellow』
『Point C: Blue』
『Point D: Yellow』

Beyond them:

『Main Cooling Tower: Yellow』
『Wide-Area Reserve: 9%』

“Point C is blue,” Yuto said.

“The shaded bench zone?”

MIST CROW checked his tablet.

“Usage is low right now. It's on the outer port walkway. Shift change isn't happening, so almost nobody is there.”

“What output is it using?”

“Eighteen percent.”

“Eighteen?”

Yuto enlarged the map.

Point C was a shaded bench zone.
There was no roof, but the warehouse wall cast a long shadow.
In the afternoon the shade would grow.
Right now, however, it was still morning.

If usage was low...

“Can we reduce C?” Yuto asked.

FROST-9 did not answer immediately.

“Reduce C and raise D.”

“Not to zero. C has shade right now. Lower it from eighteen to ten, and raise D from eight to sixteen. We can't bring D back to thirty, but we can make it easier here.”

“Then there would be no reason to use the main-tower override,” MIST CROW said.

“The override is meant to increase available output across the area.”

“If we don't need it, we don't have to use it,” Yuto answered.

“What we need right now isn't to restore only D to thirty percent. We need to move cooling that isn't being used now to a place that is being used now.”

FROST-9 looked directly at him.

“If Point C is reduced, users on the perimeter route will be affected.”

“Then we'll check whether anyone is there.”

Yuto opened the live feed for Point C.

Six benches.
Only one was occupied.
A man who looked like a delivery worker was drinking something.
The other benches were empty.
Shade covered about half the seating area.

“One person,” Yuto said.

“Only one.”

“There may be people outside the camera.”

“That's why I'll ask.”

Yuto connected to Point C's staff.

“This is Yuto Soma at Point D. Can you confirm the number of users at Point C and whether anyone is struggling physically?”

A brief pause.

『Point C Staff:
Two users. One on a bench, one in the shade beside the walkway. Both are hydrating. No urgent condition apparent.』

“If output drops from eighteen to ten, can you handle it?”

『Yes, if we guide them to the shaded benches. But we could not do that in the afternoon.』

“Please do it for eight minutes only.”

『Understood.』

MIST CROW spoke.

“Remote output changes across another point require management approval.”

FROST-9 looked at the silver control unit.

“I'll approve it.”

“FROST-9...”

“It isn't final yet,” she said to Yuto.

“C goes to ten. D goes to sixteen. If the port-wide reserve falls below eight percent, D returns to eight.”

“Understood.”

“And if Rest Station No. 2 does not recover, this arrangement is not extended.”

“I understand.”

A confirmation screen appeared on Yuto's phone.

『Wide-Area Fine Adjustment Proposal』

『Point C: 18% → 10%』
『Point D: 8% → 16%』
『Estimated Load Impact: +1.1%』
『Projected Wide-Area Reserve: 7.9%』

『Execute?』

Forty-seven seconds remained.

Yuto looked at the users at Point D.
He also pictured the two people at Point C.

Neither point would be reduced to zero.

Move only what was needed, only while it was needed.

“Execute.”

Yuto pressed the button.
FROST-9 approved it on the portable controller.

Beep.

The North Pier Seven map slowly changed.

『Point C: 18% → 15% → 12% → 10%』
『Point D: 8% → 11% → 14% → 16%』

A little more visible mist came from Point D's nozzle.

Hissss...

The cooling was clearly stronger than before.

The mother with the child looked relieved.

“The breeze is back.”

The elderly woman opened her eyes.

“This much makes a difference.”

The man in work clothes let the mist reach his neck.

“It doesn't have to be full power to help.”

『Wide-Area Reserve: 9% → 8.3% → 8.0%』

“Eight percent remains,” Nagi said over comms.

“The main cooling tower temperature isn't rising too sharply either.”

Yuto exhaled.

But FROST-9's expression did not change.

“It isn't over.”

“Rest Station No. 2 still has to recover.”

“No.”

She looked toward the center of the port.

Container cranes.
Heavy vehicles.
Distant work buildings.

“Point C's output dropped. At that moment, one of its users began moving.”

A new alert appeared on Yuto's phone.

『Point C — User Departure Detected』
『One user moving north along the port perimeter route』

“I thought staff guided them into the shade.”

Point C's staff called in.

『Soma, sorry. One user said he had to return to the north-side work area and left.』

“How is he?”

『He said he was fine, but his walking looked a little unstable.』

Haru responded immediately.

“North side means between Point A and Rest Station No. 2.”

“He'll pass through an area with reduced cooling,” Nagi said.

“And we're still under output restrictions.”

A red dot appeared on Yuto's map.

『Point C User — Heat Stress Rising』

“This is bad,” MIST CROW said.

“Raising D changed behavior at C. If this continues, the wide-area adjustment will be judged a failure.”

“It isn't a failure,” Yuto said.

“If someone moved because of the adjustment, then we send support to where they moved.”

“From where?” FROST-9 asked.

Yuto looked at Point D.

Sixteen percent now.
The users there were a little more comfortable.

But not every person absolutely needed to stay at the current level.

“I'll ask the people here.”

“Again, you're deciding at the site.”

“Yes.”

Yuto turned to the people under the roof.

“Excuse me! Someone on the north-side route may be in trouble from the heat. We're at sixteen percent here now, but if we lower this to fourteen for a few minutes, we may be able to send support toward the route they're using.”

The mother with the child answered first.

“My child is okay now.”

The elderly woman nodded.

“I'm sitting down. Two percent is fine for me.”

The man in work clothes stood.

“I'll go check the north-side route. A worker from the port may be easier for him to talk to.”

“Please don't push yourself.”

“I already drank water before pushing myself.”

The light tone made a few people laugh.

Yuto looked at FROST-9.

“D to fourteen percent. Move the two percent to support the north-side route.”

FROST-9 nodded once.

“Approved.”

But a red warning appeared.

『North-Side Route — No Local Cooling Path』

“No cooling path?”

Yuto's face tightened.

“The cooling line doesn't reach it.”

“Then how...”

Haru's voice came over comms.

『There's an old water post on the north-side route. No cooling unit, but it can provide water and emergency towels.』

“Can we use it?”

『It needs a management key. But I can see the unit number.』

Nagi spoke over him.

『That post is connected to Point B's water line. If we redirect a small amount of Point B's output, we may be able to provide chilled water there.』

“Now we're moving another point,” MIST CROW said.

New options appeared on Yuto's phone.

『Wide-Area Fine Adjustment — Chain Response』

『A: Maintain Point D and handle the north-side route through human guidance only』
『B: Temporarily redirect Point B water output to the north-side route』
『C: Use the main cooling tower temporary override authority』

The map of North Pier Seven flickered red and yellow.

Point D.
Point C.
The north-side route.
Point B.

Every time one point moved, another place felt the effect.

Yuto stared at the display.

A wide-area assignment was not simply a world with larger rewards.

It was a world where even someone far away had to be included in the choice.

## Afterword

In Episode 68, Yuto rejects FROST-9's simple choice between restoring Point D to 30% and maintaining it at 8%. Instead, he proposes a wide-area fine adjustment that redirects part of the underused cooling at Point C.

Key points in this episode:

- Point D remained at 8% while users continued to struggle with the heat, and FROST-9 proposed restoring it to 30% through a temporary main-tower override.
- Yuto noticed that Point C's shaded bench zone was using 18% output despite very low current usage.
- After checking the live feed and confirming conditions with Point C staff, he lowered C from 18% to 10% and raised D from 8% to 16%.
- Point D users became more comfortable while the port-wide cooling reserve remained at 8%.
- However, one user left Point C and began moving through the north-side route, where heat stress started to rise. This showed that even a successful adjustment can change human behavior elsewhere.
- The north-side route has no local cooling path. An old water post may be usable, but chilled water would likely require a temporary diversion from Point B's water line.
- Yuto is now offered a new choice: rely only on human guidance, redirect Point B's water output, or use the main cooling tower override.

This time, Yuto does not simply strengthen Point D. He shifts some of Point C's temporarily unused capacity so that neither location is reduced to zero.  
But wide-area control means that even a small adjustment can change where people move. One user leaving Point C has now entered a route with no cooling coverage.

The next episode focuses on this chain response.  
Should Yuto temporarily redirect Point B's water supply, or use the main cooling tower override? For the first time, he must make a decision that includes a single person who is no longer visible in front of him.
