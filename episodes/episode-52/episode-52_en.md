# Episode 52: The Sealed Lever

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 52 places Yuto and the others before the sealed “air separation damper,” forcing them to judge between on-site responsibility and CC reward.  
A large shipment has arrived, surplus cooling capacity is at 9%, and the lever is marked operation prohibited. With no time to spare, this episode depicts who takes responsibility, and in what order they move.

---

## Main Text

The night delivery waiting station.

Above the door connecting the waiting station and the temporary storage space, there was an old metal lever.

“Air Separation  
Manual Switch”

A red seal tag.

“Operation prohibited except in emergencies”

Below it, a warning from the shared cooling line was blinking.

“Surplus cooling capacity at relay base: 9% remaining”

Outside, the sound of a large truck engine was drawing closer.

Vroooom…

Yuto looked up at the seal tag.

“If we switch this, there’s a possibility we can separate the air between the waiting station and the temporary storage space.”

“A possibility,” Haru said in a low voice.

“It’s old equipment.  
There’s no guarantee it will move, and the moment it switches, another load may appear.”

“But if the large shipment enters like this, the surplus capacity will drop even further.”

Nagi answered while looking at her terminal.

“The waiting station is too cold.  
The temporary storage space still needs cooling.  
If we can separate the air, we can lower the output on the waiting-station side.”

“And if we can do that, we can send capacity toward the cargo that actually needs cooling,” Yuto said.

Katase did not move from in front of the lever.

As the night-shift supervisor, she was being asked to make the decision.

“If I operate it, it stays in the log,” Katase said.

“If something goes wrong with the equipment, responsibility comes to the site.  
If deliveries stop, it won’t end with just me.”

“I understand.”

Yuto nodded.

“That’s why we shouldn’t touch it on our own.”

Sōma looked at Yuto with surprise.

“Then what do we do?  
We don’t have time.”

“We give the supervisor the information she needs to make the decision first.”

Yuto looked at the wall display.

Waiting station: 18.2°C.  
Temporary storage space: 6.8°C.  
Shared cooling line: high load.  
Large shipment arrival: moved ahead of schedule.

“Nagi.  
How high can the waiting-station side go while still letting the delivery workers rest?”

“At least twenty-two degrees.  
Preferably twenty-four.  
The temperature gap with outside will also become smaller.”

“Haru.  
If the damper is switched, can you tell from the piping flow whether the cold air on the waiting-station side has been reduced?”

“I can.  
But as it is now, the flow meter is old and hard to read.”

“Sōma.  
Can you check with Katase how much of the large shipment absolutely requires cooling?”

“Me?”

“You seem good at asking people things.”

“Is that supposed to be a compliment?”

“Please hurry.”

Sōma smiled a little, then turned toward Katase.

“Supervisor.  
Please tell me the priority of the cargo.  
If not everything needs the same temperature, we can separate it.”

Katase looked slightly surprised, then immediately opened her terminal.

“…About thirty percent absolutely requires temperature control.  
The rest can withstand normal warehouse temperature for a short time.”

“Thirty percent,” Yuto repeated.

“Then we don’t have to cool everything the same way.”

Nagi continued.

“If we can move the air separation damper, we may be able to divide not only the waiting station but also the inside of the temporary storage space.”

“Move the cargo by type?”

“Yes.  
Put the items that need temperature control on the stronger cooling side.  
If we stop cooling cargo that doesn’t need it, we can create surplus.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“On-site information collection confirmed”  
“Limited challenge: dialogue evaluation increased”

No number appeared.

But the blue gauge extended slightly.

“Talk, separate, and cool,” Yuto said.

“Instead of moving the lever right away, decide what to do after moving it first.”

Katase stared at the tablet screen.

Outside, the air brake of a large truck sounded.

Pshhh.

It had arrived.

The automatic door of the waiting station opened, and carts loaded with cooling containers began entering one after another.

“Katase!”

A delivery worker shouted.

“The early shipment is here!  
There are twice as many cooling-designated items as usual!”

The display changed.

“Surplus cooling capacity: 6% remaining”

The air froze.

“Six…”

Haru let out a short breath.

“We can’t just keep watching anymore.”

Katase reached for the lever.

But her hand stopped.

“What if the waiting-station output doesn’t drop after switching?”

“We’ll stop it immediately,” Yuto said.

“We’ll all check it.  
But Katase, please make the decision to switch it.”

“Why?”

“Because you’re the one responsible for the people using this place and the cargo being protected here.”

Yuto looked straight at Katase.

“I want to earn CC.  
But it would be wrong for me, someone who doesn’t know this place, to decide on my own and leave only the responsibility behind.”

Katase closed her eyes for a few seconds.

Then she placed her hand on the red seal tag.

Rip.

The sound of the seal tearing echoed strangely loudly.

“I will perform the manual switch.”

Katase’s voice rang through the facility.

“Air separation on the waiting-station side.  
Temperature control takes priority in the temporary storage space.  
Everyone, watch the meters.”

“Understood!”

Haru ran toward the piping.

“I’ll watch the temperature!”

Nagi looked at the waiting-station wall display.

“We’ll separate the cargo!”

Sōma called out to the delivery workers.

“Cooling-designated boxes to the blue container side!  
Everything else over here for now. Keep the aisle clear!”

“Who are you?”

“Helping hands! Hurry!”

Yuto called out to the workers in the waiting station.

“If you’re cold, please move toward the entrance side!  
The setting may change!”

Katase pulled the lever.

Clunk.

For a moment, nothing happened.

Everyone looked at the display.

“Waiting station: 18.2°C”  
“Temporary storage: 6.8°C”  
“Surplus capacity: 6%”

No change.

“Is it not moving?” Sōma said.

“Not yet.”

Haru held his hand near the piping.

“There’s a delay before the flow switches.”

Rattle-rattle-rattle…

Inside the wall, old equipment began to turn.

The direction of the wind from the vent changed.

The cold wind in the waiting station weakened.

“Waiting station: 18.5°C”

“It rose!” Nagi shouted.

“Temporary storage: 6.7°C”

“The storage side is holding!”

Yuto clenched his fist.

“This can work!”

But right after that—

Beep-beep-beep-beep!

A different warning sounded.

“Air separation damper: incomplete switching”  
“Second system: no response”

“Second system?”

Katase’s face turned pale.

“Wait.  
This damper isn’t a single panel.  
There should be another one farther in.”

“Farther in?”

Haru checked the map.

The old equipment diagram displayed two symbols.

“D-1: Waiting-station side”  
“D-2: Loading-bay side”

“If the damper on the loading-bay side is still open, the cold air will escape that way,” Nagi said.

“That’s why the waiting station still isn’t rising enough.”

“Waiting station: 19.1°C”  
“Surplus capacity: 7%”

“It has improved a little.  
But it’s slow.”

From outside, the sound of carts being pushed came closer.

The large shipment was still coming in.

“Surplus cooling capacity: 5% remaining”

Katase said, “D-2 is above the loading bay.  
But cargo intake has already begun.  
Going there now is dangerous.”

“I’ll go,” Sōma said.

“I’ll check with the on-site people and create a safe timing to pass.”

“Don’t go alone,” Haru said.

“I’m going too.”

“But what about here?” Yuto asked.

Haru answered briefly.

“Nagi watches the meters.  
Katase continues the operation.  
You watch the people in the waiting station and the cargo flow.”

“Understood.”

Sōma and Haru ran toward the loading bay.

As Yuto watched their backs, he looked at his phone.

“Joint work confirmed”  
“Team contribution correction: active”

Then, for the first time, it appeared.

“Additional reward multiplier: ×1.4”

“Multiplier…”

Yuto’s heart jumped.

The additional estimated reward was 0.100 CC.  
There was a chance for a multiplier on top of that.

If they won, it would be huge.

A cumulative 1.0 CC came into view.

But the next warning cut through that thought.

“Warning:  
Operation of loading-bay-side D-2  
requires a manual release key.”

Katase held her breath.

“Release key…”

“Where is it?” Yuto asked.

“In the old days, the night-shift supervisor had it.  
But now…”

Katase looked at the key holder on her waist.

There were a card key and several small keys.

But only the key for D-2 was missing.

“…The previous supervisor took it out and never returned it.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

An anonymous message.

“Anonymous:  
The release key is not in the facility.  
It is in ‘that waiting room.’”

Yuto stopped breathing for a moment.

The old waiting room.

The elderly man from earlier in the day had said it.

“The machine room… in the back… Don’t touch it on your own.”

And now, here at the night waiting station, they needed the release key.

The old waiting room and the night delivery waiting station.

Two places that should have been separate had once again been connected by a single line.

“Surplus cooling capacity: 4% remaining”

Yuto stared at the screen.

Should they return to the old waiting room?  
Or should they search for another solution here?

The night deliveries had already begun.

## Afterword

Episode 52 began by asking who should operate the sealed air separation damper, and Yuto and the others executed the switch through the decision of the night-shift supervisor, Katase.

The key points this time were as follows.

- Yuto did not hide that he was aiming for a reward, while returning decision-making authority to the site supervisor
- Switching the damper began raising the waiting-station temperature from 18.2°C to 19.1°C, while maintaining the temperature in the temporary storage space
- Operation began to separate the delivery workers’ rest environment from cargo that required temperature control
- Team coordination activated an additional reward multiplier of ×1.4
- However, the second loading-bay-side damper D-2 did not respond, and a release key became necessary
- For some reason, that release key may be located in the old waiting room from earlier in the day

For Yuto, this challenge, with a multiplier on a 0.100 CC-class reward, is a major foothold for rising upward.  
But to achieve it, he must avoid stopping nighttime logistics, protect the cooling line, and even follow the past connection to the old waiting room.

In the next episode, the focus will be the “two-location night operation” surrounding the release key.  
Will Haru and Sōma head to the dangerous loading-bay side while Yuto follows the clue to the old waiting room’s key? It is unclear whether the remaining 4% surplus capacity will give them any room to breathe.
