# Episode 37: The Atmosphere of the First Day of Operation

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

As the first episode of Part 4, and Episode 37 overall, this episode begins after a gentle design has taken shape and shows the start of its actual operation.  
This time, the story does not end with a system being changed. It begins with Yuto seeing on the front line what happens when that system actually starts being used.

---

## Main Text

Monday.  
Soma Yuto arrived at the station-front plaza a little earlier than usual.

At a glance, it was hard to tell what had changed from the day before.  
The position of the mist.  
The arrangement of the benches.  
The wording on the signs.  
All of them had indeed changed into a “gentler design.”

But what truly began today was not the design itself.  
It was the moment when people began to use it.

“Cooling Commons Daily-Life Support Link  
Operation begins today at 7:00 a.m.  
・You can rest here  
・You can cool down here  
・Proxy use allowed  
・Voice guidance available  
・Routes usable without reading are prioritized”

“…It’s started.”

Yuto murmured softly in front of the sign.

People were already sitting on the benches in front of the station from early morning.  
People before their commute.  
Parents pushing strollers.  
Elderly people.  
Delivery workers.  
Students.

They could sit naturally without reading through anything.  
They could rest naturally.  
That was the difference between yesterday’s “design” and today’s operation.

Then WindArc came running up lightly.

“Good morning.  
It’s the first day, and people are already using it.”

“Morning.  
More people are sitting naturally than I expected.”

“Yes.  
It feels like the use is being communicated before the explanation.”

Yuto nodded as he looked at the row of benches.

“Just writing ‘you can rest here’ really turns it into a place where people feel allowed to sit.”

“That’s right.  
Support does not always require people to do something special.  
Just knowing ‘it is okay to be here’ makes a major difference.”

In a corner of the station-front plaza, voice guidance was playing quietly.

“This is the Cooling Commons Daily-Life Support Link.  
Those who wish to rest may please take a seat.  
If you need assistance, please speak to a nearby guide staff member.”

Even without reading the text, people could understand by listening.  
The mechanism was more settled than Yuto had imagined.

“This should reach elderly people and parents with children more easily.”

“Yes.  
And proxy use is also moving quite well.  
Family members or supporters receive the guidance first, then let the people who need it use the support directly.”

Yuto looked at a small child sitting on a bench and the parent drinking water beside them.

“So even if the person who uses it and the person who understands the guidance are different, it still reaches properly.”

“Exactly.  
That is the result of the ‘design based on people who cannot use it’ that we talked about before.”

Throughout the morning, Yuto walked around the station-front area and the shopping street.

The station-front plaza.  
The shopping street rest space.  
The library entrance.  
The city office lobby.  
The shade near the nursery school.

Everywhere, the same guidance appeared.

“You can rest here.”  
“You can cool down here.”  
“We prioritize routes that can be used without reading.”

Those short phrases were gradually increasing throughout the town.

In front of the library, a student who looked like they were studying for exams was cooling down.  
In the city office lobby, elderly people waiting for documents were sitting.  
In front of the nursery school, parents doing drop-off and pickup were taking a breath on the benches.

“…They have really become places to stay.”

Yuto felt a slight warmth in his chest.

Support had begun with electricity bill subsidies and household cooling.  
But now it had become support for places: you can rest here, you can cool down here, you may sit here.

Before noon, a message came from WindArc.

“WindArc:  
The first-day logs look quite good.  
For now, application-free place-based support is being used the most.”

“I guess support gets used more when the difficult parts are reduced.”

“WindArc:  
Yes.  
The use rate of voice guidance is also high.  
‘Usable without reading’ is having a strong effect.”

“Usable without reading…”

Yuto repeated the words inwardly.

For some people, reading text itself becomes a barrier.  
For some people, an application form becomes a barrier.  
For some people, physical strength itself becomes a barrier.

Being able to use support without crossing those barriers was probably the true entrance to support.

In the afternoon, before returning to his apartment, Yuto sat again on a bench in front of the station.  
The heat was still there.  
But the position of the mist and the direction of the wind were just right, and breathing felt a little easier.

“First-day operation log  
・Application-free place-based support is being used the most  
・Voice guidance use is high  
・Proxy use is functioning without major problems  
・Routes usable without reading are actually increasing what people can use”

As he wrote this, Yuto thought to himself.

“When a system changes,  
it may be this first-day atmosphere that matters more than any big announcement.”

In the evening, he opened his notebook and summarized the day.

“Today’s events  
・As the first day of Part 4, support operation began  
　→The same guidance began being used at the station-front plaza, shopping street, library, city office, and nursery school  
　→Application-free use, proxy use, voice guidance, and routes usable without reading functioned in practice  
・Confirmed the first-day usage situation with WindArc  
　→Application-free place-based support is being used the most  
　→Voice guidance use is also high  
　→‘You can rest here’ has become an actual place to stay”

“Today’s realization  
・A system takes shape not when it is announced, but when it is used  
・Being usable without reading, usable by proxy, and naturally sit-able becomes the true entrance to support  
・Part 4 moves from the story of design to the story of operation, bringing both the strengths and problems of support closer to the field”

Finally, one line.

“Today’s line  
・A gentle design becomes real not on paper, but in the atmosphere of the first day.”

When he checked his phone, his Cooling Credit had increased slightly.

“Earned today: 0.009 CC  
Total: 0.743 CC”

“Part 4 starts here.”

The world was still hot.  
But on the benches in the station-front plaza, more people were able to sit properly.  
The system had begun operating, and Yuto quietly recorded the first atmosphere of that beginning.

## Afterword

As the first episode of Part 4, this episode depicted the first day on which the “gentle design” actually began to operate.  
The key point here is that support was not “completed.” It had begun to be used.

- Application-free place-based support is being used the most
- Voice guidance and proxy use are reaching people who cannot read or cannot apply
- The phrase “you can rest here” is creating actual places to stay

Through this first-day atmosphere, the theme of Part 4 has shifted to system implementation and operation.

In Part 3, we followed the expansion of support, the people left out, and finally the design based on “people who cannot use it.”  
In Part 4, we will follow in greater detail how that design moves in the field, what works well, and what new problems become visible.
