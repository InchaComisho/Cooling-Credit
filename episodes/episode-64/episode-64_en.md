# Episode 64: The Reply from Outside the Rankings

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 64 is the episode where Yuto weighs the rules of upper players against his own collaborative operation at North Seventh Wharf.  
With only a little time left before the complete shutdown decision for location D, Yuto takes the risk of possibly losing his qualification for a major reward and, for the first time, sends conditions back to FROST-9 directly.

---

## Main Text

“Location D: Nearby Residential District Simple Evacuation Space”  
“Time until complete shutdown decision: 00:27:18”

The Water Supply Spot for Waiting Vehicles at North Seventh Wharf.

Yuto stared at his phone.

Replies from his companions lined the collaborative operation chat.

“Haru:  
Heading to A. I’ll check the route to the Second Rest Building.”

“Nagi:  
I’ll build a low-output plan for B. I’ll separate water supply from mist.”

“Sōma:  
I’ll go to D. I’ll ask residents about usage.”

“Katase:  
Contacting port management. I’ll also check entry procedures for collaborators.”

Everyone was already trying to move.

But FROST-9’s warning did not disappear.

“You were supposed to observe alone.”  
“If you bring in collaborators, the preliminary observation pass may be invalidated.”

“…May be, huh.”

Yuto enlarged the map of North Seventh Wharf.

A, B, C, and D.

Visiting four locations alone.

Formally, it might be possible.

But in reality, someone’s shutdown plan would be decided first, and the field would change before he could observe it.

Observe first, then submit an alternative.

That sequence would not make it in time.

“What will you do?”

RIVER-EDGE spoke from a short distance away.

At some point, he had been watching from beside the water spot tent.

“Follow FROST-9’s condition, or call your companions and lose the pass?”

“You were listening?”

“At North Seventh Wharf, information travels faster than wind.”

RIVER-EDGE looked up at the mist nozzle.

“If it were me, I would do it alone.  
Even if you call collaborators and get a proposal through, the location of responsibility becomes blurred.”

“Cooperation does not erase responsibility.”

“Doesn’t it?”

“If anything, when more people know the field, the location of responsibility becomes visible.”

Yuto said:

“I know deciding alone is faster.  
But when that decision is wrong, no one can stop it.”

RIVER-EDGE gave a small laugh.

“That line will not pierce upper-ranked players.”

“That is probably why I’m outside the rankings.”

Yuto opened the reply screen to FROST-9.

His fingers trembled slightly.

5.000 CC.

Official qualification.

The metropolitan rankings.

He did not want to let them go.

But still.

“Yuto:  
There is a limit to what I can observe alone.  
I will check the field with collaborators.  
However, the final proposal will be submitted under my name.  
My collaborators will not touch equipment on their own.  
If that still invalidates my pass, I accept it.”

Send.

It was read immediately.

No reply came.

“Time until location D complete shutdown decision: 00:24:56”

Yuto inhaled.

“Sōma-san!”

“I’m listening!”

“Send me D’s situation right away!”

“I’m at the entrance to the residential district.  
It’s hot. There are people here.  
But MIST CROW has already started explaining the field!”

“Got it. I’m coming too!”

Yuto started running.

◇

He passed along the outer road of North Seventh Wharf.

Rows of large vehicles.  
Worker pathways.  
Asphalt reflecting heat.  
There was sea breeze, but the heat would not leave.

Location D, the simple evacuation space, sat on the boundary between the port and the residential district.

A small public space.

Four roofed benches.  
One water dispenser.  
Two simple mist units.  
An old signboard read:

“Short-Term Evacuation Space During High Temperatures”

More than ten residents had gathered there.

A parent with a child.  
An elderly shopper.  
A student pushing a bicycle.  
A person returning toward the residential side after port work.

And at the center stood someone wearing a black hooded cooling vest.

“MIST CROW”

He was speaking with a tablet in one hand.

“I will repeat this.  
This evacuation space will be fully shut down from noon.  
As an alternative, we will guide people to the community center in the middle of the residential district.”

“That’s far.”

An elderly woman said.

“It takes fifteen minutes to walk, doesn’t it?”

“During the day, patrol vehicles will also be sent.”

“Will they come every time?”

“They will be adjusted according to demand.”

MIST CROW’s tone was calm.

He was not shouting.  
He was not looking down on anyone.

But the phrase “according to demand” caught in Yuto’s mind.

They would move after people came.

Then what would happen to the people who could not come?

“Excuse me.”

Yuto entered the circle.

“Please let me ask a little.”

MIST CROW looked at Yuto.

“Soma Yuto.”

“Yes.”

“The rookie with FROST-9’s observation pass.”

“I will not deny being a rookie.”

“Then look at the numbers before you interfere with a shutdown proposal.”

MIST CROW held out the tablet.

“Location D average usage rate: 11%”  
“Non-peak usage: low”  
“Cooling efficiency: 42% of wide-area average”  
“Alternative facility: 1.1 km away”

“Instead of preserving this output, sending it to port rest buildings and medical transport will save more people.”

Yuto looked at the numbers.

It was hard to argue.

If efficiency alone mattered, complete shutdown was correct.

“Usage rate 11%?”

“Yes.”

“But there are more than ten people here today.”

“Because this is a peak.”

“And for the people who need that peak?”

“We send patrol vehicles.”

“When?”

“After demand is detected.”

“What if someone collapses before they are detected?”

MIST CROW’s gaze shifted slightly.

“Do not judge by emotion.”

“This is not emotion.  
Please include the people who cannot reach the alternative in the calculation.”

Yuto looked outside the evacuation space.

The slope leading into the residential district.  
A sidewalk with little shade.  
The community center 1.1 km away.

Young people could walk.

But for someone with a cane, a parent with a child, someone carrying bags, or someone coming home after work, it was different.

“Can’t you narrow it by time instead of shutting it down completely?”

“Even with output lowered, maintenance cost remains.”

“Stop the mist, but keep water and the roofed area.  
Or operate only during the afternoon peak.”

“How many people will that save?”

“I will find that out now.”

Yuto looked at Sōma.

“Were you able to ask?”

“I’m asking now!”

Sōma was calling out to the residents.

“Who usually uses this place?  
Even only during hot hours is fine, please tell us!”

MIST CROW looked sharply at Yuto.

“You brought in a collaborator.”

“Yes.”

“The preliminary observation pass may be invalidated.”

“I understand.”

At that moment, Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“FROST-9:  
Collaborator intervention confirmed”

His heart jumped.

“Reassessing handling of observation pass”

“So it came…”

A countdown appeared beneath the message.

“Time until reassessment: 00:09:59”

Meanwhile, the time until location D’s shutdown decision also kept falling.

“Time until location D complete shutdown decision: 00:17:08”

“Nine minutes until we learn whether your qualification disappears, and seventeen until this evacuation place disappears.”

Sōma said.

“That is way too busy.”

“That is why we ask quickly.”

Yuto approached the elderly woman sitting on a bench.

“Excuse me.  
If this place shuts down, can you go to the community center?”

“Not today.”

“Why not?”

“Because there’s a slope.  
If I don’t sit here on the way back, I can’t walk all the way home.”

Another mother spoke.

“My child gets overheated easily.  
On the way home from shopping, we stop here once to calm down before going back.”

A man in work clothes said:

“When I come back from the port, I drink water here.  
On some days, if I don’t rest once before going home, driving is dangerous.”

Yuto entered the numbers.

Number of users.  
Time periods.  
Movement difficulty.  
Distance to the alternative facility.  
Need for short-term use.

Then a remote message came from Nagi.

“Nagi:  
The two mist units at location D account for 67% of power consumption.  
If we keep only roofed airflow, the water dispenser, and guide lights, required power is 33%.”

“Thirty-three percent…”

Not complete shutdown, but a 67% reduction.

With that, maybe.

“Haru:  
The route from A to the Second Rest Building is dangerous for people with movement difficulty.  
There is a large-vehicle crossing.  
A should keep low output near the entrance.”

Collaborative operation information arrived one after another.

Yuto turned back toward MIST CROW.

“Please make location D a 67% reduction instead of complete shutdown.”

“Basis?”

“Stop the two mist units.  
Keep the roof, water, airflow, and guide lights.  
Let nearby residents and people returning from the port use it briefly during peak hours.”

“Power?”

“Thirty-three percent of the current level.”

“User count?”

“More than ten at peak.  
However, some users have difficulty moving 1.1 km to the alternative.  
This is not a long-stay place. It is a place to prevent people from collapsing on the way home or while moving.”

MIST CROW looked at the tablet.

“Even so, the cooling margin that can be sent to the port rest buildings decreases.”

“It decreases compared to complete shutdown.  
But it is not zero.”

“A clean answer.”

“It is the answer from the people on the field.”

At that moment, a low sound came from overhead.

Brrrrr…

A small surveillance drone stopped above the evacuation space.

MIST CROW’s terminal rang.

“Metropolitan Cooling Evaluation Bureau: emergency observation started”

“Evaluation Bureau?”

Sōma looked up into the sky.

A display also appeared on Yuto’s phone.

“Location D alternative operation proposal:  
Third-party real-time evaluation started”

“FROST-9?”

Yuto said.

Immediately after, a message arrived from FROST-9.

“Your collaborative operation is not a rule violation.  
However, it will be evaluated not as observation, but as proposal verification.”

“If it fails, official participation qualification will be invalidated.  
If it succeeds, discretionary participation qualification will be granted.”

“Discretionary…”

Yuto read the screen.

It was not complete freedom.

But it meant he might enter the wide-area project with the discretion to create alternatives on-site.

“MIST CROW-san.”

Yuto said:

“Please let me verify whether this place can remain at thirty-three percent.”

MIST CROW looked at Yuto for a while.

Then he operated his tablet.

“Fifteen minutes.”

“Huh?”

“I will extend the complete shutdown decision by fifteen minutes.  
Within that time, verify mist shutdown, airflow retention, water guidance, and user guidance.”

The screen changed.

“Location D alternative operation verification”  
“Time limit: 00:15:00”

“If verification fails: complete shutdown plan automatically approved”  
“If verification succeeds: 67% reduction plan goes to review”

Yuto’s phone vibrated strongly.

“Priority proposal right: provisionally granted”

The mist in front of him was still spraying white.

In fifteen minutes, it would have to be stopped.

But if the roofed area, water, airflow, and guide lights could remain, and if people could use the place with peace of mind—

Then this place would not have to disappear.

“Sōma-san, user guidance!”

“Leave it to me!”

“Nagi-san, send the output switching procedure remotely!”

“I’m sending it!”

“Haru, can you come to this site?”

“I got the A log. I’m running over now.”

Yuto stood at the entrance of the evacuation space.

The road to 5.000 CC.

A challenge from outside the rankings.

And a fifteen-minute verification.

At North Seventh Wharf, the first way to preserve by reducing began.

## Afterword

In Episode 64, Yuto directly tells FROST-9 that he will choose collaborative operation against the condition that he observe alone.  
Even though he may lose his qualification for the 5.000 CC wide-area project, Yuto chooses to gather information with companions who know the field rather than try to visit all four locations alone.

The key points this time were as follows.

- Yuto told FROST-9 that he would check the field with collaborators and that he would take final responsibility for the proposal under his own name
- At location D, MIST CROW was moving forward with a complete shutdown plan, but users included elderly people, parents with children, and people returning after work who had difficulty reaching the alternative facility
- Through Nagi’s remote check, they learned that the two mist units account for 67% of power consumption, while roofed airflow, water, and guide lights can remain at 33%
- Yuto submitted a 67% reduction plan instead of complete shutdown: stop the mist, but keep airflow, water, and guide lights
- FROST-9 did not treat collaborative operation as a rule violation, but notified him that it would be evaluated as proposal verification
- MIST CROW extended the complete shutdown decision by 15 minutes and gave Yuto a chance to verify the alternative operation
- If it succeeds, he receives discretionary participation qualification; if it fails, the complete shutdown plan is automatically approved

In Episode 64, even while entering upper-player competition, Yuto chose not to abandon the collaborative operation he had built so far.  
In exchange, from this point on, the help of his companions itself will be judged as a result. If the 15-minute verification fails, location D will stop, and Yuto may lose official participation qualification.

The next episode is the 15-minute verification of location D’s alternative operation.  
When the mist stops, can users truly rest with only the roofed area, airflow, and water? Under the eyes of upper players and the evaluation bureau, Yuto must prove for the first time, through results, a “reduction that preserves.”
