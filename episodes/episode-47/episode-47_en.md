# Episode 47: The Voice of the Closed Waiting Room

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 47 moves closer to the truth behind the sound heard from inside the old waiting room.  
As the time limit of the limited challenge keeps shrinking, Yuto learns why the waiting room was closed before he learns how to open the lock.

---

## Main Text

From inside the old waiting room, the sound came again.

Clatter.

This time, it was clearer than before.

Every participant turned toward the closed glass door.

“…Is someone in there?”

The short-haired man stepped closer to the door.

Haru immediately put out an arm to stop him.

“Don’t get too close.  
We can’t see inside. We don’t know the condition of the glass either.”

“Then what do we do?”

“First, we call out.”

Haru kept a little distance from the waiting room.

“Is anyone inside?”

There was no answer.

Only the sound of wind passing through the gap in the window.

Yuto followed.

“If someone is inside, please answer, even just once!  
The door cannot be opened right now!”

A few seconds.

A silence that felt long.

And then—

“…Water.”

A faint voice came back.

Yuto opened his eyes wide.

It was the voice of an elderly man.

“Do you… have water?”

“There’s someone inside!”

Yuto shouted.

The atmosphere changed all at once.

The challenge.  
The reward.  
The key.  
The hidden condition.

All of that was pushed to the back in an instant.

“Minato!”

Yuto turned toward Minato of the regional observation team.

“There’s someone inside. We need rescue!”

“Confirmed.”

Minato was already operating her terminal.

“I will contact the venue first-aid staff and the fire department.  
Everyone, do not forcefully break any windows or doors. We will confirm the condition of the person inside.”

The short-haired man spoke in an irritated voice.

“If we wait, it might be too late!”

“If forced entry causes part of the building to collapse, the situation will become worse.”

Minato’s voice was quiet.

But there was no hesitation in it.

“Please check whether only water can be passed through the gap in the window.”

“I’ll look.”

Haru put on his work gloves and went around to the side of the waiting room.

Yuto followed him.

The upper part of the window was open just slightly.  
On the ground, there was one old wooden box.

Haru checked the strength of the wooden box and placed it against the wall.

“Don’t climb. I’ll go.”

“But it’s dangerous alone.”

“You support it from below.”

Haru put one foot on the wooden box and grabbed the window frame.

“Can you see inside?”

Yuto asked.

“…Hard to see.  
But there is someone.”

Haru’s voice changed slightly.

“He’s collapsed on the bench in the back.  
A man. Seems conscious.”

Through the gap in the window, a weak voice came.

“…Sorry.”

“It’s all right!”

Yuto took out a plastic bottle of water.

“We’ll pass you water. Please wait a moment.”

Haru shook his head.

“It won’t fit like this.  
Maybe just the cap… no, if we drop it, that’s dangerous.”

Yuto looked around.

People preparing the flea market venue.  
Tents.  
Ropes.  
Clips.  
Long poles.

“Please wait.”

Yuto ran.

“Hey, where are you going?”

“I’ll be right back!”

He rushed into the stall preparation area at the edge of the venue.

“Excuse me!  
Could I borrow a thin rope or string, and a long pole?”

The secondhand clothing stall owner looked surprised.

“What happened?”

“There’s someone feeling unwell inside the building.  
I want to pass water through the window!”

“Then use this!”

What the shopkeeper handed him was an extendable pole for hanging merchandise.

Another stall operator handed him a sturdy string.

“Don’t drop the bottle!”

“Thank you!”

Yuto returned, out of breath.

Haru looked back while still clinging to the window frame.

“Slow.”

“Thirty seconds!”

“Then that’s good enough.”

Yuto tied the string around the plastic bottle and fixed it to the end of the pole.

“We’ll push it in slowly with this.”

“Raise the angle.  
Don’t hit the window frame.”

“Got it.”

The two moved carefully together.

The gap in the window.  
The dark interior.  
The old bench.

The tip of the pole slowly entered the room.

“Can you see it?”

Yuto shouted.

“…Yes.”

The man inside answered in a hoarse voice.

“A little more… right.”

“Right, then.”

Haru gave instructions.

A few seconds later.

Click.

A small sound was heard.

It was the sound of the bottle touching someone’s hand.

“He got it!”

Yuto shouted without thinking.

The man inside did not drink immediately.

He slowly opened the cap and took only a little into his mouth.

“…That helps.”

The moment Yuto heard that voice, strength drained from his body.

His phone vibrated.

“Emergency support log confirmed.  
Appropriate coordinated action has been recorded.”

But this time, it was not a CC grant notification.

“Limited challenge: paused  
Reason: safety response prioritized.”

“Paused…”

The short-haired man looked at his screen irritably.

“Does that mean the reward stops too?”

The woman in the fluorescent vest glared at him sharply.

“You’re talking about that now?”

“It’s a four-hour limited challenge.”

“There’s a person collapsed inside.”

A tense atmosphere ran through the group.

Yuto said nothing.

He only listened to the breathing of the man beyond the window.

Before long, the venue first-aid staff arrived.  
Then the fire department rescue team.

The site suddenly became busy.

“Who manages the building?”

“We are contacting them.”

“Is this the only entrance?”

“There is a window that is slightly open.”

The rescue personnel checked the surroundings of the waiting room and carefully examined the condition of the door and windows.

Then one of them said,

“We are able to speak with the person inside.  
We will look for a way to release the lock. Everyone, please step back.”

Yuto and the others moved outside the rope.

From the back of the waiting room, the man said something softly again.

“…This place.”

“Yes?”

Yuto asked from a position that was not too close to the window.

“This place, long ago… anyone could rest here.”

His voice was weak.  
But his words were clear.

“After night shifts.  
With children.  
With heavy bags.  
On hot days, on cold days…”

Yuto listened silently.

“I told them not to close it.  
But they said no one used it anymore…”

The man’s voice broke off.

Yuto looked up at the old waiting room.

This was not merely an abandoned building.

It had once existed so that someone could rest.

And even now, there were people who needed that role.

After a while, one of the rescue workers began checking the old emergency release unit beside the door with a tool.

“…It may open.”

Those words tightened the air around them.

Click.

A dull sound.

The glass door moved just a little.

“It opened!”

The rescue worker pulled the door.

Old air flowed out from inside the waiting room.

Dust.  
Humidity.  
And—an air so cool it was surprising.

“It’s cool?”

Yuto said without thinking.

Haru also frowned.

“Wait.  
Was it true that the electricity was still alive?”

The rescue team entered and carefully carried the man out.

He looked to be in his late sixties.  
He wore a dirty hat and had an old bag hanging from his shoulder.

On the way to the ambulance, he looked toward Yuto.

“You…”

“Yes.”

“The machine room… in the back…”

The man weakly grabbed Yuto’s wrist.

“Don’t… touch it on your own.”

“The machine room?”

“It can cool… but…”

After saying that much, the man closed his eyes.

“Wait, what do you mean?”

But a rescue worker gently moved Yuto back.

“We’ll take it from here.”

The ambulance left the venue with its siren sounding.

The waiting room door was left half open.

Minato sent a notification to everyone’s terminals.

“Limited challenge will resume.  
Remaining time: 2 hours 48 minutes.”

A new item had been added to the screen, one that had not been there before.

“Additional evaluation:  
Confirm the old waiting room’s ‘cooling function.’”

“Cooling function…”

Haru looked toward the back of the waiting room.

At the end of the dark hallway.  
An old door.  
Above it, barely readable letters remained.

“Machine Room”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Hidden evaluation condition unlocked.  
Reconnecting the cooling function requires an additional choice.”

Two buttons appeared on the screen.

“Prioritize safety check”
“Prioritize reward efficiency”

The remaining time did not stop.

Yuto stared at the dark machine room door.

## Afterword

Episode 47 revealed, through the rescue of the person inside the old waiting room, the building’s hidden past and function.

The key points this time were as follows.

- The person inside was an elderly man who had known the old waiting room in the past
- Yuto and Haru did not force their way in, but cooperated with people on site to deliver water
- Although the challenge was temporarily paused, prioritizing safety opened a new evaluation route
- The waiting room still contained a “cooling function” that might still be alive

For Yuto, this rescue was not an action that directly earned CC.  
However, safely helping someone became, as a result, the entrance to an even larger challenge.

In the next episode, the choice surrounding the old waiting room’s machine room begins.  
If he prioritizes the time limit and reward, he may be able to greatly increase his CC. But if he rushes, there may be danger. For the first time, Yuto is clearly forced to choose between “safety” and “reward efficiency.”
