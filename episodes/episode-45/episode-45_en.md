# Episode 45: The Safety-First Choice

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 45 follows directly from the emergency in Episode 44, and it is the first episode where Yuto is forced to make an immediate decision between “earning” and “protecting safety.”  
Instead of rushing forward on momentum, a choice that feels like Yuto draws a new evaluation and an unexpected next stage toward him.

---

## Main Text

The reversing sound of the delivery vehicle echoed through the loading space.

Beep. Beep. Beep.

The child’s sandal was under the pallet.  
The mother stood there pale, holding the child’s hand.

“Mom, that…”

“It’s okay. It’s okay.”

Even as she said that, the mother’s voice was trembling.

On Yuto’s phone, an unfamiliar screen was displayed.

“Emergency event detected.  
Safety-priority support: select an immediate action.”

There were three choices.

“A: Attempt to retrieve the target object”  
“B: Prioritize securing the surrounding area”  
“C: Emergency contact to management staff”

“…This isn’t a choice game.”

Yuto muttered quietly.

In front of him was a real vehicle.  
A real child.  
A real danger.

There was no time to stare at the choices.

“Haru!”

“I know!”

Haru waved his arms widely toward the vehicle’s path.

“Stop, please! Stop once!”

The manager also lifted his radio.

“Vehicle, stop! Please stop!”

The reversing sound cut off once.

But the vehicle had not completely stopped.

“Stop, stop…”

Yuto pressed C on his phone.

“C: Emergency contact to management staff”

Immediately, the screen switched.

“Safety-priority contact sent.  
Continue securing the surrounding area.”

“Next is B.”

Yuto then selected B.

“B: Prioritize securing the surrounding area”

A short instruction appeared on the screen.

“Move away from the vehicle route.  
Retrieve the target object only after confirming vehicle stop.  
Guide users to a safe position.”

“Everyone, this way! Please move outside the rope!”

Yuto guided the people who had been resting with his hand.

Haru brought the mother and child to the wall side.

“We can get the sandal later.  
Stay here for now.”

“But…”

“It’s okay. We’ll get it.”

Haru’s voice was surprisingly calm.

A few seconds later.

At last, the delivery vehicle stopped completely.

The driver’s window opened.

“I’m sorry! I was late stopping!”

The manager ran over and stood in front of the vehicle.

“Hold there for now! A child’s item is still inside!”

Only after that signal did Yuto enter the loading space for the first time.

Do not run.  
Do not rush.

He looked under the pallet.

“There it is.”

The red sandal had rolled a little farther inside.

Yuto reached out and pulled it closer with his fingertips.

“Got it.”

The child looked up immediately.

“Really?”

“Yeah. Here.”

When Yuto held out the sandal, the child received it with both hands.

“Thank you!”

At that one sentence, Yuto smiled before he realized it.

“You’re welcome.”

Perhaps because the tension had broken, a small round of applause rose around them.

The mother bowed deeply.

“Thank you so much.  
I panicked…”

“It’s all right.  
I’m glad we got it after the vehicle properly stopped.”

At that moment, the phone vibrated.

“Emergency event response confirmed.  
Safety-priority action evaluated.”

Yuto stared at the screen.

“Safety action bonus: +0.005 CC”  
“Temporary stay point evaluation: +0.004 CC”

“…A total of 0.009 CC.”

Honestly, as an amount, it was not large.

But then—

“New achievement unlocked.  
Safety Route: Level 1”

“Achievement…?”

Yuto narrowed his eyes.

Then another notification appeared.

“Players with Safety Route Level 1 may meet the participation requirements for a limited challenge.”

“A limited challenge?”

Beside him, Haru was also looking at his own device.

“I got it too.”

“The same notification?”

“Yeah.  
A joint evaluation for ‘Safety Route.’  
Probably because we handled it together just now.”

Looking at the screen, Haru relaxed the corner of his mouth slightly.

“…Not bad.”

“Until just now, you were telling me not to say things so easily.”

“I did.”

“You’re not denying it.”

“It wasn’t wrong enough to deny.”

That way of saying it made Yuto laugh a little.

“So you’ll cooperate with me?”

“Don’t misunderstand.  
I was watching the outskirts first.”

“Is this your territory?”

“It’s competition.”

Haru answered immediately.

“But if it’s about safety like today, that’s different.  
I hate people who try to earn CC by doing dangerous things.”

Yuto found those words a little unexpected.

A competitor.  
But not someone who would leave danger alone.

“Understood.  
Then we compete, and cooperate when necessary.”

“How convenient.”

“If I want to rise, I want to use what I can use.”

“You’ve started talking like that.”

Around then, the manager returned to the two of them.

“You helped us.  
Honestly, this almost became much bigger than a test operation for a rest area.”

“I’m sorry.  
We let people in so suddenly…”

“No.”

The manager looked toward the edge of the venue.

A path with no shade.  
People standing with drinks in their hands.  
Parents holding children’s hands.  
Vendors with reddened faces.

“I can see that a place to rest is necessary.  
But the loading area is dangerous.  
The way we did it today can’t continue.”

“Yes.”

“But… if it’s another place, we may be able to talk.”

Yuto and Haru both looked up at the same time.

“Another place?”

The manager lowered his voice a little.

“Behind the venue, there’s an old waiting room.  
It’s been closed for a long time, but the roof is still there.  
The electricity is probably still alive too.”

“A place like that exists?”

“But there’s no key.  
And we can’t reach the management company this weekend.”

Haru said,

“Then today is impossible.”

“Normally, yes.”

The manager looked at the screen of his radio.

“But someone saw how you handled that.”

“Who?”

When Yuto asked, the manager pointed toward the venue entrance.

A woman in a navy jacket was standing there.

Despite the heat, she was dressed neatly.  
Around her neck hung a silver ID card different from the venue staff’s.

The woman walked straight toward Yuto and Haru.

“Soma Yuto.  
And Haru, correct?”

The two exchanged glances.

“…Yes.”

“I am Minato from the Cooling Commons Regional Observation Team.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated again.

“Limited challenge candidate detected.  
Regionally unconnected area: old waiting room”

At the bottom of the screen, larger letters than before appeared.

“Estimated reward: 0.050 CC or more”

“…Zero point zero five?”

Yuto caught his breath.

It was a different scale from the extra rewards so far.

Minato said quietly,

“Can the old waiting room be turned into a usable rest base?  
That is tomorrow’s limited challenge.”

Haru narrowed his eyes.

“Tomorrow?”

“Yes.  
But only players who have obtained the Safety Route achievement can participate.”

A new countdown appeared on Yuto’s screen.

“Limited challenge begins in  
17 hours 42 minutes”

The evening sun lit the buildings at the edge of town red.

Only the roof of the old waiting room could be seen quietly behind the venue.

Yuto stared at that building.

“…Tomorrow, this is where we compete.”

## Afterword

Episode 45 resolved the emergency from Episode 44 through a “safety-first decision,” and that action led to Yuto’s new achievement and the next earning opportunity.

This time, I was conscious of the following flow.

- Setup: A delivery vehicle approaches while a child’s sandal remains in the loading space
- Development: Yuto does not rush to retrieve it, but chooses contact and safety securing first
- Turn: The safety response is evaluated, and he gains the “Safety Route” achievement and a joint evaluation
- Ending: A limited challenge involving the old waiting room appears, with a reward of 0.050 CC or more

This time, Yuto was evaluated not for chasing immediate CC by jumping into danger, but for solving the situation safely.  
And Haru has become positioned as a recurring competitor: not fully an ally, but someone who can return many times.

In the next episode, the old waiting room limited challenge begins.  
A larger reward has been presented, but who will participate, and what hidden conditions are waiting? For Yuto, this looks like it will become not just a small improvement, but a serious contest that could change his rank and income.
