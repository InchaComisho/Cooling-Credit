# Episode 53: The Two-Location Night Operation

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 53 begins with surplus cooling capacity at only 4%, right on the edge of failure.  
The release key is likely in the old waiting room. But if Yuto leaves the site, the response at the waiting station may collapse. This episode depicts the tension of moving two locations at once, while drawing closer to the sender of the anonymous messages.

---

## Main Text

“Surplus cooling capacity: 4% remaining”

The warning tone at the night delivery waiting station sounded at short intervals.

Beep.  
Beep.  
Beep.

The temperature on the waiting station side was slowly rising.

“Waiting station: 19.4°C”  
“Temporary storage space: 6.8°C”

The air separation damper D-1 had somehow moved.

But the loading-bay-side D-2 did not respond.

Part of the cold air was escaping toward an unused passageway.  
Because of that, the waiting station was still cold, and wasteful load remained on the shared cooling line.

“Even if the release key is in the old waiting room…”

Yuto stared at his phone.

“Do we have time to go back now, search for it, and come back again…?”

Katase spoke while still looking at the facility display.

“The large shipment will continue coming in for another forty minutes.  
If the load drops below 3% during that time, we’ll have to prioritize refrigerated-designation cargo.”

“The waiting station side adjustment will be pushed even farther back.”

Nagi said.

“More people will stay cold.”

In the waiting station, delivery workers were wearing jackets.

People who wanted even a little rest before starting work.  
People returning from long-distance drives.  
People sitting with their eyes closed until roll call.

But all of them looked cold.

“We need to warm this place.”

Yuto said.

“But we can’t stop the cooling that protects the cargo.”

“That’s why D-2 needs to be closed.”

Katase replied.

“But we don’t have the release key.”

At that moment, a short notification arrived on Yuto’s phone.

“Anonymous:  
The machine room of the old waiting room.  
Look under the control panel for the blue toolbox.”

“…That’s too specific.”

Yuto stared at the screen.

“Who is this?”

“A guide, or a watcher.”

Nagi said.

“Either way, they know the inside of the waiting room very well.”

Haru returned from the loading-bay side.

His reflective vest was slightly dusty.

“I reached D-2.  
There is a physical lever.  
But it’s locked. Without a key, it’s impossible.”

“So we really do need the release key.”

“It’s probably in the blue toolbox.”

When Yuto said that, Haru’s eyes sharpened.

“Another anonymous message?”

“Yeah.”

“Are you going to trust it?”

“Not trust.  
Use it as a lead.”

Yuto opened the map of the waiting room and their current location.

By car, about ten minutes.  
On foot, close to twenty minutes even running.  
Even if they borrowed Katase’s car, the warehouse district at night was full of delivery vehicles.

“I’ll go.”

Yuto said.

“Haru, please stay here.  
We’ll need safety confirmation when D-2 opens.”

“You’re going to the old waiting room alone?”

“Nagi, can you come with me?”

Nagi answered immediately.

“I’ll go.  
If it’s an equipment clue, I’m less likely to miss something.”

“I’ll go too.”

Sōma said.

“I’m the one who messed up in the old waiting room machine room.”

“Sōma, stay here.”

Yuto said.

“What?”

“I want you to keep explaining things to the delivery workers and sorting the cargo.  
Right now, you’re the one who can move between Katase and the people on site.”

“Me?”

“You asked properly, and you moved people properly earlier.”

Sōma fell silent for a moment.

Then he looked away and said,

“…Fine.  
But once you find the key, come back immediately.”

“Of course.”

Katase held out a car key.

“There’s a private kei van of mine behind the facility, not a delivery vehicle.  
Can you drive?”

“Yes.”

“Drive safely.  
If you cause an accident today because you’re in a hurry, everything ends.”

“I understand.”

Yuto took the key.

His palm was slightly sweaty.

“Surplus cooling capacity: 4% remaining”  
“Additional reward multiplier: ×1.4”

He compared the two displays on the screen.

A chance to earn CC.  
But right now, just chasing the number was not enough.

“Nagi, let’s go.”

“Yeah.”

The kei van set off through the warehouse district at night.

◇

When they reached the old waiting room, it was 8:02 p.m.

The daytime bustle of the flea market had already disappeared.

The stalls had been cleared away.  
Half the venue lights were off.  
Only a few cars remained in the wide parking lot.

Only the old waiting room stood there, lit up alone.

Near the entrance bench, a man who looked like a security guard was sitting.

“Excuse me.”

When Yuto approached, the man stood up.

“The waiting station matter?”

“You know about it?”

“The regional observation team contacted us.  
If you’re going into the machine room, take these.”

The man handed over a small light and a white helmet.

“The inside still hasn’t been fully maintained.  
Don’t touch the machinery without permission.”

“Yes.”

Nagi also received a helmet.

“Thank you.”

The inside of the waiting room was quieter than it had been during the day.

The benches near the entrance had been prepared for use.  
Air came in through the ventilation windows, and the cooling function was running at low output.

But as they approached the machine room in the back, the air changed.

Vrrrrr…

A low operating sound.  
The smell of old metal.  
Peeling warning signs left on the wall.

“Under the control panel, the blue toolbox.”

Yuto said quietly.

“Even if you find it, don’t open it right away.”

Nagi said.

“We don’t know what’s inside.  
With old equipment, there may be spare parts or chemicals left along with keys.”

“Understood.”

Under the control panel, several old cables stretched across the floor.

Behind them.

There was a dust-covered blue toolbox.

“…Found it.”

Yuto crouched down.

The blue paint was peeling, and the corners were rusted.  
But small white letters remained on the box.

“Shared Cooling Line  
For Maintenance”

Nagi held her breath.

“That’s not just a toolbox.  
If it contains maintenance keys, the D-2 release key really may be inside.”

Yuto touched the clasp.

It was not locked.

Click.

The lid opened.

Inside were old tools.  
Gloves.  
Yellowed inspection sheets.  
And a small key holder.

Three keys.

“Waiting Room M-1”  
“Venue Mist V-3”  
“Delivery Relay D-2”

“D-2!”

Yuto shouted without thinking.

“We found it…!”

Nagi immediately checked the key.

“The key number matches the waiting station display.  
There’s a high chance this will work.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Release key candidate confirmed”  
“Limited challenge, second condition: progress 68%”

“68%…”

“Let’s go back.”

Nagi said.

But Yuto’s eyes turned to the inspection sheet still left in the toolbox.

There was an old handwritten note on the paper.

“When releasing D-2, check V-3 first”  
“If load on the delivery relay rapidly increases, automatically stop the venue mist”

“V-3…”

The venue mist.

When they stopped the mist during the day, the load had gone down.  
But now the flea market venue was closed.

“The venue mist may still be operating.”

Nagi read the inspection sheet.

“If it hasn’t automatically stopped, then the moment D-2 is released, the cooling line load may flow in another direction.”

“Even if we fix the waiting station, another place may stop again…”

Yuto gritted his teeth.

They had found the key.

But before they could use it, there was now one more thing they had to check.

At that moment.

From the back of the waiting room, even deeper than the machine room, there came a faint sound.

Tap.

Yuto and Nagi turned at the same time.

“…Is someone there?”

There was no answer.

Tap.

This time, it came from beyond the wall.

Nagi aimed the light.

Beside the control panel.  
Where the piping entered the wall.

An old, small speaker was embedded there.

Bzz…

Noise.

And then, a voice they had heard before.

“…Yuto-kun.  
If you’ve found the key, don’t rush next.”

“…Who?”

Yuto felt a chill down his back.

The voice from the speaker was the voice of the elderly man who had been taken away by ambulance during the day.

“Before opening D-2, check the venue’s V-3.  
Otherwise, it won’t end with tonight’s deliveries.”

Yuto stared at the speaker in the wall.

“How… do you know my name?”

Instead of an answer, the waiting room lights went out for an instant.

Pop.

And when they came back on, a new display appeared on Yuto’s phone.

“Shared cooling line: unexpected control signal detected”

“Venue Mist V-3  
Remote activation in: 00:09:59”

“Remote activation…?”

Nagi’s face stiffened.

“The venue is closed, but the mist is going to restart?”

“Someone is trying to move it.”

Yuto gripped the D-2 release key.

Ten minutes remaining.

Should they return to the waiting station?  
Should they go stop the venue V-3?

Or should they follow the identity of the mysterious voice remaining in this waiting room?

The shared cooling line at night still would not let Yuto and the others go.

## Afterword

Episode 53 entered the “two-location night operation,” in which Yuto and the others move the waiting station and the old waiting room at the same time.

The key points this time were as follows.

- Yuto and Nagi head to the old waiting room, while Haru handles the loading-bay side, Sōma handles the flow of delivery workers and cargo, and Katase handles on-site operation
- In the old waiting room machine room, they find the D-2 release key inside a blue toolbox
- However, an old inspection sheet says “When releasing D-2, check V-3 first,” revealing the risk of linkage with the venue mist
- A voice that seems to belong to the elderly man taken away by ambulance during the day comes through an old speaker in the waiting room
- In addition, a new crisis appears: Venue Mist V-3 will remotely activate in 10 minutes

Yuto obtained the release key and advanced the limited challenge’s second condition to 68%.  
However, finding the key was not the solution. The possibility emerged that someone had begun operating the entire shared cooling line.

In the next episode, the focus will be how to stop V-3 with 10 minutes remaining before remote activation.  
If the mist restarts in the closed venue, the night delivery waiting station’s cooling margin may be reduced even further. And the identity of the person sending anonymous messages and speaking through the old speaker will begin to become clearer, including whether they are an ally or someone with a different purpose.
