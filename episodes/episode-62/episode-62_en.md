# Episode 62: The Conditions for 5.000 CC

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 62 follows Yuto as he faces an extraordinary wide-area project worth at least 5.000 CC, but refuses to answer immediately and instead tries to confirm FROST-9’s way of thinking and the reality of North Seventh Wharf.  
Is it a shortcut to earning big, or a trap that will make him lose his own method? His first dialogue with an upper player moves at a brisk pace.

---

## Main Text

“North Seventh Wharf: Limited Wide-Area Project”  
“Reward: minimum 5.000 CC”

That number floated on the huge monitor inside the Central Cooling Terminal.

5.000 CC.

Yuto’s cumulative total was 1.261 CC.

Judging by the numbers alone, if he succeeded in this one project, he could surpass everything he had built up so far several times over.

“…Minimum, 5.000.”

Yuto said it without thinking.

FROST-9’s expression did not change.

“To be precise, 5.000 for basic completion.  
If additional conditions are met, it increases further.”

“Additional conditions?”

“Wide-area projects are evaluated not only by results, but also by stability.  
If we can get through three days without stopping the cooling lines, delaying logistics, or triggering emergency response, there will be an additional allocation.”

“How much?”

“Up to 12.000 CC.”

Yuto held his breath.

12.000 CC.

The first-ranked player in the metropolitan rankings had 87.420 CC.

He was still far from reaching that number.

But this was beginning to stop feeling like a distant world.

“…Why me?”

Yuto looked at FROST-9.

“There must be other upper players.  
People with more CC than me, and more experience.”

“There are.”

FROST-9 answered immediately.

“And most upper players would accept under my conditions.”

“Then there’s even less reason for it to be me.”

“No.”

FROST-9 enlarged the metropolitan map.

North Seventh Wharf.

A vast coastal area.  
Container yards.  
Large warehouses.  
Lines of delivery vehicles waiting.  
Rest buildings for workers.  
Nearby, there was also an old residential district.

“Upper players find efficient places.  
Cooling efficiency. Logistics efficiency. Equipment efficiency.  
They are good at increasing numbers.”

Blue, yellow, and red lines appeared on the map of North Seventh Wharf.

“But they are not good at deciding to preserve inefficient places.”

“Preserve?”

“Rest buildings.  
Waiting lines with little shade.  
Water supply spots used only briefly.  
Small evacuation spaces in nearby residential areas.”

FROST-9 pointed at the red dots one by one.

“Places that easily become candidates for being cut in order to protect the whole.”

Yuto was a little surprised.

Was FROST-9 not simply an efficiency-first player?

“So you want me to protect those places?”

“No.”

FROST-9 looked straight at Yuto.

“I want you to decide, in my place, whether they should be protected or cut.”

Yuto frowned.

“You said earlier that you would make the final decision.”

“I carry the final responsibility.  
But I need someone to provide the material for judgment.”

“I go to the field, look, report, and then you decide?”

“Yes.”

“Then isn’t that not so different from collaborative operation?”

“It is different.”

FROST-9’s voice lowered slightly.

“In collaborative operation, everyone is involved in the decision.  
This project is different.  
If we listen to everyone’s opinions, something will break within three days.”

Forecast data appeared on the control room monitor.

“North Seventh Wharf: extreme heat forecast”  
“Maximum feels-like temperature: 43°C”  
“Large cargo concentration: 168% of normal”  
“Night waiting vehicles: 142% of normal”  
“Regional cooling margin: 11%”

“Margin, 11%…”

Yuto looked at the numbers.

On the night of the shared cooling line, when the margin dropped to 4%, it had been that dangerous.

This time, it was wide-area.

The scale was different.

“There is not enough margin to protect everyone.”

FROST-9 said.

“That is why we need to decide in advance where to cut.”

The word chilled Yuto’s chest a little.

“Cut…”

“Lower cooling output.  
Shorten operating hours.  
Lower priority.  
No matter how you rephrase it, it is the same thing.”

FROST-9 did not hesitate.

“I will decide.  
For that purpose, I want you to go and see the field.”

Yuto looked at the red dots on the monitor.

A small water supply spot.  
A rest building used for short breaks.  
A residential evacuation space.

Certainly, if one looked only at overall efficiency, those places would likely be cut first.

But there were people there.

“I want the conditions changed.”

Yuto said.

FROST-9’s eyebrow moved slightly.

“What part?”

“If I go, I don’t just want to gather field voices. I want authority to propose alternatives.”

“You can propose.”

“Proposing is not enough.”

Yuto chose his words.

“Not just the binary choice of ‘cut or preserve.’  
Dividing by time slot, changing movement routes, leaving something at low output.  
I want discretion to try another form.”

“Discretion?”

“Small field adjustments.  
I am not going to move a major line on my own.  
But I want to make adjustments so the people there can still use something, even a little.”

FROST-9 fell silent.

Only the cooling sound of the control room echoed.

Vooooom…

“I am going because I want the reward.”

Yuto said honestly.

“I want 5.000 CC.  
I think it is a chance I need in order to rise.”

“Then you should follow my instructions.”

“But I do not want to cut people I saw in the field by numbers alone just to earn CC.”

FROST-9’s eyes narrowed slightly.

“That is pretty talk.”

“Maybe it is.”

“At North Seventh Wharf, pretty talk will not keep the line running.”

“I know.”

Yuto looked at the map of North Seventh Wharf.

“That is why I want to see the field before deciding.”

Silence continued for a while.

Eventually, FROST-9 operated her terminal.

“Limited Wide-Area Project: Pre-Participation Reconnaissance”

“North Seventh Wharf: preliminary observation pass granted”

“Grace period: 24 hours”

“I will give you one day.”

FROST-9 said.

“Look at North Seventh Wharf.  
Visit every place I marked as a candidate for cutting.”

Four red points appeared on the monitor.

“A: Third Worker Rest Building”  
“B: Water Supply Spot for Waiting Vehicles”  
“C: Harbor Outer Perimeter Shaded Bench Area”  
“D: Nearby Residential District Simple Evacuation Space”

“Four?”

“All of them are candidates for operational reduction.”

“I look at them and propose alternatives.”

“And if you cannot?”

“…I will follow your judgment.”

FROST-9 nodded.

“That is the condition.”

A new screen appeared on Yuto’s phone.

“Preliminary observation pass: started”

“Reward: observation completion +0.080 CC”  
“If an alternative operation plan is adopted: additional +0.250 CC”  
“Formal participation qualification for wide-area project: unlockable”

“0.080…”

It was not suddenly 5.000 CC.

First, he would look at the field.

He would begin from a small reward.

That felt like Yuto.

“I accept.”

Yuto said.

“But if I look at all four places and there is no method other than cutting them, I will acknowledge that too.”

“Good.”

For the first time, FROST-9 smiled faintly.

“That is what I wanted to see.”

“What?”

“Whether you would nod immediately after seeing 5.000 CC.”

Yuto felt a little displeased.

“You were testing me?”

“Yes.”

“Do you like testing people?”

“I do not like it.  
But I choose carefully when I entrust someone with the field in a wide-area project.”

FROST-9 closed the map of North Seventh Wharf.

“Tomorrow, 6:00 a.m.  
Enter from the East Gate of Seventh Wharf.”

“Understood.”

As Yuto headed toward the exit, FROST-9 spoke from behind him.

“One warning.”

Yuto turned back.

“At North Seventh Wharf, it is not only my team.  
Other upper players are also aiming for parts of the project.”

“There are competitors?”

“There are.”

Part of the ranking appeared on FROST-9’s monitor.

“Metropolitan Ranking 3rd: HEATWAVE-3”  
“Metropolitan Ranking 5th: RIVER-EDGE”  
“Metropolitan Ranking 8th: MIST CROW”

“They do not hesitate over which places to cut.”

Yuto looked at the IDs.

FROST-9 had viewed his log.

And now, he was entering the territory of upper players.

“Someone might cut a place before I can make an alternative proposal.”

“That is correct.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“North Seventh Wharf: preliminary observation pass”

“Time until start: 15 hours 42 minutes”

Below it, there was an unfamiliar display.

“Observation targets: 4 locations”  
“Competing players: detected”  
“Your current rank: outside ranking”

And finally, one line.

“Priority proposal rights will be granted to the first person who moves the field.”

Yuto stopped at the exit of the Central Cooling Terminal.

5.000 CC was still far away.

But tomorrow.

At North Seventh Wharf, before players stronger than himself, he would have to see, move, and propose around four sites.

The competition to rise upward was finally beginning.

## Afterword

In Episode 62, faced with a wide-area project worth at least 5.000 CC, Yuto did not simply accept FROST-9’s conditions. Instead, he asked for the discretion to propose alternatives beyond only “cut or preserve.”

The key points this time were as follows.

- At North Seventh Wharf, extreme heat and a concentration of large cargo overlap, and the regional cooling margin is only 11%
- FROST-9 believes that because there is not enough margin to protect everyone, they must decide in advance which places to reduce operationally
- The reduction candidates are the Third Worker Rest Building, the Water Supply Spot for Waiting Vehicles, the Harbor Outer Perimeter Shaded Bench Area, and the Nearby Residential District Simple Evacuation Space
- Yuto asked for the authority to consider alternatives that could preserve field usability, such as dividing by time slot, lowering output, or changing movement routes
- FROST-9 granted him a 24-hour preliminary observation pass, offering +0.080 CC for completion and an additional +0.250 CC if his alternative plan is adopted
- Upper players such as HEATWAVE-3, RIVER-EDGE, and MIST CROW are also involved at North Seventh Wharf, and the competition for priority proposal rights begins

Yuto is not trying to obey FROST-9 just to receive 5.000 CC.  
Instead, he is trying to bring in his own strength: seeing the field and creating another usable form. However, this opponent is not a small edge-of-town improvement like before. Upper players in the metropolitan rankings may cut inefficient places before Yuto finishes seeing the field.

The next episode begins at North Seventh Wharf at 6:00 a.m.  
In what order will he visit the four reduction candidates? Who will secure the field first? And will the first competing player Yuto meets become a collaborator, or someone who comes to crush his proposal from the start?
