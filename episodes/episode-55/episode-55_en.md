# Episode 55: Places to Cool, People to Protect

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 55 is a major climax of the night delivery waiting station arc, beginning with only 3 minutes and 30 seconds remaining in emergency maintenance mode.  
If they can release D-2 and redistribute the cooling, they may be able to protect both the delivery workers from the cold and the packages that require temperature control. But V-3A at the center of the venue has begun moving again, and someone’s remote control stands as the final obstacle.

---

## Main Text

“Emergency maintenance mode valid time: 03:30”

Yuto gripped the D-2 release key in the passenger seat of the small van.

Outside the window, the lights of the warehouse district at night flowed past.

Nagi held the steering wheel.

“One and a half minutes left!”

“Hurry. But don’t go too fast!”

“I know!”

The road on the edge of town at night was more dangerous than during the day.

Large trucks.  
Workers carrying cargo.  
Dark crossings.  
The more they rushed, the more things they could miss.

Yuto’s phone kept issuing warnings.

“V-3A: no control response”  
“Shared cooling line load: 85%”  
“Emergency maintenance mode: 02:48”

“Eighty-five… it’s rising.”

“The central mist is starting to move.”

Nagi said.

“Even if V-3B is stopped, it won’t matter if V-3A returns to full output.”

“But if we open D-2, we can stop the wasted cold air on the waiting-station side.”

“Then redistribute afterward.”

“We’ll make it.”

Yuto said it as if telling himself.

“We’ll make it in time.”

◇

The night delivery waiting station.

The small van stopped beside the loading bay.

Haru was already waiting in front of D-2.

Above the loading bay.  
An old metal inspection hatch.  
Beyond it was the manual damper lever.

“Key!”

The moment Yuto got out of the car, he threw the key.

Haru caught it with one hand.

“How much time!”

“Two minutes!”

“Emergency maintenance mode: 01:58”

Sōma was guiding delivery workers pushing carts.

“Clear the loading bay for thirty seconds!  
Temperature-controlled containers go to the blue line!  
Everything else against the wall!”

“Another adjustment?”

“It’s an adjustment so we don’t have to stop!”

Sōma’s voice echoed through the warehouse.

Katase stood in front of the display board on the waiting-station side.

A tablet was in one hand.  
A radio was in the other.

“Tell me when D-2 opens!  
I’ll lower the airflow on the waiting-station side!”

“Got it!”

Haru climbed the stepladder and opened the inspection hatch.

Clunk.

An old metallic smell came from inside.

“I can see the keyhole!”

“Will it go in?”

Yuto asked from below.

“…It goes in.”

Click.

The key turned.

But the lever would not move.

“It’s stuck!”

“Don’t push it. Pull it toward you once!”

Nagi shouted while looking at the equipment diagram.

“The lock release isn’t clockwise.  
Turn it back counterclockwise a little, then down!”

Haru adjusted his strength.

Creak…

“Emergency maintenance mode: 01:24”

The V-3A load display rose.

“Shared cooling line load: 88%”

“Hurry!”

Yuto’s throat went dry.

Then the lever suddenly moved.

Bang!

“It opened!”

Haru’s voice.

At the same time, the airflow throughout the facility changed.

The cold wind coming from the ceiling vents in the waiting station weakened slightly.

In the back of the temporary storage space, cold air began concentrating toward the refrigerated containers.

“D-2: release confirmed”  
“Emergency maintenance mode: 01:03”

“Now!”

Yuto opened his phone, which was connected to the maintenance terminal in the old waiting room.

Three allocation bars appeared on the screen.

“Waiting Station”  
“Temporary Storage Space”  
“Venue Mist V-3A”

Manual redistribution.

The numbers moved by percentage.

At the moment, they looked like this.

“Waiting Station: 41%”  
“Temporary Storage Space: 37%”  
“Venue Mist V-3A: 22%”

“The waiting station is taking too much.”

Nagi said.

“The temporary storage side doesn’t have enough.  
And V-3A is taking 22%.”

“The venue is closed, right?”

“It’s closed.  
But only the central mist system can’t be stopped.”

“Then we leave only the minimum.”

Yuto called out to Katase.

“Katase-san!  
What’s the target temperature for the waiting station?”

“Twenty-three degrees!”

“And temporary storage!”

“Keep it at seven degrees or below!”

“Haru!  
How low can we drop the venue side while keeping the line stable?”

“If V-3A is completely stopped, pressure may rebound!  
Leave eight percent!”

“Eight percent…”

Forty-six seconds remained.

Yuto moved the bars.

“Waiting Station: 28%”  
“Temporary Storage Space: 64%”  
“Venue Mist V-3A: 8%”

“Execute!”

His finger pressed the screen.

“Execute manual redistribution?”

“Execute!”

The screen froze.

One second.

Two seconds.

Long.

“Emergency maintenance mode: 00:29”

“Please…!”

The waiting station display board changed.

“Waiting Station: 20.2°C”

“It’s rising!”

Nagi said.

“Temporary Storage Space: 6.6°C”

“Refrigeration is maintained!”

Katase shouted.

“Venue Mist V-3A: shifting to low output”

Yuto held his breath.

“Shared cooling line load: 76%”

“It dropped!”

Haru leaned out from the inspection hatch.

“It’s seventy-six!”

Almost there.

But another message appeared on the phone.

“V-3A: external control conflict detected”

“Remote operator is requesting output change”

“Someone is still touching it!”

A chill ran down Yuto’s back.

The venue mist bar began moving on its own.

“V-3A: 8% → 12%”

“It’s being pushed back!”

“Stop it!”

Sōma shouted.

“I don’t have the authority to stop it!”

Yuto tapped the screen.

“External control conflict”  
“Another authority is connected along with Administrator ID 00”

“Another authority?”

Nagi drew in a breath.

“Someone is inside the line at this very moment.”

The value for Venue Mist V-3A rose again.

“12% → 16%”

The load increased.

“Shared cooling line load: 79%”

“This is bad!”

At that moment, the waiting station lights went out once.

Pop.

Only the emergency lights remained on.

The delivery workers stirred anxiously.

“Is it a blackout?”

“Are the packages okay?”

Katase shouted into the radio.

“Check the lights on the temporary storage side!  
Look at the displays on the refrigerated containers!”

A reply came a few seconds later.

“Katase-san, the refrigerated side is alive!  
But only the waiting station lights are down!”

“Only the waiting-station system?”

Yuto looked at the display board.

“Waiting Station: 21.1°C”  
“Temporary Storage Space: 6.7°C”  
“Venue Mist V-3A: 16%”

The cooling was working.

And yet only the lights had gone out.

“The external operator isn’t only touching cooling.”

Nagi’s voice hardened.

“They’re testing the facility systems.”

At that moment, an anonymous message arrived on Yuto’s phone.

“Anonymous:  
Stop.  
With that allocation, you may protect the delivery side, but the people in the waiting station are in danger.”

Yuto stared at the screen.

“In danger…?”

Another message followed.

“Anonymous:  
The emergency ventilation in the waiting station has stopped.  
The lights going out are a warning.”

Haru came down from the inspection hatch.

“Emergency ventilation?”

Katase’s face turned pale.

“Wait.  
If emergency ventilation stops, the air in the waiting station won’t circulate.  
If the doors close while a lot of people are inside…”

“Carbon dioxide levels will rise.”

Nagi said.

Yuto looked inside the waiting station.

The delivery workers.  
The people who had been sleeping.  
The people who had finally begun to feel some relief from the cold.

They had adjusted the cooling.

It looked like they could protect the cargo too.

But now the air itself was dangerous.

A new warning appeared on the phone.

“Waiting station emergency ventilation: stopped”  
“Restoration priority: highest”

And below it, choices.

“Maintain cooling redistribution”  
“Temporarily evacuate the waiting station”  
“Attempt manual restoration of emergency ventilation”

Yuto looked at the darkened waiting station.

0.100 CC.  
A ×1.4 multiplier.  
A contest that might push his cumulative total beyond 1.0 CC.

But before the numbers, there were people he had to protect.

“…Guide everyone outside.”

Yuto said.

“Sōma-san, call out to the delivery workers!  
Katase-san, is there a safe place on the temporary storage side where people can go?”

“There is!  
But only outside the refrigerated section!”

“Haru, Nagi-san.  
Let’s go check the manual restoration for the emergency ventilation!”

Haru nodded briefly.

“This time, we hurry.  
But we don’t touch anything on our own.”

Deep in the dark waiting station, somewhere, a ventilation door—

Clanged.

## Afterword

Episode 55 succeeded in releasing D-2 and manually redistributing the cooling, but a new problem emerged because of an external control conflict.

The key points this time were as follows.

- Yuto, Haru, Nagi, Sōma, and Katase divided roles and released D-2 within the remaining time of emergency maintenance mode
- The cooling allocation was changed to “Waiting Station 28%, Temporary Storage Space 64%, Venue Mist V-3A 8%”
- The shared cooling line load fell from 88% to 76%, the waiting station temperature rose to 20.2°C, and the temporary storage space maintained 6.6°C
- However, another external operator began increasing V-3A’s output, and the waiting station lights in the facility went out
- An anonymous message revealed that the waiting station’s emergency ventilation had stopped
- Yuto chose not to prioritize the reward or cooling allocation, but to temporarily evacuate the delivery workers while heading to restore emergency ventilation

Once again, Yuto did not choose to place people in danger for the sake of earning numbers.  
Even if they had succeeded in adjusting the cooling and protecting the cargo, continuing the contest would have no meaning if the air in the waiting station was dangerous for the people inside.

In the next episode, the emergency response around the darkened waiting station’s emergency ventilation begins.  
Who is performing the external operation, and why are they targeting only the waiting-station systems? Can Yuto and the others complete the 0.100 CC-class challenge while protecting both the cooling line and human safety? The final stage is drawing near.
