# Episode 67 — The Port at Seven Percent

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 67 begins with an abnormality at North Pier Seven's main cooling tower, leaving the area's wide-area cooling reserve at just 7%.  
At Point D, the emergency expansion operation continues while the second rest station is still about twenty minutes from recovery. Yuto must protect the small refuge area while also facing output restrictions that affect the entire port.

---

## Main Story

『North Pier Seven — Wide-Area Cooling Reserve: 7%』

The localized mist at Point D trembled faintly.

Hissss...

The spray was clearly thinner than before.

Under the roof sat two elderly women and a mother with her child.
On the wind-rest side were workers who had flowed in from the port.
Beside the water station were cooling towels and ice packs.

The small refuge space was already close to its limit.

『Point D — Emergency Expanded Operation』
『Until Rest Station No. 2 Recovery: 00:19:12』

『Conditions:
- Maintain user safety
- Keep impact on total port load within 5%
- Emergency departure rate: 0%
- Ensure safe transfer after Rest Station No. 2 recovers』

“Seven percent reserve for the whole port...”

Yuto stared at the display on his phone.

Even keeping the localized mist running increased the load.
The more Point D was protected, the less reserve remained elsewhere.

“What exactly happened to the main cooling tower?”

Without looking up from his tablet, MIST CROW answered.

“The heat-exchange system got too hot. The cooling tower circulates water to release heat. But port-side exhaust heat combined with the outside temperature, and the system can no longer shed heat fast enough.”

“So it isn't broken?”

“Not yet. This is protective control. It's reducing output before something actually fails.”

“So it's cutting power in order to avoid stopping completely.”

“Right. Better than a full shutdown. But if this continues, every point will be forced into output restrictions.”

Yuto's phone vibrated.

『Main Cooling Tower — Output Restriction Warning』

『Point D Local Cooling: 30% → 10%
Execution in: 00:04:59』

“Down to ten percent?” Haru said.

“That won't reach the wind-rest side.”

One of the workers frowned.

“So we're losing cooling again?”

“We came here because Rest Station No. 2 went down.”

Unease began spreading through the group.

Yuto immediately stepped into the circle.

“It isn't stopping yet.”

“But it'll drop to ten percent, right?”

“Yes. So while we still can, we're going to make sure the cooling reaches the people who need it most.”

“And who decides who needs it most?” one worker asked.

Yuto paused.

Asked directly, it was not an easy question.

Older people.
Children.
People already looking pale from the heat.
Workers who had been on shift for hours.
People about to return to the job site.

“We're not going to decide that for everyone,” Yuto said.

“We'll let people who are having the hardest time use it first. And for people who have trouble saying 'I'm struggling,' we'll check in with them ourselves.”

One elderly woman nodded.

“Then I'm fine on the windy side. I already got to sit and rest.”

“Thank you. But please don't push yourself.”

The mother with the child also spoke up.

“My child looks better than before. If we still have cooling towels, we can give up some of the mist.”

“That helps.”

Yuto looked at Haru.

“If the local mist drops to ten percent, can you angle it deeper under the roof?”

“I can. But the wind-rest side will basically lose it.”

“Then we'll increase towels and ice packs there and use the sea breeze.”

“Relying on sea breeze, huh.”

“For now, we use everything we have.”

Haru smiled faintly.

“Got it.”

Soma arrived carrying a box of water.

“The first-aid station gave us more water! But this is the last of the ice packs.”

“Understood.”

Yuto immediately wrote on a sheet of paper.

『Cooling towels: priority for those feeling unwell』
『Water: small amounts, as many times as needed』
『Wind-rest side: use the sea breeze』
『Under the roof: priority for those who need it most』

“Won't 'priority for those who need it most' cause arguments?” Soma asked.

“That's why nobody decides unilaterally. People who can say what they need should say it. If someone looks like they're struggling to speak up, we'll ask them.”

“That's complicated.”

“But we'll do it.”

MIST CROW's tablet sounded.

『Until Output Restriction: 00:03:46』

“Yuto,” MIST CROW said.

“Your condition at Point D was to keep the impact on the total port load within five percent.”

“Yes.”

“If you maintain the current localized mist after the main-tower restriction, Point D's impact will rise to 6.2%.”

“That exceeds the limit.”

“Correct. If nothing changes, the demonstration fails.”

Yuto looked at the screen.

『Point D — Projected Load Impact: 6.2%』

Only 1.2% over.

A tiny amount.

But shaving away that tiny amount meant taking cooling away from someone.

“What if we drop local mist to eight percent?”

Nagi's voice came through the comms.

『At 8%, the overall impact falls to 4.9%. But the cooling sensation will drop sharply.』

“Eight percent...”

“Even then, if we concentrate it under the roof, we can keep a minimum amount,” Haru said.

“The wind-rest side would be entirely dependent on airflow and towels, though.”

Yuto looked at the people around him.

Workers.
Residents.
Children.
Older people.

He had to decide now.

But not by deciding alone.

“Everyone!” Yuto called out.

“The port's main cooling tower is under severe stress, so we need to reduce the mist here even further.”

Everyone looked toward him.

“If we keep it at thirty percent, it will affect the entire port. But if we lower it to eight percent, we can keep this place open.”

“Does eight percent even do anything?” someone asked.

“Honestly, it won't feel as cool as it does now,” Yuto answered.

“But it won't go to zero. We'll keep the airflow under the roof, water, and cooling towels. And once Rest Station No. 2 recovers, we'll safely transfer people who are able to move.”

A brief silence followed.

A man in work clothes spoke first.

“Better than shutting down the whole port.”

“But what about the people who are really struggling?” one of the elderly women asked.

“They'll get priority in the deeper shaded area under the roof,” Yuto replied.

“And if needed, we'll call the first-aid station. We won't leave anyone here struggling just to keep the site running.”

The child's mother looked at Yuto.

“Then do it.”

Several others nodded.

“I'm okay on the windy side.”

“If there's water, I'm fine.”

“I'll speak up if I feel like I'm going down.”

Yuto felt a slight heat in the back of his throat.

This wasn't about cutting someone off.

It was about changing how everyone used what little remained.

“Haru.”

“Already on it.”

Haru moved to the control terminal.

『Point D Local Mist
Output: 30% → 8%』

“Execute.”

Hissss...

The mist weakened.

The white spray was barely visible now.

But from the nozzle angled deeper under the roof, a faint trace of cool air still reached the shaded area.

『Point D — Projected Load Impact: 4.9%』

“Within condition,” MIST CROW said.

Immediately afterward, a low alarm sounded across the port.

Woooooom.

『Main Cooling Tower — Output Restriction Executed』

Equipment across the port reduced output at once.

The air around Pier Seven seemed to grow heavier.

『Wide-Area Cooling Reserve: 7% → 9%』

“It came back!” Soma shouted.

“Nine percent reserve!”

“The main tower hasn't exited protection mode,” MIST CROW said.

“But we avoided an emergency shutdown.”

Yuto looked beneath the roof.

The users were still there.

It was hot.
It was not comfortably cool.

But nobody had abruptly left.

『Emergency Departure Rate: 0%』

“Still zero,” Nagi said over comms.

“Airflow routing and the water route are still stable.”

Yuto's phone vibrated.

『Point D — Emergency Expanded Operation』
『Interim Evaluation: Conditions Maintained』

“Still only interim,” Haru said.

“Until Rest Station No. 2 is back, we've got...”

『Estimated Rest Station No. 2 Recovery: 00:11:38』

“Eleven minutes.”

Yuto nodded.

“Now the real part begins.”

At that moment, a white management vehicle approached from the port road.

The logo of the Metropolitan Cooling Terminal was printed on its side.

The vehicle stopped in front of the refuge space.

The door opened, and FROST-9 stepped out.

White jacket.
Same unreadable expression.

But in her hand was not a tablet. It was a silver portable control unit.

“Yuto.”

“FROST-9.”

“The protective control on the main cooling tower can be temporarily overridden.”

Yuto's expression changed.

“Overridden?”

“We can return Point D's mist to thirty percent. We can redirect reserve here without waiting for Rest Station No. 2 to recover.”

“But if you do that...”

“The port-wide reserve drops again. Probably to around six percent.”

FROST-9 raised the silver unit.

“But the people here would feel better immediately.”

MIST CROW spoke in a low voice.

“You're going to use the emergency override authority?”

“It can only be used once.”

FROST-9 looked only at Yuto.

“Decide. Do you cool the people in front of you at thirty percent right now, or keep it at eight percent to protect the port-wide reserve?”

A new display appeared on Yuto's phone.

『Wide-Area Assignment — Emergency Choice』
『Selection Deadline: 00:01:59』

『A: Restore Point D local cooling to 30%』
『B: Prioritize port-wide reserve and maintain 8%』

Yuto looked at the people beneath the mist.

Then he looked toward the cranes lined up deeper in the port—and toward the unseen rest stations beyond them.

One minute and fifty-nine seconds remained.

## Afterword

In Episode 67, an abnormality in North Pier Seven's main cooling tower drove the wide-area reserve down to 7%, and Yuto chose to reduce Point D's localized mist from 30% to 8%.

Key points in this episode:

- The main cooling tower had not failed. Instead, extreme port-side waste heat and high outdoor temperatures prevented enough heat rejection, so protective controls began restricting output.
- Keeping Point D's local mist at 30% would have pushed its share of total port load to 6.2%, exceeding the demonstration condition of 5% or less.
- Nagi calculated that reducing output to 8% would keep Point D's impact to 4.9%.
- Rather than deciding unilaterally, Yuto explained the situation and options to the people using the site, combining shade, airflow, water, and cooling towels to keep Point D open.
- After the main cooling tower entered restricted-output mode, the wide-area cooling reserve recovered from 7% to 9%, while Point D maintained an emergency departure rate of 0%.
- FROST-9 then arrived with emergency authority to temporarily override the main tower protection system and restore Point D's mist to 30%.
- Yuto was forced into a new choice: strongly cool the people directly in front of him now, or preserve reserve for the entire port.

This time, Yuto did not treat output reduction as abandonment. He used it as a way to keep the site from going to zero while still preserving the wider system.  
However, if he uses the temporary override offered by FROST-9, the burden on the people in front of him would immediately ease—at the cost of potentially reducing reserve somewhere else in the port.

The next episode begins with a two-minute emergency choice.  
With FROST-9 watching, Yuto must decide whether to restore Point D to 30% or maintain 8%. And when Rest Station No. 2 recovers, whether Point D's users can be transferred safely will determine whether his alternative-operation plan truly succeeds.
