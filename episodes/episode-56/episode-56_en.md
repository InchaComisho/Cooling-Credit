# Episode 56: Emergency Ventilation in the Dark

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 56 is an emergency phase in the night delivery waiting station arc.  
Although the cooling redistribution has succeeded, emergency ventilation has stopped, and the air in the waiting station itself has begun to become dangerous. While evacuating the delivery workers, Yuto and the others restore ventilation deep inside the dark facility and move one step closer to the identity of the external operator.

---

## Main Text

The waiting station lights had gone out.

Only the red emergency lights remained.

The walls.  
The benches.  
The faces of the delivery workers.

Everything was dyed in a thin red color.

“Waiting station emergency ventilation: stopped”  
“Restoration priority: highest”

That was what Yuto’s phone displayed.

Below it, the cooling line values were fluctuating.

“Shared cooling line load: 79%”  
“Waiting station: 21.1°C”  
“Temporary storage space: 6.7°C”  
“Venue Mist V-3A: 16%”

“Everyone, this way, please!”

Sōma raised his voice.

“We’re moving everyone out of the waiting station once!  
Do not enter the cargo side. Stay in front of the white line!”

“What, is it a blackout?”

“Just the lights?”

“The air conditioning is still running.”

Unease spread among the delivery workers.

But Sōma did not use the forceful tone he had used earlier. He looked each person in the eye and spoke.

“This is just to be safe.  
Can you move places for five minutes?  
We’ll check this area.”

Maybe that way of speaking worked. The first person to stand up was the delivery worker who had spoken to Yuto and the others earlier.

“All right.  
Everyone, let’s move for now.”

The others began moving little by little.

Katase held her radio and directed them to the outside of the temporary storage space.

“We’ll use the area outside the refrigerated section, beside the label shelves, as a temporary waiting area!  
Get water out! Bring chairs too!”

“Understood!”

The workers pushing carts immediately moved.

After confirming that, Yuto turned to Haru and Nagi.

“Where is the emergency ventilation?”

Katase answered.

“In the back of the waiting station.  
Behind the roll-call room, there should be a manual ventilation panel.”

“There should be?”

“No one has touched it manually for more than ten years.”

“More old equipment.”

Haru said.

“Today is nothing but old things.”

“If old things remain, then the possibility that they can still be used remains too.”

Yuto said.

“Let’s go.”

◇

Deep inside the waiting station.

Behind the roll-call room was a passage that was almost never used.

Red emergency light.  
Old supplies piled on the floor.  
Thick ducts stretching along the wall.

Nagi shone her small light.

“The air feels heavy.”

Haru said.

Yuto felt it too.

It was not cold.  
But even when he breathed in, it felt a little shallow.

“Don’t stay too long.  
We check it, and if necessary, we go back immediately.”

Nagi said.

At the end of the passage was an old iron door.

“Emergency Ventilation Panel  
Authorized Personnel Only”

“Everything is operation prohibited today.”

Yuto gave a bitter smile.

“This isn’t the time to laugh.”

Haru said, though the corner of his mouth softened slightly.

They opened the door.

Inside was a ventilation panel covering almost the entire wall.

A large breaker.  
A red emergency lever.  
Small indicator lights.  
And a stopped blower.

There was none of the low vrrrrrm sound that should have been there.

“This is what stopped.”

Nagi said.

“We need to see the cause.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Emergency ventilation system: external control lock detected”

“External control again…”

“Someone stopped the ventilation too?”

Haru’s voice lowered.

“Not just cooling.  
Lights, ventilation, the venue mist.  
They’re touching the peripheral systems connected to the shared line one after another.”

“For what?”

Yuto said.

“To make the waiting station dangerous and make us fail the challenge?”

“If that were all, they could just stop the cooling.”

Nagi shook her head.

“They’re choosing only the places where the load will rise, with this level of precision.  
It’s as if they’re testing the limit of the whole line.”

Those words chilled Yuto’s spine.

Testing the limit.

Was someone intentionally trying to break this shared cooling line?

Red text appeared on the ventilation panel screen.

“External lock active”  
“Administrator authority required”

Yuto checked the display still remaining on his phone.

“Administrator ID 00: emergency maintenance mode”

However, the emergency maintenance mode time had already ended.

“Emergency maintenance mode: expired”

“We can’t use it anymore…”

Haru checked the ventilation panel.

“There’s a manual lever.  
If we pull this, we might be able to activate it while ignoring the external lock.”

“Might, huh.”

Yuto said.

“But once manual activation is turned on, there’s a possibility we won’t be able to stop it right away.”

Nagi read the old label.

“Emergency Ventilation  
When manually activated, prioritize outside-air intake”

“Outside-air intake…”

“If people are in the waiting station, it’s necessary.”

Yuto said.

“But hot outside air will come in.”

“Ventilation comes before cold right now.”

Haru answered.

“Cooling can be adjusted. Air has no substitute.”

At that moment, Katase’s voice came from the far side of the passage.

“Yuto-kun!”

“Katase-san?”

Katase rushed in, gripping her radio.

“The temperature on the refrigerated container side has started rising.  
From 6.7 degrees to 7.4 degrees.  
The cooling line’s V-3A is going up again.”

Yuto looked at his phone.

“Venue Mist V-3A: 16% → 20%”  
“Shared cooling line load: 83%”

“This is bad.”

Nagi said.

“If we activate emergency ventilation, outside air will enter the waiting-station side.  
That will change the cooling load too.”

“But we can’t leave ventilation stopped.”

“I know.  
That’s why, if we do it, it has to be now.”

Katase looked at the manual lever on the ventilation panel.

“I’ll take responsibility.”

“Katase-san.”

“I’m the person responsible for this facility.  
I decided to evacuate the people here, and I will decide to turn on ventilation.”

For a moment, Yuto could not say anything.

Then he nodded.

“We’ll watch the numbers.  
If anything changes, we’ll tell you immediately.”

“Please.”

Katase placed her hand on the red lever.

“Emergency ventilation, manual activation.”

Clank.

The lever went down.

One second.

Two seconds.

Nothing happened.

“Is it not moving?”

Sōma’s voice came through the radio.

“Katase-san, more people are waiting outside.  
Please come back quickly.”

“Wait just a little longer!”

Katase replied.

Three seconds.

Four seconds.

And then—

Gooooooong!

Deep inside the facility, a huge blower began to turn.

The ducts in the wall shook.

Outside air rushed in from the waiting station vents.

“It moved!”

Haru shouted.

Yuto’s phone showed a change.

“Emergency ventilation: manual activation confirmed”  
“Carbon dioxide concentration: beginning to decrease”

“Yes!”

But at the same time, another value jumped.

“Waiting station: 21.1°C → 23.8°C”  
“Shared cooling line load: 83% → 87%”

“The load went up because outside air came in.”

Nagi said.

“But the air is coming back.”

“What about the refrigerated side?”

Katase asked.

The display data came through the radio.

“Temporary storage space: 7.8°C”

Katase’s expression froze.

“It’s close to the limit.”

“If we could lower V-3A here…”

Yuto said.

But there was no control response.

The external operator was still pushing V-3A up.

“Venue Mist V-3A: 20% → 24%”

“Who are you…!”

Haru nearly struck the ventilation panel wall with his fist, but stopped himself.

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

An anonymous message.

“Anonymous:  
Don’t chase V-3A.  
The operator is not at the venue.”

“Not at the venue?”

A following message appeared.

“Anonymous:  
Look at the underground wiring room of the old waiting room.  
The ‘master unit’ is there.”

“Underground wiring room…”

Nagi opened the equipment diagram.

“An underground level in the waiting room…?”

She enlarged the display.

The old waiting room machine room.  
Below it was a faint gray line.

“Old maintenance space  
Underground wiring room”

“It really exists.”

Katase said.

“But who is going there now?  
The refrigerated side is at the limit.  
Emergency ventilation has only just started.  
We can’t leave this place.”

Yuto looked at the screen.

Shared cooling line load: 87%.  
Temporary storage space: 7.8°C.  
V-3A: 24%.

And then, the final line of the anonymous message.

“Do not stop the master unit.  
‘Switch it over.’”

“So again, it’s not stop it, but divide it.”

Yuto said quietly.

“Someone… keeps saying the same thing.”

At that moment, Sōma’s tense voice sounded over the waiting station radio.

“Katase-san!  
One refrigerated-designation container has gone red!  
The temperature is rising!”

Katase’s face went pale.

“Which shipment?”

“Medical delivery!  
Scheduled for handoff in ten minutes—”

A warning sounded on the other side of the radio.

Beep beep beep beep!

Yuto’s phone vibrated again.

“Limited challenge: final emergency condition detected”

“Maintain the temperature of the medical delivery container and  
resolve the control conflict at the shared cooling line master unit at the same time”

And below it, a new number.

“Estimated additional reward on success: 0.150 CC”

“…0.150.”

Yuto looked at the number.

But he immediately closed the screen.

“The reward comes later.”

He looked at Haru, Nagi, and Katase.

“We protect the medical delivery container.  
Meanwhile, we find the master unit and stop the external operation… no, switch it over.”

Katase slowly nodded.

“There’s no time.”

Yuto said.

“So this time, we don’t move in three locations. We move in four.”

The waiting station.  
The temporary storage space.  
The venue mist.  
The old waiting room’s underground wiring room.

The true night of the shared cooling line had only just begun.

## Afterword

In Episode 56, the team successfully restored the waiting station’s emergency ventilation manually. However, the cost was that the cooling load rose due to outside-air intake, and the temperature in the temporary storage space climbed to 7.8°C.

The key points this time were as follows.

- The delivery workers were temporarily evacuated, prioritizing the safety of the people in the waiting station
- Emergency ventilation had been externally locked, but Katase manually activated it through her responsibility as the site supervisor
- Carbon dioxide concentration began to decrease, but because outside air entered, the shared cooling line load rose to 87%
- Venue Mist V-3A was raised to 24% by the external operator, cutting into the refrigerated side’s margin
- An anonymous message revealed that the “master unit,” the center of external control, is in the old waiting room’s underground wiring room
- The medical delivery container’s temperature approached the danger zone, creating a new final emergency condition and the possibility of an additional 0.150 CC

Even after seeing the large reward display of 0.150 CC, Yuto first chose to protect the medical delivery container.  
Earning CC to rise upward and not breaking someone’s important cargo or work—balancing those two things is becoming Yuto’s way of winning.

In the next episode, the team launches a four-location operation to save both the “master unit” in the old waiting room’s underground wiring room and the medical delivery container whose temperature continues to rise.  
Is the sender of the anonymous messages truly an ally? What does it mean to “switch” the master unit rather than “stop” it? The night shared cooling line arc is finally entering its core.
