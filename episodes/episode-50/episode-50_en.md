# Episode 50: The Five Minutes of Reconnection

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 50 closes the first half of the old waiting room limited challenge.  
With only a little time left until overload, can Yuto and the others protect the venue mist while reconnecting the old waiting room as a place people can truly use? As a milestone worthy of Episode 50, this episode depicts a major turning point and opens the door to the coming night.

---

## Main Text

“Shared cooling line load: 89%”  
“Time until overload: 6 minutes 57 seconds”

In front of the old waiting room, Yuto gripped his phone tightly.

The venue mist had already entered its rest period.  
The users had moved under the roof of the waiting room, somehow enduring the heat with fans and simple chairs borrowed from vendors.

But the load was still high.

“What else do we need to reduce?”

Yuto asked.

Nagi, with the thermometer still pressed against the piping, answered briefly.

“The venue-side output has dropped.  
If it’s still 89%, either the indoor side of the waiting room is running halfway, or another connection destination is using it heavily.”

“The night delivery waiting station?”

“That is very possible.”

Haru looked toward the entrance of the old waiting room.

“But even if we go there now, we won’t make it in time.  
We have to do what we can here.”

“Do we shut down the old waiting room completely?”

Sōma said.

“That would lower the load, right?”

“Wait.”

Yuto stopped him.

“If we simply shut it down, we may lose the evaluation for reconnecting the waiting room as a resting base.  
And inside, it’s cooler than outside. If we can make this place usable, we won’t have to run the mist at full output all the time.”

“But the machine room is dangerous.”

“We may not need to touch the machine room.”

Yuto opened the old operation record they had found earlier.

“When using the waiting room, switch external misting to low output.”

Below that, there was a faded note.

“Use manual ventilation together with indoor cooling.”

“Manual ventilation…”

Yuto looked at the wall of the waiting room.

Near the top, old ventilation windows were lined up.  
Some were still closed.  
Some were half open.

“Maybe the waiting room cooling is unstable because ventilation has stopped?”

“Even if the cooling device is running, efficiency drops if the air isn’t circulating.”

Nagi said.

“And that also puts a load on the device.”

“Then we open the ventilation windows.”

Sōma immediately tried to move.

“Wait.”

Haru grabbed his arm.

“They’re high up. We don’t even have a stepladder.  
Don’t climb recklessly.”

The remaining time was 6 minutes 12 seconds.

Yuto looked around.

The flea market venue.  
Tent poles.  
Long poles.  
Vendors.  
The secondhand clothing shop owner who had helped them earlier.

“Pole!”

Yuto ran.

“You’re borrowing something again?”

Haru’s voice flew after him.

“I am! And if there’s a stepladder, that would be perfect!”

At the edge of the venue, the clothing shop owner laughed as soon as he saw Yuto.

“What is it this time?”

“I want to open the ventilation windows of the waiting room!”

“First a fan, now windows?  
You’re busy today.”

“Just for today, please!”

“Take them!”

The owner handed him a folding stepladder and an extendable pole.

“Just don’t do anything reckless!”

“Yes!”

When Yuto returned, Haru was already standing under the ventilation windows.

“I’ll go up.”

“By yourself?”

“You hold the stepladder from below.  
Nagi checks how far the windows open.  
Sōma watches from the entrance to see whether air is flowing inside.”

“Got it.”

The four of them, who had been strangers not long ago, no longer hesitated.

Haru climbed the stepladder.  
Yuto held it from below.  
Nagi directed the angle of the pole.  
Sōma looked from the entrance toward the back of the waiting room.

“The first one will work!”

Click.

The ventilation window opened.

A little cool air flowed out from inside the waiting room.

“Shared cooling line load: 86%”

“It dropped!”

Yuto’s voice rose.

“One more!”

“Hurry, five minutes left!”

Haru extended the pole toward the second window.

But the window lever was rusted in place.

“It won’t move!”

“If you force it, it’ll break!”

Nagi shouted.

“Push from the side! Don’t pull it. Lift it a little!”

“Got it!”

Haru changed the direction of the pole.

Creak, creak…

The sound of metal scraping.

And then—

Click.

The second window opened.

Air began to flow inside the waiting room.

A cool wind passed between the old benches and moved toward the entrance.

The people waiting under the roof looked a little surprised.

“Is it cool inside?”

“The wind came through.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Waiting room ventilation assistance confirmed”  
“Indoor cooling efficiency is increasing”

“Shared cooling line load: 81%”

“We can do this…!”

Two windows remained.

But the third window was positioned too high.

Even with the stepladder extended as far as possible, it would not reach.

“I’ll lend you my shoulders.”

Sōma said.

“Dangerous.”

Haru replied at once.

“But we’re out of time!”

“Time until overload: 3 minutes 49 seconds”

Yuto looked up.

The third window.  
Just a little more.

But if they took a reckless chance on top of the stepladder, someone could fall.

After prioritizing safety all this time, would they really make a dangerous gamble here?

“…We don’t do that.”

Yuto said.

Sōma turned around.

“What?”

“We don’t stand on shoulders.  
We find another way.”

“Do we have time for that?”

“We create the method we need.”

Yuto ran his eyes over the surroundings.

The vendor tents.  
Long aluminum poles.  
Hooks used to fasten cloth.  
And the extendable pole from earlier.

“Can we connect two poles?”

The clothing shop owner shouted from a little distance away.

“We can! There’s a connector adapter!”

“Please lend it to us!”

The owner ran over.

“You really mean to bring this waiting room back, don’t you?”

“Yes!”

“Then do it to the end!”

They connected the two poles.

Because it was longer, it flexed more easily.  
If they mishandled it, they could break the window.

Haru climbed halfway up the stepladder and supported the base of the pole.  
Yuto supported the middle.  
Nagi watched the angle of the tip, and Sōma checked the change in airflow from the entrance.

“Left, just a little more!”

“It’ll bend more than this!”

“Just a little!”

“Time until overload: 2 minutes 31 seconds”

The tip of the pole touched the third window’s lever.

But even when they pushed, it did not move.

“It’s stuck!”

Yuto’s hands tightened.

“Yuto, don’t use too much force!”

Haru shouted.

At that moment.

A wind blew back from inside the waiting room.

Because the first two windows had opened, the air inside shifted slightly, changing the pressure on the frame of the third window.

The lever lifted just a little.

“Now!”

Nagi’s voice.

Yuto did not push. He slid it sideways.

Click.

The third window opened.

Vmmmm…

From the back of the waiting room, a low, steady sound echoed.

The irregular vibration of the cooling device gradually settled.

The red display on the phone changed to yellow.

“Shared cooling line load: 74%”  
“Overload avoidance confirmed”

“We did it…!”

Yuto nearly sank to the ground.

Applause rose from the people waiting under the roof.

Someone said,

“Can we go inside?”

Minato checked the entrance of the waiting room and looked toward the rescue team and the manager.  
The safety check was not complete.  
But it seemed the path and the benches near the entrance had been judged usable.

“For short-term use only, we will open the benches near the entrance.  
Please do not approach the machine room side.”

At those words, people slowly entered the waiting room.

Old benches.  
Opened ventilation windows.  
A slowly flowing cool wind.

A mother with a child sat on a bench near the entrance.

“It’s cool here.”

“Yeah.”

The child smiled softly.

Watching that scene, Yuto felt heat rise deep in his chest.

A waiting room on the edge of town that no one had been using.  
Now, it had once again become a place where someone could rest.

His phone vibrated.

“Limited challenge: first condition achieved”  
“Old waiting room: reconnected as a short-term resting base”

“Joint contribution reward provisionally granted.  
+0.058 CC”

“0.058…”

Yuto stared at the number.

It was clearly different from the small additional rewards he had earned before.

“Safety route contribution: +0.012 CC”  
“User flow improvement: +0.009 CC”

“Today’s provisional earnings: 0.079 CC”

Yuto’s cumulative total jumped.

“Cumulative total: 0.862 CC”

“…That came all at once.”

Haru said, slightly out of breath.

“It’s still provisional.  
If we fail the second condition, part of it may be reduced.”

“I know.”

Yuto did not close the screen.

A new display had appeared there.

“Second condition: confirm the load at the night delivery waiting station”  
“Available start time: today, 19:00”  
“Additional estimated reward: 0.100 CC”

Below that, a small red message appeared.

“Warning:  
The night delivery waiting station is currently in use.  
Unauthorized cooling adjustment will affect users’ working environment.”

Haru looked at the display.

“Tonight will be harder.”

“Checking the cooling line load in a place people are using.”

Nagi said.

“And those people will be working.”

Sōma looked into the waiting room with a different expression than before.

“Next time, I won’t charge in first.”

“Really?”

Yuto asked.

“I already failed once.”

“Then we cooperate?”

Sōma was silent for a moment.

“…If the reward split isn’t bad.”

“You still say that part.”

“You want to rise, don’t you?  
Both you and me.”

Yuto smiled a little.

“That’s true.  
But if we break the places where we can earn, we won’t rise.”

The afternoon light shone into the waiting room through the opened ventilation windows.

The old building had not fully returned yet.  
Even so, today, it was certainly letting someone rest again.

At that moment, a message arrived on Yuto’s phone from an unknown number.

“If you’re going to the night delivery waiting station, be careful.  
That place is ‘too cold.’”

The sender was unknown.

Then another message followed.

“If you stop it the wrong way, tonight’s deliveries will stop.”

Yuto stared at the screen.

Who had sent this?  
Why did they know about the waiting station?

And what did “too cold” mean?

The clock had just passed four in the afternoon.

Three hours remained until night.

## Afterword

Episode 50 succeeded in reconnecting the old waiting room as a place where people can actually rest.  
Instead of merely stopping the overload, Yuto and the others revived the manual ventilation described in the old operation records, lowering the cooling line load to 74%.

The flow this time was as follows.

- Setup: With only a little time left before overload, they need to improve the cooling efficiency of the waiting room
- Development: From the old operation records, they find a way to use manual ventilation
- Turn: Faced with a ventilation window that is too high, they choose a safe method using connected poles instead of a dangerous one
- Ending: They achieve the first condition for the old waiting room, and Yuto provisionally earns 0.079 CC. However, a suspicious warning arrives concerning the night delivery waiting station

In Episode 50, Yuto reaches a cumulative total of 0.862 CC.  
It is still not a number that can be called a major rise, but it is a clear step forward: someone who began with small daily CC earnings has started producing results in a 0.050 CC-class limited challenge.

From the next episode, the night delivery waiting station becomes the stage.  
What is this “too cold” waiting station? Who sent the anonymous message? And will the chance for an additional 0.100 CC truly push Yuto upward? A new contest begins on the edge of town at night.
