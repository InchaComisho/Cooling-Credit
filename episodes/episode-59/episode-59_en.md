# Episode 59: Backup Circulation on the Rooftop

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 59 follows Yuto and Nagi as they head to the old waiting room’s rooftop external valve to stop the medical delivery container’s temperature from rising.  
If they can activate the backup circulation route, they may be able to protect both the occupied waiting station and the medical delivery. But because the supposedly nonexistent approver “R-01” is intervening in the same route, the final few minutes become an unpredictable contest.

---

## Main Text

“Medical delivery container: 9.4°C”  
“Until allowable upper limit: 00:04:56”

The underground wiring room beneath the old waiting room.

The moment Yuto saw the display, he started running.

“To the rooftop!”

Nagi grabbed the light and followed him.

The underground stairs.  
The machine room.  
The dim hallway.  
The benches near the entrance.

During the day, people had rested in this waiting room. Now only the night wind passed through it.

“Where are the stairs to the rooftop?”

Yuto shouted.

“Outside!  
There should be an emergency staircase behind the machine room!”

Nagi enlarged the old drawing on her phone.

4 minutes and 31 seconds remained.

Yuto rushed out of the waiting room.

The night air was hot.  
But because he had been running, the heat inside his chest was even stronger.

Behind the building.

A rusted emergency staircase came into view.

“Authorized personnel only”

There was no time to worry about that sign.

“Helmet!”

Nagi said.

“Put it on!”

Yuto put on the helmet he had borrowed from the guard during the day.

Nagi did the same, and the two of them began climbing the stairs.

Clang.  
Clang.

The old iron staircase rang under their feet.

“Medical delivery container: 9.5°C”  
“Until allowable upper limit: 00:04:02”

“It’s rising…”

“Don’t look. Run!”

Nagi’s voice.

“Right now, watch your footing before the numbers!”

Yuto nodded and grabbed the handrail.

The door leading to the rooftop was half open.

Wind blew through.

Bang.

The door swung hard.

“Did someone get here first?”

Yuto stopped.

On the rooftop were old outdoor units.  
Ventilation ducts.  
Rusted pipes.  
And in the center, a thick valve.

“Backup Circulation  
External Manual Valve”

Beside it stood a figure.

Black work clothes.  
A hood pulled low.

His back was turned as he stood before the valve.

“Who are you?”

Yuto shouted.

The figure did not turn around.

In his hand was a small terminal.

On its screen, a red display was showing.

“External approver: R-01”

“R-01…”

Nagi said quietly.

“Could he really exist?”

The figure slowly turned around.

An old protective mask covered his face.  
Only his eyes could be seen.

“You’re late.”

The man’s voice was low.

“Soma Yuto.”

Yuto held his breath.

“You know me?”

“How could I not know the player who shook this line again and again tonight?”

“Are you the one who shook it?”

“No.”

The man turned his eyes back toward the valve.

“I am trying to restore it.”

“Restore it?”

“The shared cooling line was originally meant to prioritize refrigerated transport above all else.  
The comfort of the waiting station and the venue was nothing more than a secondary use.”

Yuto’s heart pounded hard.

“So you’re saying it’s fine if the delivery workers are cold?”

“If they are cold, they can wear jackets.  
But if medical transport temperature control fails, it cannot be undone.”

“That’s why you stopped even the waiting station ventilation?”

The man’s eyes narrowed slightly.

“The optimization server made that judgment.  
I merely supported it.”

“Supported it?”

“I am R-01.  
The second approver of this line.  
Sakakibara built the line for the field. I have maintained it to protect logistics.”

“You were on the same team as Sakakibara-san?”

“In the past.”

R-01 placed his hand on the valve.

“If the backup circulation route is opened, the medical container’s temperature can be lowered.  
But in exchange, cooling on the waiting-station side will drop.  
I believe that is acceptable.”

“There are people in the waiting station.”

“They have evacuated for now.”

“They can’t stay evacuated forever.”

“Then will you take responsibility?”

R-01’s voice sharpened.

“When the medical delivery fails.  
When the cargo is ruined.  
When delivery is delayed and something fails to reach someone.  
Will you say ‘human occupancy matters too’ and take responsibility?”

Yuto could not answer.

The medical delivery container.

It might contain something needed for someone’s treatment.  
It might contain something needed for testing.  
If it failed, it really might be impossible to undo.

But.

“…Cutting off one side is not the only form of responsibility.”

Yuto said.

“That is why we use the backup circulation route.  
We leave the waiting station’s normal system intact and send cooling to the medical container through another route.”

“Idealism.”

“We haven’t tested whether it works yet.”

“It is an old route.  
Open it fully, and it will take cooling from the waiting-station side.  
Open it halfway, and it will not reach the medical container.”

“Then we don’t choose full open or zero. We adjust while measuring.”

Nagi stepped forward.

“We open it in stages while checking flow rate and temperature.  
We watch the waiting station temperature, the temporary storage temperature, and the medical container temperature at the same time.”

R-01 looked at Nagi.

“There is no time.”

“Medical delivery container: 9.6°C”  
“Until allowable upper limit: 00:02:51”

Everyone looked at the screen.

“Two minutes and fifty-one seconds.”

R-01 said.

“There is no time to measure as you go.”

“We’ll make it in time.”

Yuto looked at the small flow meter beside the valve.

The needle pointed to zero.

Below it were faded markings.

“Low output: 1–3”  
“Auxiliary circulation: 4–6”  
“Emergency full open: 7–10”

“Auxiliary circulation…”

Nagi checked the drawing.

“If we stay between four and six, we may be able to send auxiliary cooling to the refrigerated section without completely cutting down the waiting-station side.”

“Everything is ‘may’ with you.”

R-01 said.

“But with your full-open plan, we also don’t know what will happen to the waiting-station side.”

Yuto looked at R-01.

“I don’t want to protect someone by placing someone else outside the numbers anymore.”

The wind blew.

The rooftop pipes gave a low sound.

R-01 stared at Yuto.

Eventually, he held the terminal toward Yuto.

“Then you turn it.”

“What?”

“I will give approval.  
But you operate the valve.  
Your name will remain in the result log.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Backup circulation route: temporary operation authority granted”

“Approver: R-01”  
“Operator: Soma Yuto”

“Operation record will be reflected in Cooling Credit evaluation”

0.150 CC.

The words crossed his mind.

If this operation succeeded, the reward would be large.

If it failed, someone’s medical delivery would be affected.

“…Understood.”

Yuto reached for the valve.

Cold metal.

Nagi watched the terminal and said:

“Stop at one.  
First, one.”

“One.”

Yuto slowly turned the valve.

Creak…

The needle moved from zero to one.

“Backup circulation: 1”

Katase’s voice came through the communication line.

“Medical container is still 9.6.  
No change yet.”

“Two.”

Yuto turned it farther.

“Backup circulation: 2”

“Waiting station: 22.7°C”  
“Medical delivery container: 9.5°C”

“It went down!”

Sōma’s voice.

“Only a little!”

“Three.”

“Backup circulation: 3”

“Medical delivery container: 9.4°C”  
“Waiting station: 22.5°C”

“It’s working.”

Nagi said.

“But it’s slow.”

“Until allowable upper limit: 00:01:48”

“To four.”

Yuto said.

R-01 watched with sharp eyes.

“Four is auxiliary circulation.”

The valve grew heavier.

Yuto put both hands into it.

Creeeak…

“Backup circulation: 4”

At that moment, the rooftop pipes shook hard.

Gong!

“Careful!”

Nagi shouted.

A little white vapor sprayed from beneath the valve.

Yuto did not let go.

“Temperature!”

“Medical delivery container: 9.2°C!  
It’s going down!”

“Waiting station: 22.1°C”

“It’ll work!”

But a warning appeared on R-01’s terminal.

“Shared cooling line: backup circulation route pressure rising”

“The pressure is rising too much.”

R-01 said.

“If you go to five, it will burden the pipes.”

“But at four, we won’t make it in time.”

“Until allowable upper limit: 00:00:58”

Yuto listened to the other side of the communication line.

The medical container alarm.  
Sōma’s voice guiding people at the waiting station.  
Haru’s brief report.

“Venue V-3A holding at 8%. We can’t lower it any farther.”

Everything was converging on the rooftop valve.

If he turned it to five, they might save the container.  
But if the pipes broke, the shared cooling line would stop.

At that moment, Sakakibara’s voice came through the line.

“Yuto.  
Do not turn the valve to five.”

“But there are only fifty seconds left!”

“If you turn it to five, the backup route will hold.  
But the low-output cooling of the waiting room will drop.  
The ventilation assist beneath the rooftop will stop.”

“Then what do we do?”

“Keep it at four and lower V-3A to six.”

“V-3A?”

“The venue is unmanned.  
There is no need to maintain eight.  
At six, the pressure escape path remains.  
That margin can be sent to the medical side.”

R-01 immediately shook his head.

“If you lower the venue mist to six, the master unit will conflict again.”

“But right now, Yuto and the others have the master unit held.”

Nagi said.

“It is worth trying.”

Yuto opened the communication line.

“Haru!”

“I’m listening.  
I’ll open the manual box.  
I’ll lower V-3A to six.”

“Is it safe?”

“The seal has release approval.  
There are no people here.  
I’ll do it.”

“Please!”

“Until allowable upper limit: 00:00:31”

R-01 watched the terminal.

His finger was over the emergency stop button.

“If it fails, I return it to full open.”

“Understood.”

Yuto held the valve at four.

“Nagi-san, pressure!”

“Barely.  
But it isn’t red yet!”

Twenty seconds.

“V-3A: 8% → 7%”

Haru’s voice.

“Almost there!”

“Medical delivery container: 9.1°C”

“It’s going down!”

Ten seconds.

“V-3A: 7% → 6%”

At the same time.

“Backup circulation route: auxiliary flow increasing”  
“Medical delivery container: 8.9°C”

The alarm stopped.

“Allowable upper limit avoided”

For several seconds, silence fell over the rooftop.

Yuto could not take his hand off the valve.

Nagi slowly exhaled.

“…We protected it.”

Over the communication line, Sōma shouted:

“Medical container, 8.8!  
It’s still going down!”

Katase’s voice.

“Refrigerated side stable.  
The waiting station is holding around 22 degrees too.”

Haru’s voice.

“V-3A fixed at six.  
The venue is quiet.”

Yuto looked up at the night sky.

His clothes clung to him with sweat.  
His hands trembled from the cold metal.

Even so, there was a definite feeling deep in his chest.

“…We protected both.”

For a while, R-01 said nothing.

Eventually, he removed his protective mask.

He was a man in his late fifties or so.

A tired face.  
But his eyes were straight.

“It was not idealism.”

He looked at Yuto.

“You watched the numbers, listened to the field, and moved the system.  
If that is how you do it, I will acknowledge it.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Backup circulation route: activation successful”  
“Final emergency condition: progress 94%”

“94%?”

Yuto looked at the screen.

“Unfinished condition:  
Select a permanent operator for the shared cooling line”

“Permanent operator…”

R-01’s expression changed.

“It still isn’t over.”

Sakakibara’s voice entered the line.

“Yuto.  
Tonight’s emergency response succeeded.  
But who will operate the line, and under what priorities, has not been decided.”

“Sakakibara-san.”

“Neither I nor R-01 can run the field anymore.  
That is why the master unit is now asking for a new operator.”

A large display appeared on Yuto’s screen.

“Shared Cooling Line  
Provisional Operator Selection”

“Candidates:  
Soma Yuto  
Haru  
Nagi  
Sōma  
Katase  
R-01”

“Selection deadline: 00:09:59”

On the night rooftop, Yuto held his breath.

A reward beyond 0.150 CC.

And perhaps something larger than that: the position of operator.

But was that a chance to rise upward?

Or was it a chair too heavy, carrying someone’s life and safety?

## Afterword

In Episode 59, Yuto and Nagi operated the old waiting room’s rooftop external valve and activated the backup circulation route.  
Rather than making the medical delivery container and the waiting station compete for cooling, they lowered Venue Mist V-3A to 6% and stabilized backup circulation at 4, successfully protecting both.

The key points this time were as follows.

- R-01 was the second approver of the shared cooling line and someone who had prioritized logistics cooling
- Yuto chose staged backup circulation in order to protect not only the medical delivery but also the safety of the people in the waiting station
- Raising backup circulation to 4 caused the medical delivery container’s temperature to begin dropping, but also brought a risk of rising pipe pressure
- At Sakakibara’s suggestion, they lowered Venue Mist V-3A from 8% to 6% and sent that margin to the medical side
- The medical delivery container avoided its allowable upper limit, and the waiting station also maintained around 22°C
- The final emergency condition advanced to 94%, but a new final condition appeared: “Select a permanent operator for the shared cooling line”

This time, with a 0.150 CC-class reward at stake, Yuto succeeded in the most difficult form of balancing two needs.  
However, this is not the end. The shared cooling line has only been protected through emergency response. It remains undecided who will set priorities and operate it responsibly from now on.

In the next episode, provisional operator selection begins.  
Will Yuto himself become the operator? Will Haru, Nagi, Katase, R-01, or someone else who knows the field take the role? Will he take a major chance to rise upward, or protect his freedom to keep earning as a player? For Yuto, it becomes the heaviest choice so far.
