# Episode 63: North Seventh Wharf at 6:00 a.m.

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 63 is the first day of the North Seventh Wharf preliminary observation pass.  
At 6:00 a.m., Yuto enters the site ahead of the upper players and tries to visit the four operational reduction candidates. But waiting for him at the first rest building is a competing player who has already begun moving forward with a decision to cut.

---

## Main Text

5:58 a.m.

North Seventh Wharf, East Gate.

Soma Yuto looked up at the enormous container cranes.

Even though it was still morning, the harbor was already moving.

The low sound of engines.  
The warning beeps of forklifts.  
The metallic sound of containers being lifted.  
A damp wind blowing in from the sea.

And it was already hot.

“…This early, and it’s already like this?”

On his phone, the North Seventh Wharf preliminary observation pass was displayed.

“Time until start: 00:01:47”

“Observation targets: 4 locations”  
“A: Third Worker Rest Building”  
“B: Water Supply Spot for Waiting Vehicles”  
“C: Harbor Outer Perimeter Shaded Bench Area”  
“D: Nearby Residential District Simple Evacuation Space”

“Competing players: detected”

Yuto looked at the map beside the gate.

North Seventh Wharf was wide.

A, the rest building, was close to the center of the container yard.  
B, the water supply spot, was near the line of waiting vehicles.  
C, the shaded benches, were along the outer harbor walkway.  
D, the evacuation space, was outside the harbor at the entrance to the residential district.

Just visiting all of them would take time.

And there were competing players too.

“First, A.”

The Third Worker Rest Building.

The first place FROST-9 had listed as a reduction candidate.

It was not a large facility.  
It was a simple building where workers could rest for short periods.

If one thought only about cooling efficiency across the whole area, it would be easy to cut.

But if workers used it, perhaps it should not be cut.

“North Seventh Wharf preliminary observation pass started”

6:00 a.m.

His phone sounded.

“Observation log started”

Yuto began running.

◇

The Third Worker Rest Building stood in the shadow of stacked containers.

A small building like a prefab structure.  
A helmet rack for workers at the entrance.  
An exhaust vent for the cooling unit on the wall.  
Voices could already be heard from inside.

But.

A white temporary fence had been set up in front of the entrance.

“Starting today at 9:00  
Cooling output scheduled to be reduced to 40%”

“It’s already started…”

As Yuto approached, the person standing beside the fence turned around.

Short reddish-brown hair.  
A black cooling vest.  
A terminal with a red band at the neck.

“You’re late.”

Yuto looked at the display on that person’s terminal.

“HEATWAVE-3”

Third in the metropolitan rankings.

“You’re HEATWAVE-3?”

“That’s right.  
You’re Soma Yuto.”

“You know me?”

“There’s no way I wouldn’t know the newcomer FROST-9 brought in.”

HEATWAVE-3 pointed at the fence around the rest building.

“This place drops to 40% at nine.  
If we don’t create margin by noon, the entire harbor won’t hold.”

“You’ve already decided?”

“I have.”

“Did you ask the people on site?”

“I did.”

HEATWAVE-3 answered without hesitation.

“Morning use is low.  
Before the post-nine peak, they can be guided to the nearby Second Rest Building.  
Four minutes on foot.  
If it’s hot, we’ll increase water supply along the transfer route.”

“Four minutes…”

At midday, four minutes was not short.

“It is morning now.”

HEATWAVE-3 looked at Yuto.

“But the forecast says the perceived temperature will exceed forty degrees by eleven.  
What will you do if keeping this place at 100% causes a larger rest building to go down then?”

It was hard to argue back.

HEATWAVE-3’s decision was not simply cold.

A destination had been prepared, and extra water supply would be added.

Instead of simply cutting it, a minimum alternative had been created.

“Then let me watch how it is used a little more before it goes to 40%.”

Yuto said.

“If the reduction starts at nine, I can check how many morning workers use it and where the movement routes flow.”

“And what changes if you check?”

“If there are people who cannot go to the Second Rest Building, I’ll think of a way not to lower this place completely.”

“For example?”

“Leave only short-use cooling.  
Keep cooling near the entrance.  
Create priority seats for people who cannot move.  
Or divide how the same output is used.”

HEATWAVE-3 smiled slightly.

“Just as FROST-9 said.”

“What did she say?”

“That you bring ‘ways to keep things’ into places marked for cutting.”

“…What exactly has FROST-9 been telling people?”

“Not everything.  
But I heard you are the player who created collaborative operation.”

HEATWAVE-3 opened part of the fence.

“Go look.  
However, the nine o’clock reduction does not change.  
If you want to change it, show me by nine, in numbers, who cannot be moved to the Second Rest Building.”

“In numbers?”

“This is a wide-area project.  
Not impressions. Give me the number of people, the time, and the travel distance.”

“Understood.”

Yuto entered the rest building.

◇

Inside, it was already somewhat crowded.

Benches along the wall.  
A water dispenser.  
Small vents blowing cool air.  
A work schedule posted on the wall.

Workers from the container yard were taking short breaks.

Some had removed their helmets and were drinking water.  
Some sat with their eyes closed.  
Some checked the next instruction on their phones.

Yuto spoke to a worker near the entrance.

“Excuse me.  
This rest building is scheduled to have its cooling lowered to 40% starting at nine…”

“I know.”

The man wiped away sweat as he spoke.

“They told us at the morning meeting.”

“There’s a plan to move people to the Second Rest Building.”

“The Second?”

The man gave a bitter smile.

“For people who can walk, sure.”

“There are people who can’t?”

“People with bad legs.  
People who’ve hurt their backs doing cargo work.  
People who are weak in the heat but come in anyway because they lose daily pay if they rest.”

Yuto took notes.

“How many are there?”

“Among the people who rest here, maybe three or four each time.”

“After nine as well?”

“More after nine, if anything.”

Another female worker joined the conversation.

“The Second Rest Building is far, but the real problem is that the path there is in direct sun.  
They say four minutes, but you have to go around between containers. If you’re carrying something, it takes longer.”

“I heard they may increase water supply.”

“Water helps.  
But it’s different from having a place to rest nearby.”

Yuto looked outside the entrance.

In the direction of the Second Rest Building, there was indeed a broad container yard.  
There was little shade.  
Large vehicles passed through as well.

Four minutes for someone who could walk.  
But for people who were tired, in pain, or carrying something, it was different.

“Thank you.”

Yuto made one round inside the room.

There were fourteen users just during the six o’clock hour.  
Four of them mentioned problems with their legs or backs.  
Two said they had a history of heatstroke.  
There were also people who sat for only a short time and immediately returned to the field.

A small rest building.

But it was not just margin.

“…A lot of short-time use.”

Yuto checked the log.

“Third Rest Building  
Average stay: 8 minutes 12 seconds”

Few people stayed long.

Even so, those eight minutes of cooling were recovery time for returning to the field.

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Observation log: location A  
User information confirmed”

“Until priority proposal right:  
Field alternative plan required”

“There’s still B, C, and D.”

He checked the time.

7:22 a.m.

There was more than an hour and a half until nine.

To see every location, he had to hurry.

As Yuto headed for the exit, HEATWAVE-3 was waiting.

“Got the numbers?”

“Some.”

“Conclusion?”

“I’m not saying you should cancel the 40% reduction yet.”

HEATWAVE-3 looked slightly surprised.

“But I want to preserve short-time cooling near the entrance.  
Some users have difficulty moving to the Second Rest Building.”

“How many?”

“Four at this point in the morning.  
It’s likely to increase after nine.”

“Possibility won’t pass.”

“That’s why I’ll look at the next location and include the alternative movement route in the proposal.”

For a while, HEATWAVE-3 looked at Yuto.

“You have until nine.  
If you don’t submit a plan by then, it goes to 40% as scheduled.”

“Understood.”

Yuto ran off.

Next was B.

The Water Supply Spot for Waiting Vehicles.

◇

7:48 a.m.

The Water Supply Spot for Waiting Vehicles.

It was a place where delivery vehicles entering the harbor formed a line.

Large trucks.  
Light delivery vehicles.  
Refrigerated trucks.  
Drivers waited for their turns inside their vehicles.

The water supply spot was under a simple tent.

Two water dispensers.  
A small mist nozzle.  
Six folding chairs.

But another player was already there.

A gray jacket.  
Thin-framed sunglasses.  
A pale blue display on the terminal.

“RIVER-EDGE”

Fifth in the metropolitan rankings.

RIVER-EDGE stood in front of the water dispensers, looking at the tent roof.

“Soma Yuto.”

He called Yuto by name before Yuto could approach.

“Yes.”

“You’re late.”

“Everyone says that.”

“If your rank is lower, you have no choice but to arrive earlier.”

RIVER-EDGE showed the display for the water supply spot.

“Starting today at 12:00  
Mist operation will stop  
Water dispensers reduced to one unit”

“You’re cutting this too?”

“I am.”

“Why?”

“Cooling efficiency is poor.  
The mist disperses because of vehicle exhaust heat.  
Waiting vehicles can use air conditioning.  
Water is also available at the nearby management building.”

“Did you ask the drivers?”

“I did.”

RIVER-EDGE looked toward the line of vehicles.

“Most people who use this place only use the water.  
The chairs are hardly used.  
The mist is merely pleasant.”

“So you think reducing it won’t be a problem?”

“That is my view.”

Yuto looked under the tent.

Indeed, the chairs were empty.

But then.

An elderly driver slowly climbed down from the driver’s seat of a large truck.

He was holding his back.

He took water from the dispenser and sat in a chair.

He closed his eyes while sitting.

Eight minutes.  
Ten minutes.

Eventually, the man stood up and cooled the back of his neck under the mist.

RIVER-EDGE’s expression did not change even after seeing that.

“There are exceptions.”

“It may not be an exception.”

Yuto looked at the vehicle line.

Some vehicles waited with their air conditioning on.  
Some had their engines off and windows open to save fuel.  
Some drivers stretched outside their vehicles.

“Not everyone can use air conditioning.  
The longer they wait, the more they worry about fuel and turn it off.”

“Then they can go to the management building.”

“Can a large truck leave the waiting line and then return to it?”

RIVER-EDGE narrowed his eyes slightly.

“I have not checked that far.”

For the first time, Yuto spoke a little more firmly.

“This is not just a ‘water supply spot.’  
It is a place where people who cannot leave the waiting line recover their bodies for a short time.”

“Can you prove it?”

“I will.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Competing player: RIVER-EDGE  
Pre-registration of reduction proposal detected”

“Location B: time until operational reduction: 00:39:58”

“Forty minutes…”

RIVER-EDGE put away his terminal.

“If you can prove it, I’ll withdraw the proposal.  
If you cannot, the mist stops and the water dispensers go down to one.”

“Understood.”

Yuto headed toward the vehicle line.

Time remaining.

About one hour until A was reduced.  
Forty minutes until B was reduced.

He still had not seen C or D.

At that moment, a new notification appeared on his phone.

“Competing player MIST CROW  
has submitted a complete shutdown plan  
for location D, the Nearby Residential District Simple Evacuation Space”

“Time until decision for location D: 00:29:42”

“…Three at once?”

Yuto looked at the map of North Seventh Wharf.

A.  
B.  
C.  
D.

He was alone.

There were multiple competitors.

And the reduction proposals would not wait.

At that moment, the collaborative operation chat on his phone vibrated.

“Haru:  
I saw the competing logs at North Seventh Wharf.  
Where are you now?”

“Nagi:  
If you need equipment diagrams, I can check them remotely.  
I’ll send them if needed.”

“Sōma:  
If you need people gathered and interviewed, I’ll come too.  
I’m checking port entry procedures with Katase-san.”

Yuto stared at the screen.

He had not been told to come alone.

FROST-9 had told him to go see the field.

And from the beginning, Yuto’s strength had not been running alone.

“…Everyone, please help.”

Yuto typed.

“Yuto:  
A is the rest building.  
B is the water supply spot.  
D is the evacuation space.  
Competitors are moving reduction plans forward.  
I can’t make it in time alone.”

Replies came immediately.

“Haru:  
I’ll check the route from A to the Second Rest Building.  
I’ll measure why people can’t move.”

“Nagi:  
I’ll build a plan to keep B’s mist at low output.  
I’ll check vehicle exhaust heat and the water dispenser power.”

“Sōma:  
I’ll go to D.  
I’ll talk to residents.  
Before the complete shutdown, I’ll find out who uses it.”

“Katase:  
I’ll contact the port management side.  
I’ll arrange entry as external collaborators.  
But do not overdo it.”

Yuto smiled slightly.

“Can collaborative operation work at the harbor too?”

But the next moment.

A direct message came from FROST-9.

“You were supposed to observe alone.”

Yuto’s finger stopped.

Another message followed below it.

“If you bring in collaborators, the preliminary observation pass may be invalidated.”

Fifty-eight minutes until A was reduced.  
Thirty-seven minutes until B was reduced.  
Twenty-seven minutes until the decision on D’s complete shutdown.

Yuto looked back and forth between the screen of his collaborative operation companions and FROST-9’s warning.

Follow the rules of the upper players.

Or push through with his own method.

The morning at North Seventh Wharf was no longer willing to wait.

## Afterword

In Episode 63, Yuto observes the first two locations at North Seventh Wharf and learns that the upper players have already begun moving forward with operational reductions.

The key points this time were as follows.

- At location A, the Third Worker Rest Building, HEATWAVE-3 is moving ahead with reducing cooling output to 40% starting at 9:00
- Yuto confirmed that while many users rest there for short periods, some people have difficulty moving to the Second Rest Building
- At location B, the Water Supply Spot for Waiting Vehicles, RIVER-EDGE proposes stopping the mist and reducing the water dispensers to one unit
- It becomes clear that some drivers may not be able to use in-vehicle air conditioning to save fuel and may not be able to leave the waiting line
- At location D, the Nearby Residential District Simple Evacuation Space, MIST CROW has submitted a complete shutdown plan, with less than 30 minutes until the decision
- Yuto decides that he cannot make it in time alone and asks Haru, Nagi, Sōma, and Katase from collaborative operation for help
- However, FROST-9 warns him that if he brings in collaborators, the preliminary observation pass may be invalidated

In Episode 63, Yuto faces the harshness of “wide-area project competition” for the first time.  
The competing players’ plans are not simply cruel. HEATWAVE-3 has prepared a transfer destination and water supply, and RIVER-EDGE has also checked usage conditions before submitting a reduction proposal. Even so, they have not fully picked up the smaller difficulties on the field.

In the next episode, Yuto must decide whether to fight alone under FROST-9’s rules or bring in collaborative operation while risking the loss of his preliminary observation pass.  
With only a little time left before the decision to shut down location D completely, Yuto’s choice will affect his very qualification to join the 5.000 CC project at North Seventh Wharf.
