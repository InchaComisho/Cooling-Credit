# Episode 61: An Invitation from FROST-9

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 61 begins the day after the shared cooling line arc ends.  
Yuto has reached a cumulative 1.243 CC and has become eligible for the metropolitan rankings. But what arrives that morning is not a message of congratulations, but a short message from the upper player “FROST-9.” The next stage of his rise quietly opens.

---

## Main Text

Monday, 7:00 a.m.

Soma Yuto woke up on the bed in his apartment.

His body felt heavy.

The previous night.  
The old waiting room.  
The night delivery waiting station.  
The rooftop backup circulation valve.  
The shared cooling line.

He had run, thought, shouted, and made decisions that much.

He had only managed to sleep for a few hours.

“…I want to rest today.”

Yuto buried his face in his pillow.

But his phone vibrated.

“Shared cooling line: previous day log confirmed”

Rubbing his eyes, he looked at the screen.

“Safety routes: normal”  
“Waiting station and medical delivery priority allocation: continuing”  
“Venue Mist V-3A: low-output maintained”  
“Collaborative operation daily pool: 0.050 CC”

“Your previous day contribution allocation: +0.018 CC”

“…0.018.”

After a major limited challenge, it looked small.

But this was not a one-night bonus.  
As long as collaborative operation continued, if he was useful in the field, CC would slowly keep increasing.

“Cumulative total: 1.261 CC”

“It’s stacking up…”

Yuto smiled just a little.

Small improvements.  
Field checks.  
People’s voices.  
Decisions that avoided danger.

That accumulation had crossed 1.0 CC.

But the moment he saw the next notification, all sleepiness vanished.

“New message”

“Sender: FROST-9”

Yuto stared at the screen.

The mysterious upper player who had viewed his log immediately after it was registered as an achievement eligible for the metropolitan rankings.

The message was short.

“I read your collaborative operation log.  
I want to talk tonight.”

Then, location information.

“Meeting place: Central Cooling Terminal  
Time: 20:00”

“Central Cooling Terminal…”

It was in the opposite direction from the edge of town.

A large-scale cooling hub in the center of the metropolitan area.

Yuto scrolled the screen.

“Note:  
You are free to accept or decline the invitation.  
However, reply by noon.”

“By noon… that’s too sudden.”

Yuto got out of bed.

◇

9:00 a.m.

Yuto was sitting on a bench in the station-front plaza.

The usual mist.  
The usual signboard.

“You can rest here”  
“Free of charge”  
“No application required”

Words he had seen many times at the beginning of Part 4.

After finishing a major challenge at the edge of town, this place looked strangely quiet.

“You seem troubled.”

The one who called out to him was WindArc.

Yuto was a little surprised.

“It’s been a while.”

“I heard about the shared cooling line.  
They say you did something quite big at the edge of town.”

“Everything somehow made it in time.”

“That wasn’t just ‘somehow.’”

WindArc sat beside Yuto.

“Reconnecting the old waiting room.  
Readjusting the venue mist.  
Cooling allocation at the night delivery waiting station.  
Launching collaborative operation.  
Among people who saw the field logs, it has become quite a topic.”

“So maybe that’s why this came.”

Yuto showed him FROST-9’s message.

WindArc’s expression changed slightly.

“From FROST-9?”

“You know them?”

“Only the name.”

“Only the name?”

“They are a player who has stayed near the top of the metropolitan rankings for a long time.  
They are said to run multiple large cooling projects individually.”

“…Individually.”

Yuto remembered the operator selection from the night before.

He had not chosen the path of deciding alone.

But FROST-9 ran large projects individually.

“What kind of person are they?”

“Almost no face or real name is public.  
But judging from the ranking records,  
they have earned a lot of CC through wide-area cooling efficiency.”

“Efficiency…”

That word caught slightly in his mind.

R-01.  
The Regional Optimization Server.  
Efficiency that placed human occupancy outside the numbers.

Yuto looked at the screen.

“I want to talk tonight.”

“Should I go?”

WindArc did not answer immediately.

“Whether you should meet is something you decide, Yuto.  
But if you are going to refuse an invitation from an upper player, you should refuse knowing exactly what you are refusing.”

“If it’s just listening to what they have to say.”

“I think that is fine.”

Yuto thought for a while.

He did not know what FROST-9 wanted.

Did they want to praise the shared cooling line?  
Invite him into the world of metropolitan rankings?  
Or fold collaborative operation into one of their own efficient projects?

But rather than fear without knowing.

Meet, see, and judge.

“I’ll go.”

Yuto typed a reply.

“This is Soma Yuto.  
I will go to the Central Cooling Terminal at 20:00.”

Sent.

It was read immediately.

And then came the reply.

“Understood.  
Come alone.”

Yuto’s fingers stopped.

◇

Afternoon.

A new message came into the shared cooling line collaborative operation chat.

“Haru:  
 Daily log checked. No issue on the venue side.  
 V-3A fixed at 6%.”

“Nagi:  
 Waiting room ventilation and low-output cooling are also stable.  
 However, I want to inspect the backup circulation route again tonight.”

“Sōma:  
 Night delivery waiting station is crowded again tonight.  
 Katase-san says she will slightly change how temporary storage is divided.”

“Katase:  
 Medical delivery side has no issue.  
 However, if the temperature rises, readjustment will be necessary.”

Yuto looked at the screen.

Until yesterday, he would have been the one running through the field.

Now, everyone was moving in their own place.

Collaborative operation had properly begun to turn on its first day.

“…There are parts that run even without me.”

It felt a little lonely.  
But also reassuring.

Then he received a direct message from Nagi.

“I heard about you meeting FROST-9.”

“From who?”

“Haru.  
Haru told Sōma.  
Sōma asked Katase-san.  
Katase-san sent it to me.”

“Information moves fast…”

“It’s collaborative operation.  
If you’re going somewhere dangerous alone, share it.”

“Yuto:  
Central Cooling Terminal. 20:00.  
Just talking.”

“Nagi:  
They told you to come alone, didn’t they?”

Yuto was a little surprised.

“Yuto:  
How do you know?”

“Nagi:  
It would be written on your face.”

“Yuto:  
It’s a message, though.”

“Nagi:  
Then it’s written in your wording.”

After a short pause, Nagi sent more.

“I’m not telling you not to go.  
But the Central Cooling Terminal is not a sightseeing facility.  
It is a wide-area equipment adjustment hub.  
If you enter it, decide what you will bring back before you go.”

“What I will bring back…”

Yuto wrote that down.

Information.  
Negotiation.  
A new project.  
Or the way an upper player thinks.

If he could bring nothing back, there was no meaning in meeting.

◇

7:40 p.m.

Central Cooling Terminal.

Yuto got off at the station and looked up at the huge building.

It was incomparable to the old waiting room at the edge of town.

Glass walls.  
Tall cooling towers.  
Thick pipes running overhead.  
Management vehicles coming and going.

A large display stood at the front of the building.

“Metropolitan Cooling Terminal  
Wide-Area Load Adjustment and Emergency Supply Hub”

“…So this is the world of upper players.”

At the entrance, a receptionist holding a terminal was waiting.

“You are Soma Yuto, correct?”

“Yes.”

“FROST-9 is waiting for you.  
This way, please.”

He was not guided to a general waiting area.

Authentication gate.  
Management elevator.  
A control floor lined only with numbers.

Inside the elevator, Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“You have entered a metropolitan ranking target area”

“Player observation intensity: rising”

“Observation intensity?”

It was an unsettling phrase.

The elevator stopped.

The doors opened.

There was a huge control room.

A wall full of monitors.  
A map of the metropolitan area.  
Countless cooling lines.  
Load displays changing by color.

Blue.  
Yellow.  
Red.

And on the central monitor.

“Metropolitan Ranking  
1st: FROST-9”

A number was displayed.

“Cumulative Cooling Credit: 87.420 CC”

“…Eighty-seven?”

Yuto stopped without thinking.

He had 1.261 CC.

The number of digits was different.

“Surprised?”

A voice came from the back of the control room.

A white jacket.  
Long black hair.  
A woman who looked slightly older than Yuto.

She stood in front of the monitors and turned around.

“Soma Yuto.  
Welcome to the Central Cooling Terminal.”

“…You’re FROST-9?”

“Yes.”

FROST-9 glanced at Yuto’s phone.

“The player who chose collaborative operation.  
The one who abandoned an individual daily 0.030 CC and moved to the side that distributes 0.050 CC.”

“You saw all the logs?”

“Only the necessary parts.”

“What did you want to talk about?”

FROST-9 operated the metropolitan map.

Several cooling lines appeared.

Among them, one area blinked red.

“Next priority area candidate: North Seventh Wharf”

“I want you to look at this.”

“North Seventh Wharf?”

“For three days starting the day after tomorrow.  
Extreme heat and a concentration of large cargo will overlap.”

The red area on the map slowly spread.

“There will not be enough cooling margin.  
Logistics, workers, waiting vehicles, nearby rest areas.  
If we try to protect all of them, someone’s priority will have to be lowered.”

Yuto remembered the night of the shared cooling line.

“What do you want me to do?”

FROST-9 looked straight at Yuto.

“Cooperate with me.  
But this time, it will not be collaborative operation.”

A new display appeared on the monitor.

“North Seventh Wharf: limited wide-area project”

“Reward: minimum 5.000 CC”

Yuto’s breath stopped.

5.000 CC.

A number far beyond the largest reward he had seen so far, 0.150 CC.

“What are the conditions?”

Yuto asked.

FROST-9 answered quietly.

“Follow my instructions.  
I will make the final decision on field judgment.”

On the control room monitor, North Seventh Wharf glowed red.

Yuto looked back and forth between the number 5.000 CC and FROST-9’s eyes.

The greatest chance so far to rise upward.

But it was also an invitation into the “single right answer” that he had chosen against yesterday.

## Afterword

Episode 61 depicts the day after the shared cooling line arc, and the upper player FROST-9, ranked first in the metropolitan rankings, appears before Yuto.

The key points this time were as follows.

- As the first day’s allocation from collaborative operation, Yuto received +0.018 CC, bringing his cumulative total to 1.261 CC
- FROST-9 is an upper player in the metropolitan rankings with an overwhelming cumulative record of 87.420 CC
- Yuto was invited to the Central Cooling Terminal and saw the scale of wide-area cooling and the world of upper players for the first time
- FROST-9 presented a project to respond to extreme heat and heavy cargo concentration at North Seventh Wharf
- The reward is a minimum of 5.000 CC, making it an enormous chance for Yuto to rise upward at once
- However, the condition is that he follow FROST-9’s instructions and entrust the final decision-making authority to her

Episode 61 is the episode where Yuto’s story begins to move from “collaborative operation at the edge of town” to “metropolitan-scale competition and high rewards.”  
5.000 CC is far greater than the 1.261 CC Yuto has built up so far. But in order to receive that reward, he must temporarily entrust FROST-9 with the method he has relied on: listening to field voices and building priorities with his companions.

In the next episode, Yuto does not answer immediately, but tries to check the information about North Seventh Wharf and FROST-9’s operating policy.  
Is FROST-9 truly a cold efficiency-first player? Or is she simply making harsh choices to protect the metropolitan area as a whole? Before the temptation of 5.000 CC, Yuto’s own way of winning will be tested.
