# Episode 42: A Hint of a Temporary Bonus

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 42 keeps the flow of Part 4 while raising the tempo a little in a more entertaining direction.  
Yuto earns Cooling Credit through a small on-site improvement, and the episode ends in a way that makes the reader wonder what will happen next.

---

## Main Text

Thursday.  
Soma Yuto sat on a bench in the station-front plaza, greeting the morning with a slightly more restless feeling than usual.

There was one reason.

That morning’s notification contained wording he was not used to seeing.

“Today’s priority target:  
If the stay experience is improved in a ‘place that is easy to use,’ additional Cooling Credit may be generated.”

“…Additional?”

Yuto looked at the screen twice.

His usual CC increased according to daily use and small achievements.  
But when the word “additional” appeared, it bothered him strangely.

Then WindArc arrived.

“Did you see it?”

“I did.  
Something feels different today.”

“Yes.  
From recent operation data, we’ve begun to see places where a small improvement can greatly change the user experience.”

“So that means a temporary bonus of some kind?”

“Yes.  
To put it simply, it is a form of bonus credit for people who notice an improvement in the field and actually make it move.”

“I see…”

Yuto looked across the plaza.

The benches in front of the station.  
The mist.  
The voice guidance.  
The flow of people.

None of it was bad.  
But only during the morning hours, there was one place where the flow became slightly sluggish.

“…There.”

Yuto’s eyes went to the small cluster of people forming at the corner of the guidance board.  
People stopped to read, people who wanted to sit hesitated a little, and the flow of passersby narrowed slightly.

“You noticed it.”

“Yes.  
Isn’t the position of that guidance board a little off?”

“Actually, several people have pointed out the same thing.  
But so far, it has still been in ‘wait and see’ status.”

“So if we fix it today…?”

“Yes.  
If the improvement actually changes the user experience, it becomes eligible for additional CC.”

Yuto stood up.

“Then we have to do it.”

During the morning, Yuto spoke with the staff and shifted the position of the guidance board slightly.  
They adjusted its distance from the benches, arranging the flow so that people who stopped to read could naturally continue toward the seats.

They also changed part of the guidance text.

“This is a place where you may sit if you are tired.  
It is free.  
No application is required.  
If you are unsure, please sit down first.”

“That last sentence looks effective,” one staff member said with a smile.

“‘If you are unsure, please sit down first’ is quite strong for loosening hesitation.”

Before long, the change became visible.

People who had been standing in front of the guidance board sat down directly on the benches.  
A parent and child who had been watching the situation rested without hesitation.  
An elderly person sat down, saying, “If it’s all right to sit, then I will.”

The flow had changed.

“Oh, that’s good…”

Yuto let the words slip out.

WindArc nodded while looking at the terminal.

“The usage rate is rising.  
And the time until people begin their stay has become shorter.”

“So the time spent hesitating has decreased.”

“Exactly.  
And this kind of change is evaluated as an ‘experience improvement.’”

Before noon, a notification arrived on Yuto’s phone.

“Additional Cooling Credit has been granted based on the reflected improvement.  
+0.004 CC”

“…It came!”

Yuto’s eyes opened wide in an instant.

Then another notification followed.

“Part of today’s experience improvement bonus condition has been achieved.  
Additional candidate targets have appeared.”

“Candidates…?”

WindArc smiled a little.

“Yes.  
It is not over yet.  
Today, we may be able to go one level further.”

“What, there’s more?”

“There is.  
Actually, there is one more place that concerns me.”

When Yuto looked over, it was the resting space on the shopping street side this time.  
At the end of one bench, sunlight tended to reach in, making that seat a little hard to use in the afternoon.

“…I see.  
So we’re supposed to do that one too.”

“That would be the idea.”

Yuto smiled slightly.

“So today is a day to earn, huh?”

WindArc nodded at those words too.

“Yes.  
And if improvements continue, they may lead not only to additional CC, but also to a ‘right to propose’ the next step.”

“A right to propose?”

“Yes.  
It is not official yet, but it would be a mechanism where people who produce results in the field are shown the next improvement candidates first.”

“That’s pretty interesting.”

“Isn’t it?”

In the afternoon, Yuto headed to the shopping street.

He checked the angle of the sunlight, looked at the bench layout,  
and while consulting with the staff, put up a simple shade sheet.

“This alone makes a big difference, doesn’t it?”

“Yes.  
What matters is not whether people can sit, but whether they can keep sitting comfortably.”

The reaction appeared immediately.

People began sitting in the seat that had been empty until then,  
and more people rested for longer.  
A passerby stopped in, saying, “This place looks all right.”

The flow changed a little again.

In the evening, another notification sounded.

“Experience improvement result confirmed.  
Additional Cooling Credit has been granted.  
+0.006 CC”

“Yes…!”

The total display jumped up just a little.

“Earned today: 0.010 CC  
Total: 0.771 CC”

Yuto’s mouth relaxed without him meaning to.

But right after that, something happened.

A new alert appeared on WindArc’s terminal.

“…Hm?”

“What is it?”

“This is… still in the confirmation stage,  
but it seems that one more priority area for an additional bonus has appeared.”

“One more?”

“Yes.  
And it is a rather unexpected place.”

“Where?”

WindArc showed the terminal to Yuto, looking slightly reluctant.

“Candidate target:  
Nighttime ‘route home’ flow”

Yuto looked at those words and stayed silent for a while.

“…At night?”

“Yes.  
And there is a possibility of a fairly large response.”

The lights in the station-front plaza began to turn on little by little.  
A quiet flow of people, different from the bustle of the daytime, moved through the area.

Yuto stood up and looked toward the night road.

“…This is getting interesting.”

## Afterword

Episode 42 raised the tempo a little and strengthened the flow where Yuto earns Cooling Credit through on-site improvements.  
To make each episode leave the reader wanting to know what comes next, the ending introduces a new priority candidate: the nighttime “route home” flow.

The key points this time were as follows.  
- Yuto notices something himself and makes a small improvement in the field
- That improvement immediately leads to a felt change and an increase in CC
- Another earning opportunity appears, creating a hook for the next episode

Going forward, rather than pushing operation meetings too far to the front, the story will gradually strengthen the flow where Yuto can earn, notice things, and grow through his own actions first.
