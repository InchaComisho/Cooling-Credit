# Episode 46: The Key to the Old Waiting Room

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 46 begins the limited challenge set at the old waiting room.  
Yuto takes on a major contest with Haru: a reward of 0.050 CC or more. But beyond the door, a condition neither of them expected is waiting.

---

## Main Text

Sunday, eight in the morning.

Soma Yuto stood behind the flea market venue at the edge of town.

In front of him was an old waiting room.

A low roof.  
Walls whose color had faded.  
A half-peeled signboard.  
A glass door that remained shut.

Apparently, it had once been used by people working at the nearby factories and by people waiting for deliveries.

But now, no one used it.

Even so, the roof remained.  
There were walls.  
There were windows.  
And inside it was a “possibility of rest” that was far too scarce at the edge of town.

“Limited challenge begins in  
00:03:12”

Yuto tightened his grip on his phone.

The screen showed the message he had seen over and over since yesterday.

“Regionally unconnected area: Old Waiting Room  
Estimated reward: 0.050 CC or more”

“…Seeing it again, that’s huge.”

For ordinary small improvement bonuses, it was usually 0.004 CC or 0.007 CC.  
This time, it was at least 0.050 CC.

If it went well, he could increase his total at once.

“That face says you’re thinking only about the reward.”

A voice came from behind him.

When Yuto turned around, Haru was there.

The same black cap as always.  
A large backpack.  
But today, he was carrying slightly more equipment than yesterday.

Work gloves.  
A portable light.  
A folding simple mask.  
A small tool case.

“Preparing for a safety check?”

“Of course.  
It’s an old building. It wouldn’t be funny if we just walked in and the floor collapsed.”

“I should have at least brought work gloves.”

“You didn’t?”

“…I didn’t.”

Haru sighed in disbelief and took a new pair of work gloves from his backpack.

“Borrow these.  
Don’t lose them.”

“Thank you.”

“Don’t misunderstand.  
If you get hurt, our joint evaluation might drop.”

“You really are a competitor.”

“Obviously.”

At that moment, more people appeared in front of the waiting room.

Minato in her navy jacket.  
The manager from yesterday.  
And three players Yuto did not know.

One was a tall woman wearing a fluorescent vest.  
Another was a university-age young man with glasses.  
The last was a short-haired man standing with his arms crossed, staring at the waiting room.

“…So there are other participants.”

Yuto said it quietly.

“Limited doesn’t mean only the two of us.”

Haru’s expression stiffened slightly.

Minato stepped forward.

“Good morning.  
To all players who have obtained Safety Route Level 1:  
We will now begin the Old Waiting Room limited challenge.”

Everyone’s phones vibrated at the same time.

“Limited challenge begins”

The rules appeared on the screen.

“Objective: Reconnect the old waiting room as a short-time resting base  
for the weekend edge-of-town area.”

“Evaluation items:  
1. Safety  
2. Ease of use  
3. Cooperation with the field  
4. Actual use”

“Time limit: 4 hours”

“Four hours…”

Yuto looked up at the building.

Even cleaning alone would not leave enough time.  
Safety confirmation.  
Securing movement routes.  
Installing guidance.  
Getting actual people to use it.

There was far too much to do.

But one more sentence appeared at the bottom of the screen.

“Note:  
Completion by a single participant is predicted to be difficult.  
Cooperation is permitted.  
However, the final reward will be distributed according to contribution.”

“…Cooperation recommended, huh.”

Haru said in a low voice.

“But contribution-based distribution.”

“In other words, we’ll also be fighting over credit.”

Yuto looked at the other participants.

The woman in the fluorescent vest was already checking the cracks in the wall and the roof.  
The young man with glasses was opening a map of the surroundings on his phone.  
The short-haired man was staring intently at the lock of the waiting room.

“Yuto.”

“Yes.”

“We decide first.  
Do we team up, or do we move separately?”

Yesterday, he might have hesitated a little.

But they only had four hours.

“Let’s team up.”

Yuto answered immediately.

“We can think about the reward later.  
Right now, opening it and making it usable comes first.”

For a few seconds, Haru looked into Yuto’s eyes.

Then he nodded shortly.

“Fine.  
Then I’ll check the building’s safety.  
You look at the outside route and the flow of users.”

“Understood.”

Just as the two of them were about to move, the short-haired man stepped away from the lock.

“There’s no key.”

The area fell silent.

The manager frowned.

“We looked yesterday too, but couldn’t find it.  
We’ve contacted the management company, but it’s Sunday, so there’s no reply.”

“You started the challenge without a key?”

The young man with glasses asked in a confused voice.

Minato answered calmly.

“Determining the possibility of use is also part of the evaluation this time.  
Opening the door is not the only correct answer.”

“So if we can’t get inside, we make something outside.”

The woman in the fluorescent vest said.

“That is one possibility.”

The short-haired man lightly tapped the door.

“Then opening the lock would be fastest.”

“You can’t just break it.”

Yuto said.

“Who said I was breaking it?”

“The way you said it was a little…”

“Just call a locksmith.”

The short-haired man said it while operating his phone.

“If it’s emergency service, there are mobile locksmiths who will come out.”

“What about the cost?”

Haru asked.

“No idea.  
But if the reward is 0.050 CC or more, it should pay for itself.”

That bothered Yuto.

Investing money up front while counting on a large reward.  
If it succeeded, the payoff would be big.  
But if it failed, nothing would remain.

As a contest to climb upward, it might be right.

But—

“Wait a second.”

Yuto looked to the side of the waiting room.

There was a small notice board along the wall.  
Peeling paper.  
An old evacuation route diagram.  
A faded notice showing “waiting room hours.”

Under it was a small metal box.

“…What is that?”

The manager approached and narrowed his eyes.

“An old message box?  
No… wait.”

A rusty label remained on the box.

“For emergencies  
Facility management key”

“A key?”

Yuto said.

Haru immediately checked the area around it.

“The box itself has a lock.”

“But we don’t have that key either, right?”

At that moment—

The young man with glasses pointed to the side window of the waiting room.

“Excuse me.  
Isn’t that window open just a little?”

Everyone turned.

Indeed, the upper part of the window was raised by just a few centimeters.

Had the wind opened it?  
Or had it been left half-closed from the beginning?

The short-haired man moved closer.

“Can someone fit a hand through?”

“That’s dangerous.”

Haru stopped him.

“What if the glass is cracked?  
We can’t see the inside either.”

“So we do nothing?”

The air grew tense.

The time limit continued ticking down.

“Time remaining: 3 hours 42 minutes”

It was already starting to grow hot outside.  
The flea market opening time was approaching.

The people who would need a resting place would arrive soon.

Yuto stared at the window.

Call a locksmith.  
Build a temporary rest space outside.  
Wait for the management company to respond.  
Look for a way to enter through the window.

Every option had time costs and risks.

Then Yuto’s phone vibrated softly.

“Hidden evaluation condition detected.  
Check surrounding information?”

“…Hidden evaluation?”

A small icon he had never seen before was blinking on the screen.

Should he press it?  
Or should he move immediately?

Just as Yuto reached his finger toward it—

From inside the waiting room—

Clatter.

Something fell over.

Everyone stopped moving.

“…Is someone inside?”

The short-haired man said in a low voice.

For just a moment, a cold breeze blew out through the gap in the window.

Yuto held his breath.

Inside the old waiting room, which was supposed to be closed, there was now unmistakably a human presence.

## Afterword

Episode 46 began the old waiting room limited challenge in earnest.  
From this point, Yuto’s CC earning moves beyond everyday small improvements into a larger contest involving a time limit, reward distribution, and competition with other players.

The structure this time is as follows.

- Setup: Yuto and Haru head to the old waiting room, aiming for a reward of 0.050 CC or more
- Development: Other players also participate, and cooperation and competition begin at the same time
- Turn: There is no key to the waiting room, and they hit the first wall in making the building usable
- Ending: Two mysteries remain: a hidden evaluation condition notification and a sound heard from inside the building

Yuto and Haru chose to cooperate for the time being.  
However, since the reward is distributed according to contribution, they may not remain complete allies until the end.

In the next episode, we will follow whether someone is really inside the waiting room, and what the “hidden evaluation condition” on Yuto’s phone means.  
This contest does not seem likely to end with simply opening a lock.
