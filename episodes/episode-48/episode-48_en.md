# Episode 48: Beyond the Cooling Function

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 48 enters the core of the old waiting room limited challenge.  
Faced with the two choices of “safety check” and “reward efficiency,” Yuto chooses the way he wants to rise. And what is found in the machine room is not merely an old cooling device.

---

## Main Text

At the back of the old waiting room.

Beyond the dim hallway, there was the door to the machine room.

A rusted metal door.  
An old warning notice.  
Faded red letters.

“Authorized personnel only.”

Two choices were floating on Yuto’s phone.

“Prioritize safety check”  
“Prioritize reward efficiency”

Remaining time: 2 hours 41 minutes.

“…That’s blatant.”

Haru peered at the screen.

“It looks like a game, but basically it means whether to hurry or to check first.”

“If we hurry, the reward goes up?”

“Probably.  
If someone reconnects the old machine room quickly, they’ll be evaluated as the ‘first player to succeed.’”

The short-haired man said from a short distance away.

“Then the answer is obvious.”

Without hesitation, he operated his phone.

“Prioritize reward efficiency”

A light ping sounded.

A new display appeared on the short-haired man’s screen.

“Efficiency-priority route selected.  
Partial advance investigation rights granted.”

“See?”

The man approached the machine room door.

“Wait!”

Yuto raised his voice.

“The rescue team still hasn’t finished checking the building’s safety.  
It’s dangerous to go inside.”

“This isn’t where that person collapsed earlier.  
This is the machine room.”

“That’s why it’s dangerous.  
Old wiring, old machinery—we don’t know what’s left in there.”

“If you’re scared, watch from there.”

The man shrugged.

Haru said in a low voice.

“At least give your name.”

“Soma.”

“I’m Soma too, though.”

Yuto said reflexively.

“Not that Soma.  
It’s Soma, written as Sōma.”

Sōma gripped the door handle.

“I hate waiting and missing out on a reward.”

With a creak, the door opened.

Cold air flowed out from inside.

Yuto held his breath.

Cold air.

The reason the waiting room had been slightly cooler than outside.

“It really is running…”

Inside the machine room, old large-scale devices were lined up.

Thick pipes along the wall.  
Small blinking lamps.  
An old control panel.  
And a low vibration.

Vrrrrrrr…

It had not completely stopped.

Sōma took one step inside.

“See?  
No problem.”

At that moment.

Beep-beep-beep-beep!

A sharp warning sound rang from the control panel.

Red lamps began flashing all at once.

“Pressure abnormality”  
“Ventilation stopped”  
“Inspection not performed”

A warning also appeared on Yuto’s phone.

“Danger judgment: proximity to unverified equipment detected.  
Secure a safe distance.”

“Sōma, get out!”

Haru shouted.

“It’s fine.  
It’s probably just an old error display!”

Sōma reached toward the control panel.

The next instant.

Bang!

Something shook violently in the back of the machine room.

The floor trembled.

“Whoa…!”

Sōma lost his balance.

From the side of a device, a thin stream of something like white steam sprayed out.

“Everyone, step back!”

Minato’s voice flew through the air.

The venue manager immediately stretched a rope across the entrance to the hallway.

“Move away from the machine room!”

Sōma grimaced and hurried back outside.

His hands were fine.  
He had only nearly fallen.

But the composure he had shown moments earlier was gone from his face.

“…What was that?”

“That’s what I was saying.”

Haru’s voice was stern.

“Even if a machine hasn’t stopped, that doesn’t mean it’s safe.”

Yuto looked at his phone.

“Efficiency-priority route: risk judgment confirmed”  
“Safety-check route: additional information available”

On Sōma’s screen, red marks had increased.

“Safety risk added: equivalent to -0.010 CC”

“Minus…?”

Sōma glared at the screen.

“I never heard about that.”

“It was written in the rules.”

The woman in the fluorescent vest said coldly.

“Safety is one of the evaluation items.  
You simply chose without reading.”

Sōma could not answer.

Yuto stared at his own screen.

The choice was still there.

“Prioritize safety check”  
“Prioritize reward efficiency”

Remaining time: 2 hours 23 minutes.

If he chose the safety check here, he would spend time.  
While he did that, the other players might complete the outside resting point.

If he wanted to prioritize the reward, this was exactly the moment to hurry.

“Yuto.”

Haru said.

“I’m choosing the safety check.  
What about you?”

Yuto remembered the words of the man who had been carried into the ambulance.

“This used to be a place where anyone could rest.”

He wanted to make it a place where people could rest.

But if he let people into a dangerous place, there would be no point.

“…Safety check.”

Yuto pressed the button.

“Prioritize safety check”

The screen turned blue.

“Safety-check route selected.  
Access to surrounding information unlocked.”

A map appeared.

The old waiting room.  
The machine room.  
The roof.  
The vents.  
The emergency exit.  
And a narrow passage behind the building.

At the end of it, an unfamiliar label appeared.

“External cooling unit”

“External… cooling unit?”

Haru was looking at his own screen as well.

“It’s not inside the machine room.  
There’s another device behind the building.”

Minato approached.

“For those who chose the safety-check route, the building’s old equipment drawings are disclosed.  
This waiting room was not cooled only by an indoor unit.”

“The external unit might still be alive?”

“There is a possibility.  
However, confirming it requires going around to the rear of the building.”

“Then let’s go.”

As Yuto began to move, Minato stopped him.

“Please wait.  
There is another problem behind the building.”

“A problem?”

“The back of the building is not part of the venue’s managed area.  
It is the property of the adjacent warehouse company.”

Yuto enlarged the map.

Sure enough, there was a fence behind the waiting room.  
Beyond it were old containers and a warehouse loading entrance.

“If we get permission…”

“Because it is Sunday, we have not yet been able to reach the warehouse company’s manager.”

“Another problem of keys and permission, huh.”

Haru smiled bitterly.

“But this time, it’s not a door. It’s a boundary line.”

Remaining time: 2 hours 12 minutes.

Then the woman in the fluorescent vest spoke.

“Wait.  
We may be able to check the external unit’s condition without going behind the building.”

“How?”

The woman looked up at the roof of the waiting room.

“The ventilation duct.  
If that is connected to the external unit, we can tell whether it is operating from the air flow.”

“Are you going to climb onto the roof?”

Sōma said.

“That’s dangerous.”

“I’m not climbing.  
We’ll only check from outside.”

The woman took out a small thermometer she had been carrying.

“We measure wind direction and temperature difference.  
If it is releasing heat outside, the area near the exhaust outlet should be hotter than the surroundings.”

Yuto opened his eyes wide.

“That means we might be able to confirm the cooling function without touching the machine room.”

“Exactly.”

Haru gave a small smile.

“What’s your name?”

“Nagi.”

“Nagi.  
Let’s do it.”

The four of them went around the outside of the waiting room.

Yuto searched behind the guidance board for the ventilation duct outlet.  
Haru checked the safety of the surroundings.  
Nagi used her thermometer to measure the temperature along the wall.  
Sōma stood a little apart, looking frustrated as he watched his terminal.

“…I’ll help too.”

Sōma said.

“As long as you don’t do anything unnecessary.”

Haru replied.

“I know.”

Sōma pointed to the pipes along the wall.

“This pipe splits in two halfway through.  
It’s probably not only for the external unit.  
There’s another system.”

Yuto looked at the map.

“You’re right.  
The old equipment drawing also shows a branch halfway through.”

“What does it connect to?”

Nagi asked.

Yuto enlarged the screen.

The thin line crossed the roof of the waiting room and extended toward the edge of the venue.

There, an old label remained.

“Town Outskirts Shared Cooling Line”

“Shared… cooling line?”

Everyone fell silent.

It was not a device for only one waiting room.

Perhaps it connected to part of the venue.  
Or the warehouse.  
Or even somewhere farther away.

Yuto’s phone vibrated strongly.

“Major discovery confirmed.  
Limited challenge evaluation conditions will be updated.”

“New objective:  
Do not merely open the old waiting room.  
Identify the destination of the ‘shared cooling line.’”

“…This just got bigger.”

Haru said.

And immediately after that.

A sudden cheer rose from the other side of the venue.

“The mist came on!”

Someone’s voice.

At the edge of the flea market venue, white mist was spraying from an old water mist nozzle that was supposed to have been inactive.

Shhhhh…

Fine cooling mist spread through the summer light.

But a red warning appeared on Yuto’s screen.

“Shared cooling line: unauthorized operation detected”  
“Time until overload: 18 minutes”

Yuto looked at the mist.

It was cool.  
People were happy.

But if it continued like this, something would stop.

“…Next, we either stop it,  
or use it up.”

At Haru’s words, Yuto held his breath.

## Afterword

Episode 48 showed Yuto choosing “safety check,” which led him beyond the simple reuse of the old waiting room and toward the existence of the “shared cooling line.”

The flow this time is as follows.

- Setup: Sōma chooses the reward-efficiency route and causes a dangerous trouble in the machine room
- Development: Yuto accepts the possibility of sacrificing time and reward, and chooses the safety-check route
- Turn: It is revealed that the old waiting room has an external cooling unit and a shared cooling line connected to a wider area
- Ending: The venue mist suddenly starts operating, and the danger of overload approaches in 18 minutes

Yuto prioritized safety this time.  
But that was not simply because he was cautious. By gathering information on the safety side, he reached a discovery that may connect to a larger reward and greater influence.

In the next episode, the contest will be how to handle the remaining 18 minutes before overload.  
If they stop the mist, they may disappoint the people currently cooling off in front of them. If they keep using it, the entire shared cooling line may shut down. Yuto and the others will have to search, within the limited time, for a way to “cool while protecting.”
