# Episode 72 — A Route That Turns Four Minutes into Two

## Preface

Episode 72 concludes the head-to-head contest at Point A, the Third Worker Rest Hut.
With strong winds disrupting the roll-call tent and the L-4 auxiliary branch needing to return to Point C, Yuto has only eight minutes left. Instead of trying to increase cooling, he attempts to beat HEATWAVE-3's 40% reduction plan by reducing the burden of movement itself.

---

## Main Story

『Emergency branch: Select an action』

『A: Keep L-4 at Point A and re-secure the roll-call tent』
『B: Return L-4 to Point C and reduce the Third Rest Hut to 40%』
『C: Create a separate short-stop route toward the Second Rest Hut』

『Priority Proposal Contest — Time remaining: 00:08:17』

At North Pier Seven, the Third Worker Rest Hut was being battered by wind.

The side curtain of the roll-call tent snapped violently.

Wet cooling towels scattered, and the water hose nearly slipped free from its connector.

Workers inside the temporary tent stood up uneasily.

“This is going to stop being a place to rest.”

“Did the water stop?”

“Should we go back inside?”

Yuto's phone kept counting down.

『Time remaining: 00:07:58』

The L-4 auxiliary branch was using 0.5% at Point A. But demand was beginning to rise again at Point C and along the northern passage.

If he kept L-4 here, the Point A plan could continue. But emergency response elsewhere would be delayed.

If he returned it to Point C, the tent would lose chilled water and the Third Rest Hut would have to move closer to HEATWAVE-3's 40% plan.

Then there was option C.

Create another short-stop route toward the Second Rest Hut.

“How?” Soma asked while holding down the flapping curtain.

Yuto opened the map between the Third and Second Rest Huts.

Six and a half minutes one way. Thirteen minutes round trip.

That distance was the real burden for workers with injuries or very short breaks.

But did they really have to walk the entire distance in one stretch?

“...It's far because we're making them walk all the way to the Second Rest Hut at once.”

Haru looked at him.

“What are you thinking?”

“Instead of moving people all the way to the cooling, what if we create a recovery point halfway?”

Yuto zoomed in on the route.

Exactly between Point A and the Second Rest Hut stood a small cargo-verification structure.

『Safety Check Booth』

Normally it was only used to confirm cargo labels and vehicle numbers. But it had a roof, partial walls, electricity, and a water connection.

“Here.”

Yuto pointed at it.

“We make the Safety Check Booth a two-minute recovery relay point.”

“It has no cooling,” HEATWAVE-3 said.

“We don't need full cooling.”

Yuto replied quickly.

“We turn it into a place with shade, wind, and water. Then people don't have to walk the whole six and a half minutes without stopping.”

Nagi's voice came through the channel.

『I checked the drawings. Separate from L-4, the waste-heat recovery line from the Second Rest Hut passes nearby.』

“Can we use it?”

『Not for cold air. But we may be able to draw a small amount of lower-temperature water for cooling towels.』

“That's enough.”

HEATWAVE-3 sounded surprised for the first time.

“You're not increasing cooling capacity. You're using a flow that would otherwise be discarded.”

“If the Second Rest Hut is running at 100%, there should be something usable along the route.”

『Time remaining: 00:06:42』

MIST CROW checked the facility schedule.

“The booth has power. But it is normally used for cargo checks.”

“Is it in use now?”

“Next vehicle check is in twenty minutes.”

“Then let us use it.”

“Who authorizes that?”

Yuto immediately looked for the responsible site manager and found a woman wearing an armband.

He ran to her.

“Are you responsible for the Safety Check Booth?”

“Yes. Why?”

“Please let us use it for the next twenty minutes as an intermediate rest point. Workers moving from the Third Rest Hut to the Second can drink water, cool their necks, and break the walk into shorter sections.”

She looked toward the unstable tent and the workers still resting inside the hut.

“What about cooling?”

“We may be able to borrow a small amount of low-temperature water from the Second Rest Hut's waste-heat recovery line. More importantly, we can return L-4 to Point C and keep emergency reserve capacity available elsewhere.”

She checked her watch.

“Fifteen minutes. When vehicle checks resume, clear the booth immediately.”

“Thank you!”

Yuto turned back.

“I choose C!”

『C: Create a separate short-stop route toward the Second Rest Hut』

『Selection confirmed』
『L-4 auxiliary branch: Returned from Point A』
『Third Rest Hut: Operational redesign initiated』

“Return L-4 to Point C.”

Nagi answered.

『Understood. Chilled water at A will stop, but emergency reserve capacity returns to Point C and the northern passage.』

“What about the tent?” Soma asked.

“We close it,” Yuto said.

“Anyone who truly needs sheltered rest stays inside the Third Rest Hut. Short-break users transfer through the Safety Check Booth to the Second Rest Hut.”

HEATWAVE-3 frowned.

“So you're moving them after all. That's my plan.”

“No.”

Yuto shook his head.

“I'm not making them walk the whole distance at once. We divide six and a half minutes into roughly two minutes, a short recovery, then another two to two and a half.”

HEATWAVE-3's expression shifted.

“A road that turns four minutes into two.”

“Exactly.”

◇

The team moved fast.

Chairs from the tent were transferred to the booth. The side curtain was removed and fixed as shade instead of a windbreak. The L-4 branch was released.

『L-4 auxiliary branch: 0.5% → 0.0%』
『Point C / Northern Passage: Reserve capacity restored』

Nagi connected to the management terminal at the Second Rest Hut.

『Low-temperature water diverted from the heat-recovery line. Automatic cutoff above 0.2%.』

“Is 0.2 enough?”

『Not enough to cool the space, but enough for towels and wrist cooling.』

Water began filling a small tank at the booth.

Towels were soaked. Paper cups were arranged.

Haru posted a large arrow sign.

『Third Rest Hut → 2-minute recovery → Second Rest Hut』

Soma called to the workers.

“Anyone who doesn't need to remain inside, go two at a time! You can sit halfway! Water and towels are available!”

The first two workers left the Third Rest Hut.

Two minutes later they reached the Safety Check Booth.

“There's wind here.”

“And the water's actually cool.”

They sat for about ninety seconds, cooled their necks, and continued.

『Third Rest Hut users: 7 → 5』
『Safety Check Booth users: 2』
『Second Rest Hut: Acceptance confirmed』

『Time remaining: 00:02:38』

“Next two!” Soma called.

The route began to function.

Walking was no longer something they had to endure in one uninterrupted stretch.

『Relay-rest route confirmed』
『Mobility-limited users at Third Rest Hut: Stable』

HEATWAVE-3 stared at the figures.

“Third Rest Hut output?”

“55% maintained.”

“And total regional load?”

Nagi replied.

『Third Rest Hut 55%, relay booth 0.2%, L-4 returned. Total load is almost the same as HEATWAVE-3's 40% reduction plan. Corridor congestion is lower under Yuto's plan.』

HEATWAVE-3 was silent for a moment.

“Almost the same load... while keeping the room viable.”

『Time remaining: 00:01:21』

One worker with a limp tried to stand.

“I'll go too.”

Yuto stopped him.

“Please don't force yourself. The 55% is being kept for people who need to stay.”

“But if I stay, your numbers look worse.”

“That doesn't matter.”

“You're competing, aren't you?”

“We are.”

Yuto met his eyes.

“But if I make someone who can't safely walk do it just to win, then it isn't my plan anymore.”

The worker slowly nodded.

“Then I'll stay here.”

『Mobility-limited user: Continued stay confirmed』

『Time remaining: 00:00:32』

The last pair arrived at the Safety Check Booth just as another gust slammed into the shade panel.

Haru grabbed the support pole. Soma caught the water tank. Two workers helped hold the side sheet.

For several long seconds, everyone held the structure together.

Then Yuto's phone rang.

『Priority Proposal Contest — End』

The display went dark.

『Calculating results』

“...Well?” Soma asked.

HEATWAVE-3 said nothing.

FROST-9's voice came over the channel.

『The evaluation bureau has started the final comparison.』

“What are the results?”

『Not yet. Another evaluation condition has been added.』

A new warning appeared.

『North Pier Seven — Wide-area cooling reserve: 8.4% → 6.9%』

Nagi's tone tightened.

『It's not the main cooling tower. A sudden high-load event has started in the northern cargo-handling zone.』

FROST-9 spoke calmly.

『The Third Rest Hut contest is still being scored. But the real North Pier Seven operation is starting earlier than planned.』

A red zone spread across the map.

『Northern Cargo-Handling Zone — Emergency Cooling Request』

Below it appeared another timer.

『Wide-area mission formal start: 00:45:00』

Yuto looked between the pending contest results and the expanding red zone.

The winner had not yet been decided.

But the 5.000 CC mission was already beginning.

## Afterword

Episode 72 resolves the final eight minutes of the Point A showdown by having Yuto redesign movement instead of trying to add more cooling.

Key points:

- Yuto chooses option C rather than keeping L-4 at Point A or simply adopting HEATWAVE-3's 40% reduction.
- The 6.5-minute one-way walk to the Second Rest Hut is broken into shorter segments with a recovery stop at the Safety Check Booth.
- L-4's 0.5% auxiliary capacity is returned to Point C and the northern passage.
- Up to 0.2% of low-temperature water from the Second Rest Hut's waste-heat recovery line is used for towels and hand/wrist cooling at the relay booth.
- The Third Rest Hut remains at 55%, allowing workers with leg, back, or mobility problems to stay.
- Total wide-area load becomes nearly equal to HEATWAVE-3's 40% plan, while Yuto's route reduces corridor congestion and preserves local access.
- Before the contest result is announced, wide-area cooling reserve falls from 8.4% to 6.9% due to a sudden high-load event in the northern cargo-handling zone.

Yuto's alternative does not increase total cooling. It changes a difficult uninterrupted journey into a route with recovery points.

The next episode will reveal the result of the proposal contest and move into the emergency request from the northern cargo-handling zone. With only 6.9% reserve remaining, Yuto must learn whether he has earned formal discretionary access to the 5.000 CC mission just as the port's truly unstoppable work begins.
