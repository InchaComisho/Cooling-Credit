# Episode 54: Ten Minutes Until Remote Activation

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 54 begins with a crisis: only 10 minutes remain until Venue Mist V-3 is remotely activated.  
Having obtained the D-2 release key, Yuto is forced to choose among three options: return to the waiting station, stop the venue mist, or pursue the mysterious voice remaining in the old waiting room.

---

## Main Text

“Venue Mist V-3  
Remote activation in: 00:09:59”

The machine room of the old waiting room.

Red numbers blinked on Yuto’s phone screen.

9 minutes 42 seconds.  
9 minutes 41 seconds.  
9 minutes 40 seconds.

In his hand was the D-2 release key.

It was the key needed to move the loading-bay-side damper at the waiting station.  
If they used it, they could suppress the escape of cold air from the night delivery waiting station.

But if Venue Mist V-3 was remotely activated, the load on the shared cooling line would spike again.

“Which way do we go?”

Nagi said.

Her words were short.  
But their meaning was heavy.

“If we return to the waiting station, we can open D-2.  
If we go to the venue, we may be able to stop V-3 from activating.”

“We can’t go to both.”

Yuto opened the machine room map.

The old waiting room.  
The venue.  
The night delivery waiting station.

The three locations were connected by the shared cooling line.

And somewhere, someone was trying to remotely activate V-3.

“…Wait.”

Yuto looked at the speaker.

The same voice as the elderly man who had been taken away by ambulance during the day had come from there.

“Check V-3 before opening D-2.”

The owner of that voice knew V-3 was dangerous.

“Nagi-san.  
Do we have to go to the venue to stop V-3?”

“If it can be activated remotely, there is a chance it can be stopped remotely as well.  
But we would need authority.”

“From the control panel in the old waiting room?”

“…According to the wiring diagram, the waiting room is a maintenance base for the old line.  
We might at least be able to check V-3’s status from here.”

“Then we look for a way to stop it from here.”

Yuto made his decision.

“We won’t run to the venue or the waiting station.  
First, we stop V-3 from this waiting room.”

“There is no guarantee we can.”

“It has more possibility than running and not making it in time.”

Nagi nodded once.

“Understood.  
I’ll check the wiring diagram.  
Yuto, look for the control panel.”

8 minutes 51 seconds remained.

◇

The machine room control panel was behind the blue toolbox.

A rusted door.  
Faded switches.  
A dim liquid crystal display.

Most of the characters displayed there were almost unreadable.

“Shared Cooling Line Maintenance Terminal”  
“Waiting Room M-1”  
“Venue Mist V-3”  
“Delivery Relay D-2”

“They’re all connected.”

Yuto held his breath.

In the lower right of the screen, there was a small warning mark.

“External control reservation: V-3”  
“Execution time: 20:12”

“External control reservation…”

“Someone has registered an activation time.”

Nagi crouched beside the control panel.

“And from this terminal, we may be able to cancel it.”

“Let’s do it.”

“Wait.  
With old equipment, a cancel operation can sometimes become an overwrite of the activation command.  
If we touch it carelessly, we could lose not only V-3 control, but D-2 control as well.”

Yuto’s hand stopped.

7 minutes 58 seconds remained.

“Then what should we do?”

“Check the authority level.”

Nagi operated the menu on the screen.

Old logs lined up on the dim display.

“V-3 activation reservation”  
“Registered by: Administrator ID 00”  
“Authentication: old maintenance authority”

“Administrator ID, zero-zero.”

Yuto said it aloud.

“Whose ID is that?”

“I don’t know.  
But it isn’t normal site supervisor authority.”

At that moment, speaker noise came again from below the control panel.

Bzz…

“…Can you hear me?”

Yuto looked up.

“I can hear you!”

This time, the voice sounded closer than it had during the day.

“Do not stop V-3.”

“What?”

Nagi’s expression changed.

“If you stop it, the pressure from the delivery relay will escape toward D-2 all at once.  
The moment you insert the key, the loading-bay side will become dangerous.”

“Then what are we supposed to do?”

Yuto shouted before he could stop himself.

But the answer did not come immediately.

Bzz…

“Do not ‘stop’ V-3.  
‘Separate’ it.”

“Separate it?”

Nagi spread out the wiring diagram.

“Wait… V-3’s mist line is divided into two systems, the venue center and the surrounding area.”

“The place where the mist came out during the day?”

“Yes.  
If only the center is used, the surrounding section can be cut.  
Not a full stop, but a shift down to low-output operation.”

“But the venue is already closed.”

“Because it is closed, the center alone may be enough.”

Yuto looked at the control panel.

Two items were displayed.

“V-3A: central mist”  
“V-3B: peripheral mist”

Both were under activation reservation.

“Cancel only V-3B?”

“That may have less effect on the overall control.”

“May, huh.”

“It’s old equipment.”

6 minutes 21 seconds remained.

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Limited challenge: emergency branch”  
“Please select”

“A: Stop all V-3 systems”  
“B: Lower only the V-3 peripheral mist to low output”  
“C: Maintain the external control reservation”

“…Another choice.”

Yuto stared at the screen.

With A, the load would definitely drop.  
But the voice had said it was dangerous.

With C, they would do nothing.  
But if V-3 operated at full power, the waiting station’s margin would be cut down.

That left B.

But B was not a half-hearted choice.

Do not stop everything. Leave only what is needed.  
It was the same choice Yuto had been repeating: “cool while protecting.”

“B.”

Yuto pressed it.

“B: Lower only the V-3 peripheral mist to low output”

A new confirmation screen appeared on the control panel.

“V-3B control change”  
“Peripheral mist: stop”  
“Central mist: maintain low output”  
“Execute?”

“Execute.”

Yuto pressed it.

The screen went dark for a moment.

Vwoooom…

The sound of the waiting room’s cooling equipment changed slightly.

Nagi looked at her terminal.

“The load… is dropping.”

“Shared cooling line load: 82%”  
“Night delivery waiting station surplus capacity: 7% remaining”

“We got three percent back!”

Yuto exhaled.

However, a new error appeared on the screen.

“V-3B: stop confirmed”  
“V-3A: no control response”

“The central mist isn’t responding?”

Nagi said.

“Part of the external control reservation remains.”

“But the load went down.”

“For now.  
But the central mist may return to full output on its own.”

At that moment, the voice came again from the speaker.

“That is fine.  
Dropping V-3B creates room to open D-2.”

“Who are you?”

Yuto spoke toward the control panel.

“Why do you know about us?  
Why do you know about the equipment?”

Noise.

Then a slightly long silence.

“I am one of the people who built this line long ago.”

Yuto and Nagi looked at each other.

“The waiting room, the venue, and the delivery waiting station.  
We connected them so people would not collapse from heat, so they could rest even at night.”

“You did?”

“But the people who maintained it disappeared.  
Only the records remained, only the mechanism remained, and before anyone noticed, the question of who this line existed for had vanished.”

The voice cut off.

Yuto remembered the elderly man who had been placed in the ambulance during the day.

Was he the person who had built this shared cooling line?

“Are you the person who was in that waiting room?”

There was no answer.

Instead, new guidance appeared on the control panel screen.

“Administrator ID 00: emergency maintenance mode temporarily opened”

“After D-2 release, delivery relay output can be manually redistributed”

“Manually redistributed?”

Nagi read it aloud.

“After opening D-2, you can choose how much cooling goes to the waiting station, temporary storage, and venue.”

“Then we can keep the cargo cooling while making the waiting station not too cold.”

“Maybe.”

4 minutes 18 seconds remained.

Yuto connected a call to Katase.

“Katase-san, can you hear me?”

“I can hear you.  
We are back to 7% surplus here.  
But the large shipment is still coming in.”

“I have the D-2 release key.  
I stopped V-3B and created room to open D-2.”

“Really?”

“But after D-2 is released, we need to manually redistribute output.”

“Who will operate it?”

Yuto looked at the control panel.

The old waiting room.  
The venue.  
The delivery waiting station.

This old terminal would decide the cooling of three distant locations.

“I’ll do it.”

The moment he said it, even he was a little surprised.

Katase fell silent.

“Soma-kun.  
That is too heavy for someone who is not the site supervisor to decide.”

“I know.”

Yuto drew in a breath.

“That is why I won’t decide on my own just by looking at the numbers.  
I will listen to Katase-san, Haru, and Nagi-san, to the voices from each site, and move accordingly.”

On the other end of the call, someone pushed a cart.

“…Understood.  
I will make the judgment here.  
You move your hands.”

“Yes.”

Haru’s voice came in.

“Yuto, I’m in front of D-2.  
I’m waiting for the key.”

“I’m heading there now.”

Nagi said:

“V-3B on the venue side is stopped.  
All that remains is to open D-2 and restore the flow.”

“However.”

Yuto looked at his phone.

“V-3A: no control response”

The central mist was still silent.

There was no telling when it might return to full output.

“…It isn’t over yet.”

Yuto and Nagi ran to the small van.

3 minutes 32 seconds remained.

On Yuto’s screen, as he gripped the D-2 release key, the final warning appeared.

“Emergency maintenance mode valid time: 03:30”

“If D-2 is not released within the time limit,  
Administrator ID 00 authority will be locked again”

Beyond the night road, the lights of the delivery waiting station came into view.

There, Haru was waiting, looking up at the area above the loading bay.

And farther beyond that.

From the direction of the venue where no one was supposed to be.

Faintly.

Shhhhh…

The sound of mist beginning to move again could be heard.

## Afterword

In Episode 54, facing the remote activation of Venue Mist V-3, Yuto chose not to “stop everything,” but to “lower only the peripheral mist.”

The key points this time were as follows.

- From the old waiting room maintenance terminal, Yuto confirmed that V-3’s central mist and peripheral mist were separate systems
- By stopping V-3B, the peripheral mist, while maintaining V-3A, the central mist, at low output, the waiting station’s surplus cooling capacity recovered from 4% to 7%
- The person speaking through the speaker identified himself as one of the people who built the shared cooling line
- Administrator ID 00’s emergency maintenance mode was temporarily opened, creating the possibility of redistributing cooling output after D-2 is released
- However, central mist V-3A does not respond to control and appears to be starting up again
- The emergency maintenance mode has 3 minutes and 30 seconds remaining. Yuto returns to the waiting station with the release key

This time, Yuto did not choose the shortcut to earn CC.  
He chose to reduce the load without breaking the whole system. That decision restored surplus capacity on the waiting station side and created the minimum margin needed to release D-2.

Next time, the final stage will be releasing D-2 within the remaining emergency maintenance mode time and deciding how to allocate cooling among the waiting station, temporary storage, and venue mist.  
However, the central venue mist V-3A has begun moving again under someone’s remote control. Before Yuto and the others can open D-2, the shared cooling line may once again approach its limit.
