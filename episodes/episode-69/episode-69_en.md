# Episode 69 — The Invisible One

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 69 follows the “invisible one” created by North Pier Seven’s wide-area fine-tuning.  
After output at Point C was reduced to protect Point D, one user began to suffer heat stress in the north-side passage. Yuto learns that in a wide-area assignment, watching the overall numbers is not enough. He also has to follow the one person who moved because of those changes.

---

## Main Story

『Wide-Area Fine-Tuning — Chain Response』

『A: Maintain Point D and respond in the north-side passage with human guidance only』
『B: Temporarily divert Point B water output to the north-side passage』
『C: Use the main cooling tower temporary override authority』

North Pier Seven, Point D.

Three options were displayed on Yuto’s phone.

The user in the north-side passage.

After Point C’s cooling had been reduced to 10%, the person had left the shaded benches and started walking back toward the port.
The red dot on the screen was moving slowly north.

『Target User: Rising Heat Stress Detected』

“Are you going to move the whole system again for one person?” MIST CROW asked.

His voice was not accusatory.
It sounded more like confirmation.

“Point D still has a lot of users. If you divert Point B’s water, drivers waiting in vehicles will be affected. If you use the temporary override on the main cooling tower, the reserve for the whole port drops again.”

“I know.”

Yuto did not look away from the map.

“But the person in the north-side passage needs help now.”

“Send people,” FROST-9 said.

Still wearing her white jacket, she held the portable control unit.

“Option A. Send staff or a first-aid worker there, stop the person from walking, and move them back into shade. That way you do not have to move the cooling line.”

“That has the lowest load impact,” Nagi said over comms.

“But it takes four minutes on foot to reach the north-side passage. A rescue vehicle cannot enter right now because containers are being moved through the route.”

“Four minutes...”

Yuto checked the target user’s position.

The red dot was still moving.

But the walking speed was falling.

『Target User — Walking Speed Decreasing』
『Estimated Time to Northern Shade: 06:12』

“Six minutes,” Haru said.

『I’m close. If I run, I can reach the north-side passage in two minutes.』

“Alone?”

『Not alone. I’ll call out to the workers on the route. If all we need to do is move the person into shade, we don’t have to touch any equipment.』

Yuto was about to choose A immediately.

But Nagi stopped him.

『Wait. There is almost no shade on the passage where the target user is. We need a way to get water there before Haru arrives.』

“Then we divert water from Point B.”

Yuto looked at the options again.

“Not all of it. Just enough to reactivate the old water post in the north-side passage temporarily.”

MIST CROW answered.

“If you redirect Point B’s water line, pressure on the vehicle waiting area will fall.”

“By how much?”

“Up to twenty percent.”

“Then I’ll ask Point B.”

Yuto opened a channel immediately.

“Point B staff, can you hear me? We may need to use the old water post in the north-side passage for five minutes. If we divert twenty percent, can the vehicle waiting area still operate?”

Static.

The sound of idling engines.

Then the staff member answered.

『There are seven people in line right now. We’re already down to one dispenser, so there’s a little waiting.』

“Can you split the pressure for five minutes?”

『...Maybe, if we prioritize refilling bottles and temporarily stop using paper cups. But we’ll have to explain it to the drivers.』

“Please do. Someone in the north-side passage is starting to show signs of heat stress.”

『Understood. We’ll explain it here.』

Yuto looked at MIST CROW.

“I choose B. Five minutes only. We activate the water post and get water there until Haru arrives.”

“That is not mist cooling.”

“Water is enough for now.”

Yuto selected B.

『Temporarily Divert Point B Water Output to North-Side Passage』

『Diversion Time: 05:00』
『Point B Water Pressure: -20%』
『North-Side Water Post: Preparing to Start』

“I approve.”

FROST-9 operated the portable control unit.

Beep.

A small blue point lit up on the map of the north-side passage.

『North-Side Water Post: Start』

But then.

Red text appeared on the post display.

『Manual Lock Release Required Before Water Supply Can Begin』

“Another lock?” Yuto muttered.

Haru came over comms.

『I see it! I’m at the old post!』

“There’s a manual lock!”

『I know. I found the number plate. But there’s no key.』

“What number?”

『K-17.』

Nagi answered immediately.

『If it’s K-17, there’s a spare key in Point B’s management box. But only staff can open that box.』

“Point B staff!”

『We hear you. Opening the management box now.』

“How do we get the key north?”

『Someone has to run it over. But it’s a long way.』

Yuto looked at the map.

Seven minutes on foot from Point B to the north-side passage.

Haru’s location.
The target user.
The water post.

The target user was still about one hundred meters from the post.

『Target User — Walking Speed Decreasing』
『Heat Stress: Caution → Warning』

“We won’t make it...”

Then Soma’s voice came through.

『Yuto! I’m close to the north side!』

“Soma?”

『I got a ride from Point D in a port management vehicle. I’m at the northern junction. If I can get the key there, I can deliver it in two minutes!』

“Can Point B bring the key to the junction?”

『Already contacted them! They’re running too!』

Yuto felt something connect in his chest.

Move the cooling.
Redirect the water.
Carry the key.
People run.

A wide-area assignment was not only about giant control panels.

It was also about who would carry one small key, and where.

◇

North-side passage.

Haru stood in front of the old water post.

A rusted metal box.
A small faucet.
A sign saying it was out of service.

A little farther ahead, the target user had sat down against a wall.

“Are you all right?”

Haru ran over.

The man looked to be in his fifties.
A port delivery company logo was printed on his work clothes.

“...I’m fine. Just a little hot.”

“You don’t look fine.”

“I have to get back to the site...”

“Water first.”

“There’s water?”

“We’re about to get it.”

Haru looked back at the post.

Still locked.

『North-Side Water Post: Standby』

Yuto’s voice came over comms.

“Haru, Soma is bringing the key!”

『How long?』

“Two minutes!”

“Two minutes...”

The man’s breathing was shallow.

Haru pulled out the cooling towel he had been carrying.

“Cool your neck.”

“No, save it for someone else...”

“Right now, you are someone else.”

Haru said it more firmly than before.

The man accepted the towel without another word.

At the northern junction, Soma was running.

A small key was in his hand.

“K-17!”

A Point B staff member shouted from behind him.

“Don’t drop it!”

“I won’t!”

Soma rounded the corner.

But a large forklift crossed the passage in front of him.

Beep. Beep. Beep.

“You’ve got to be kidding me!”

The route was blocked.

Soma started to step in front of the forklift, then stopped himself.

Even in a hurry, he could not do something dangerous.

Haru’s earlier words came back to him.

『We move fast. But we don’t touch things recklessly.』

Soma stopped and waved both arms at the forklift driver.

“Excuse me! Someone is sick on the north side! I need to get a water-post key through!”

The driver looked surprised.

Then immediately pulled the forklift aside.

“Get on!”

“What?”

“The rear step. Hold on!”

“Is that okay?”

“Hurry!”

Soma climbed on without hesitation.

The forklift moved slowly, but steadily, toward the north-side passage.

◇

『North-Side Water Diversion Remaining: 02:18』

At Point D, Yuto watched the screen.

Water was already coming through the line.

If the key turned, the post would work.

But once the diversion time ended, Point B’s water pressure had to be restored.

“Soma, how is it going?”

『Almost there!』

Haru came through next.

『The man is conscious. But I don’t think we should make him stand.』

“What about the first-aid station?”

『Called them. They’re heading north.』

“Water first.”

Yuto looked at MIST CROW.

“Can we extend the diversion?”

“The line at Point B is growing.”

“How many?”

“Fifteen.”

RIVER-EDGE joined the channel.

『I’m watching Point B. Pressure is lower, but the drivers are prioritizing bottle refills. Since paper cups are suspended, the system is still moving.』

“RIVER-EDGE?”

『You were right. Some drivers can’t leave the waiting lane. If it’s just five minutes, we can support it from here.』

Yuto was surprised.

“Thank you.”

『Save the thanks. Just make sure you help the person on the north side.』

MIST CROW checked the numbers.

“Point B is more stable than projected.”

“Can we extend?”

“Three minutes only.”

“Please.”

FROST-9 said nothing as she operated the control unit.

『North-Side Water Diversion Extended: 03:00』

At that moment.

『Wide-Area Reserve: 8.0% → 7.6%』

The number fell.

“The reserve...”

“Three minutes only,” FROST-9 said.

“Any more than that and the whole port becomes vulnerable.”

◇

North-side passage.

The forklift stopped.

“Go!”

Soma jumped down.

“Haru!”

“Key!”

Soma threw it.

Haru caught it.

He inserted it into the water post lock.

K-17.

Click.

The lock turned.

The old display flickered once.

『North-Side Water Post: Active』

Gurgle.

For a moment, air sputtered through the pipe.

Then.

Rush—

Cold water flowed from the faucet.

“It’s working!” Haru shouted.

He filled a bottle and carried it to the man.

“Drink slowly.”

The man accepted the bottle with trembling hands.

One sip.

Then another.

A cooling towel around his neck.
The wall providing partial shade.
Cold water.

Little by little, his breathing settled.

『Target User — Heat Stress: Warning → Caution』

The indicator on Yuto’s screen turned blue.

“It came back...”

At Point D, Yuto clenched his fist without thinking.

『Chain Response: Success』

『Point C User Safety Confirmed』
『Point B Water Diversion — Proper Operation Confirmed』
『North-Side Passage Emergency Response Confirmed』

『Priority Proposal Right: Increased』

MIST CROW looked at Yuto.

“By following one person, we also learned what Point B really is.”

“Yes.”

“Water supply isn’t just equipment. For drivers who cannot leave the waiting lane, it is cooling that comes to them without making them move.”

FROST-9 put away the control unit.

“The wide-area reserve is 7.6%. That is not a light cost for such a small response.”

“I understand.”

“Even so, this response was correct.”

Yuto looked surprised.

He had not expected FROST-9 to say it so clearly.

“But,” she continued.

FROST-9 looked toward the center of North Pier Seven.

“If we move the wide-area line every time we save one person, it will not last for three days.”

“...Right.”

“So next, we build something that lets us help one person without moving the entire line every time.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

『North Pier Seven — Pre-Observation Pass』

『Emergency Response Logs Confirmed for Points A, B, and D』

『Point C — Port Outer Perimeter Shaded Bench Zone
Unobserved』

『Observation Deadline: 00:18:42』

“C is still left.”

Yuto looked at the map.

The shaded bench zone where he had reduced output earlier.

There should have been only two users.

One had already walked north.

The other was still there.

Then the Point C staff member came over comms.

『Yuto. About the other person at Point C...』

“What happened?”

『We found an old cooling box under the bench. The display is dead, but something inside is still running.』

A new message appeared on Yuto’s phone.

『Point C — Unregistered Equipment Detected』

『Small-Scale Cooling Branch: Response Detected』

FROST-9’s expression changed.

“...That equipment is not on the plans.”

The shaded bench zone on the port’s outer perimeter.

There might still be a pocket of cooling capacity there that no one knew existed.

## Afterword

Episode 69 follows the user who moved into the north-side passage after cooling allocation at Point D and Point C was changed, and shows how the team used the wide-area line, water supply, and on-site coordination to protect that one person.

Key points in this episode:

- Rather than use the main cooling tower override, Yuto chose to divert Point B’s water output to the old north-side water post for five minutes.
- The north-side water post was manually locked, requiring the spare K-17 key to be brought from Point B.
- Haru stayed with the affected user and used a cooling towel while Soma delivered the key with help from a forklift driver.
- At Point B, RIVER-EDGE and the waiting drivers cooperated by prioritizing bottle refills over paper-cup use.
- Extending the diversion by three minutes lowered the wide-area reserve from 8.0% to 7.6%, but the target user’s heat-stress level improved from Warning to Caution.
- FROST-9 acknowledged the response as correct, while warning that a system that has to move the wide-area line every time one person needs help cannot survive three days.
- At Point C, an unregistered small-scale cooling branch not shown on the official plans was discovered beneath the benches.

This time, Yuto refused to abandon the “invisible one” inside a wide-area assignment.  
But that success cost the system reserve, lowering it to 7.6%. Compassion alone cannot sustain a three-day operation. The next step is to find a way to support people before they reach the danger point, without making the whole system move each time.

Next episode, Yuto heads to Point C, the only remaining unobserved location at North Pier Seven.  
What is the small cooling branch that does not appear on the plans? Can dormant equipment fill the “cooling gaps” like the north-side passage? And whether Yuto was the first to find it—or someone else already knew—may become the next source of competition.
