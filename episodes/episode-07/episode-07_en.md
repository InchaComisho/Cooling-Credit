# Episode 7: The Logs Come Back

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

In Episode 7, Yuto begins receiving concrete reactions and requests in response to the cooling report he published inside **ThermoBalance**.

It is the first form of a cycle where his logs change someone else's actions, and the results return to him again.

---

Thursday.

Soma Yuto woke up a little earlier than usual.

He was curious how many people had seen the report he had published the previous night, “Small Cooling Logs in Daily Life.”

He picked up the phone beside his pillow and opened **ThermoBalance**.

First, his cumulative Cooling Credit.

“Cumulative Cooling Credit: 0.251 CC”

Then, the viewing status of the cooling report.

“Report views: 7  
Reactions: 3”

“…More people saw it than I expected.”

This was different from a social media account with zero followers.

The report inside the system seemed to have been automatically recommended to nearby players.

When he opened the reactions, several short comments were lined up.

“Logs from the perspective of daily movement routes are useful.  
(Silver-tier player)”

“I'll try the convenience-store back-room ventilation in a similar environment.  
(Bronze-tier player)”

“The school-route danger-avoidance guidance might work in our area too.  
(Rank hidden)”

“…The hidden-rank one makes me even more curious.”

He did not know who they were.

But somewhere, someone had seen his log and thought they might try it.

That alone warmed his chest a little.

“Today, I just move as usual.”

His life was not going to change dramatically.

Convenience-store work.

Cooling actions on the way home.

The report existed as an extension of that.

Yuto bit into bread, got ready quickly, and went outside.

The convenience-store back room was as hot as ever.

But since his previous proposal, the rules for opening the shutter and creating airflow had settled in slightly.

“I'll keep the shutter half-open during the morning again.”

“Please do. Since we started that, it feels like fewer people are about to collapse in the back room.”

Yuto gave a small nod at the manager's words.

He did not look at his phone.

If he looked at the screen while working, he would be scolded.

Even so, he could not help wondering how much heat was escaping through this airflow.

During break time, while drinking water in the corner of the back room, he secretly ran a quick simple scan.

“Localized heat stagnation: equivalent to -0.8°C compared with previous measurement  
Ventilation effect: Continuing”

“It is continuing better than I thought…”

He could tell not only from the number, but from the feel of the workplace.

The amount of sweat.

The heaviness of the air.

When the manager or Takashi said it felt better than before, it was not just their imagination.

By the time his convenience-store shift ended, a small notification appeared on his phone.

“Your cooling log ‘Convenience-store back-room ventilation’ was referenced by a player in another area.”

“Another area…?”

He opened the details.

“Referencer: Bronze-tier player (Area: neighboring city)  
Comment:  
‘I will try a similar ventilation improvement inside a logistics center.’”

“So similar environments really are everywhere.”

Something he had thought was only part of his own living zone overlapped with another town.

Warehouses.

Logistics centers.

Back rooms.

Their heat structures were similar.

“So this is what it means to put logs outside…”

Holding that small morning notification in his mind, Yuto headed toward the station front.

The afternoon was free.

Today too, he would look for cooling actions that could fit into daily life.

As he walked through the station front, something felt slightly out of place in his vision.

The paper he had placed near the root of a shopping-street tree, “It is a little cooler here.”

Next to it, a new piece of paper had been attached.

“+ If the bench is shifted slightly, it becomes even cooler.”

“…Who wrote this?”

Yuto approached and found himself laughing.

The bench had been moved only a few centimeters toward the shaded side.

It was not an official relocation, only nudging it to the edge.

Even so, the amount of shadow falling on the sitting position had clearly increased.

He ran a light scan with **ThermoBalance**.

“Street-tree shade usage rate: Slight increase  
Bench cooling effect: Slight increase  
Cooperative action history exists”

“Cooperative action again…”

It was the same as at the riverside entrance.

Someone had seen his paper and added one step of improvement.

His phone vibrated.

“Additional bonus for cooling improvement through cooperative action  
+0.005 CC”

“Even a bonus appears?”

At that point, all he could do was laugh.

His log remained in the town as a single piece of paper.

Someone picked it up and improved it slightly.

As a result, the cooling effect grew.

The system watched it and evaluated it as cooperation.

There were team battles in this kind of form too.

“I wonder which player around here did it.”

He could not see a face.

He did not know the name.

But at least he could see the rank tier.

When he opened the notification details, short information appeared.

“Street-tree bench improvement proposer:  
Bronze-tier player (handle hidden)”

“Hidden…”

He immediately thought of WindArc, but it did not necessarily have to be him.

There were already multiple players in this town.

He did not need to identify just one person.

“I should keep this as an addendum to the report.”

Before returning to his apartment, Yuto sat on a nearby bench and opened his notebook.

“Street-tree bench improvement (other player):  
- Even shifting it a few centimeters toward the shade increases cooling effect  
- Cooperative action bonus exists  
- Combining paper guidance with real movement is effective”

His logs were no longer only his own.

Other people's ideas were being layered onto them.

He felt that mixing those into the record would matter later.

In the afternoon, Yuto headed to the riverside.

In the tree shade of the entrance plaza, the papers from him and WindArc still remained.

A new sheet had been added there.

“Thank you for using this place.  
On hot days, please rest on the shaded side without pushing yourself.”

“…Polite.”

Judging from the wording, it might have been WindArc.

He had a good balance of light joking and seriousness.

Several people were sitting in the shade of the plaza.

Someone returning from a run.

Someone on a walk.

Compared with before, there were clearly fewer people standing in the middle of the entrance plaza.

His phone vibrated.

“Riverside entrance shade usage rate: +12% compared with previous day  
Time spent in dangerous environment: Equivalent to -15% compared with previous day  
Your log was referenced”

“Referenced…”

He opened the details.

“Reference source: Nearby Cooling Report ‘Small Cooling Logs in Daily Life’  
Referencer: WindArc (Bronze tier)  
Comment:  
‘I will continue the riverside entrance shade guidance.’”

“So it was WindArc.”

Seeing the name made him strangely satisfied.

The log he had written had been seen by WindArc and applied on-site.

The result came back as numbers.

“So logs come back like this.”

His action → log → someone else's action → environmental change → number → his screen.

That cycle had begun to turn slowly.

At the bottom of the riverside stairs, Yuto hesitated a little.

WindArc might be there.

He might not be.

In the end, Yuto did not see him that day.

Instead, a short message arrived on the screen.

“Message from nearby player  
Sender: WindArc  
Message:  
‘I read your report. The daily movement route perspective is really helpful.  
Would you like to look at a slightly larger place together someday?’”

“…Together.”

It was the first moment he had received a concrete proposal for cooperation.

A larger place.

Probably a corporate facility, or a wide area managed by the city.

A place that one person could not possibly cover on foot.

“It still feels a little early for me…”

At the same time, he also felt that it might be time to start looking at such places.

For now, he was operating at the living-zone level.

But someday, he would need to reach toward the boundary between living zones and corporate zones.

WindArc was at that entrance.

Yuto took one deep breath and wrote a reply.

“Reply:  
‘Thank you for reading the report.  
Going outside my living range is a little scary,  
but if the timing works, I would like to look together.’”

When he pressed send, the phone vibrated softly.

“Emergence of cooperative relationship detected  
Expected cooperative bonus: Slight increase”

“What is an expected cooperative bonus…?”

As always, the system was merciless.

It tried to handle even changes in human relationships as numbers.

But he did not feel bad about it.

The Cooling Credit he earned that day from the convenience store, street tree, and riverside actions totaled 0.038 CC.

“Today earned: 0.038 CC  
Cumulative total: 0.289 CC”

“0.3 is coming into sight.”

0.289 CC cumulative.

Still small.

But it was definitely continuing to increase.

At the end of his notebook, Yuto added one line for the day.

“Today's observations:  
- Logs are not only mine  
- Others apply them, improve them, and the results return as numbers  
- The possibility of moving together with someone has become a little more real”

It was not yet enough to call it a team.

But his unseen companions were slowly becoming visible people.
