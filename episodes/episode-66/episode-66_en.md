# Episode 66 — Expanding a Small Place

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 66 begins just before the alternative-operation demonstration at Point D ends, when workers start flowing in from Rest Area No. 2.  
If the small refuge space suddenly fills up, Yuto's idea of “reducing while preserving” may collapse. This episode follows an improvised expansion operation designed to receive more people without overloading the limited equipment.

---

## Main Story

『Point D: Rapid increase in users predicted』  
『Current alternative operation may exceed capacity』

North Seventh Wharf, temporary refuge space near the residential district.

Yuto looked toward the port.

Workers in helmets were walking toward him.

One.  
Two.  
Five.

And more were coming behind them.

More than ten.

“People from Rest Area No. 2...”

There were already people under the roof.

Elderly women.  
A mother with a child.  
A man returning from shopping.  
A worker on his way back from the port.

The benches were full.

A small line had formed again at the water dispenser.

One mist nozzle near the entrance was running at 30 percent for the child.

If any more people came—

The air would stop moving.  
Water would run short.  
The roofed area would become crowded.  
Someone would be pushed back into the sun.

“Yuto!”

Haru came running from the port side.

“Rest Area No. 2 lost too much cooling output.”

“Because of HEATWAVE-3's reduction?”

“No.  
Not the reduction itself.  
The outdoor unit at Rest Area No. 2—the place they were sending people to—temporarily shut down.”

“A failure?”

“Not sure yet.  
But the workers lost their place to rest.”

Yuto's phone vibrated.

『Point A / Rest Area No. 2  
Cooling supply decrease detected』

『Predicted inflow to Point D: +14 people』

“Fourteen...”

With the people already there, the space under the roof would clearly be over capacity.

MIST CROW looked at his tablet.

“The demonstration is over.”

『Point D / Alternative Operation Demonstration  
Time limit: 00:00:00』

Yuto had no time to wait for the result.

“What is the ruling?”

“Not out yet.”

“We can't wait.”

Yuto said it flatly.

“People are coming here now.”

“The demonstration time is over.  
Anything after this is outside the evaluation.”

“I don't care.”

Yuto stepped out from under the roof.

The workers approached with tired faces.

“We heard we could rest here.”

“Air conditioning at Rest Area No. 2 stopped.”

“Can we get water?”

Their voices overlapped.

Yuto took one deep breath.

“Yes.”

Then he spoke clearly.

“But the roof alone isn't enough.  
We're going to expand the space now.”

“Expand it?” Haru asked.

Yuto looked around the refuge space.

The roof.  
The benches.  
The mist near the entrance.  
A narrow walkway toward the residential district.  
And beside it, an unused bicycle parking area.

The bicycle area had no roof.

But sea breeze flowed through it.

And a nearby hedge would cast shade there later in the day.

“Haru, can we bring people into that bicycle area?”

“There is no roof.”

“I know.  
We separate people who absolutely need shade from people who can manage if there is airflow.”

“You're classifying them?”

“We let them choose.”

Yuto turned toward the workers.

“Anyone feeling unwell, people with children, and elderly visitors—please use the roofed area.  
If you only need a short break, please use the side where the wind passes through.”

“This side is fine for me,” one worker said immediately.

“Sometimes wind feels better after working outside.”

“If there's water, I can wait a little.”

Another worker agreed.

Yuto felt relieved.

“Thank you.  
We'll split the water support into two areas.”

There was only one water dispenser.

But the insulated bag Haru had brought still contained cooling towels.

“Haru, move the insulated bag to the bicycle area.”

“Got it.”

“What about the mist?” MIST CROW asked.

“If we stop the entrance nozzle, the child...”

“The child can use towels and cold packs deeper under the roof.  
Can we redirect the mist toward the bicycle area where the wind moves through?”

Yuto checked the nozzle angle.

It was independently controlled.

The pipe itself was fixed, but the spray direction looked adjustable.

“Can we do it?”

Haru came closer.

“Just changing the angle, yes.  
But if we send it there, Point D's 61 percent load reduction will fall apart.”

“People are there now,” Yuto replied.

“If the number of people who need it changes, the calculation changes too.”

MIST CROW's eyes shifted slightly.

“I don't know how the evaluation bureau will judge that.”

“They can judge it afterward.”

Yuto opened the mist-control terminal.

『Point D / Local Cooling』  
『Output: 30%』  
『Direction: Roof entrance side』

“I'll change the direction.”

『Wind compensation required when changing direction』

Yuto watched the sea breeze.

If the mist blew straight ahead, it would drift toward the walkway.

Slightly diagonal.

Toward the deeper part of the bicycle space.

“Haru, hold this side.”

“Like this?”

“A little more left.”

“Left from whose perspective?”

“Toward the sea!”

“Say that from the start!”

Haru locked the nozzle into position.

Click.

『Local cooling direction change complete』

Hissss...

A weak mist drifted toward the bicycle area.

It was not as cool as under the roof.

But the breeze carried the cooled air across the necks of the standing workers.

“Oh.”

“This helps.”

One worker smiled.

“Nice that it doesn't soak you.”

Yuto immediately pulled out a piece of cardboard.

『Wind Rest Side』  
『If you feel unwell, use the roofed area』  
『Water and towels here』

Soma shouted over the comms.

『Yuto! I'm heading to D now!  
People from the residential side are giving up seats to port workers.  
But we're short on water!』

“Any vending machines nearby?”

『Yes, but not much is cold!』

“Ice?”

『The port first-aid station says they can send more!』

“Please do!”

『Got it!』

Soma's voice cut off.

Meanwhile, Yuto watched the flow under the roof.

The mother with the child.  
The elderly woman.  
A worker who looked pale.

People who felt worse gathered beneath the roof, while people who recovered a little moved to the wind-rest side.

People moved.

And the place became, just a little, larger.

“Yuto.”

MIST CROW approached.

“Your plan is no longer a 67 percent reduction.”

“I know.”

“Including the local mist, the load reduction has fallen to 48 percent.”

“I know.”

“The entire port only had 11 percent spare cooling capacity to begin with.  
If you keep expanding Point D, another location will be affected.”

“Then we won't make Point D carry this alone.”

Yuto answered without hesitation.

“We restore Rest Area No. 2 as quickly as possible.  
This is temporary intake until then.”

“Rest Area No. 2 belongs to HEATWAVE-3's sector.”

“This isn't the time to talk about sectors.”

Yuto's voice became stronger.

“People are coming from there now.  
If this place shuts down too, where do they go next?”

MIST CROW did not answer.

The observation drone above them dropped slightly lower.

『Point D / Emergency user increase detected』  
『Evaluating extension measures following alternative-operation demonstration』

“Evaluating...”

A message from FROST-9 arrived on Yuto's phone.

『The increase in users was not anticipated.  
Restoring Point D output will affect the overall North Seventh Wharf plan.』

Yuto replied at once.

『Rest Area No. 2 stopped.  
Please look at the failure of the destination, not only at Point D's load.』

A few seconds later:

『FROST-9:  
Confirming with HEATWAVE-3.』

Then a comm link opened.

『HEATWAVE-3:  
The cooling shutdown at Rest Area No. 2 is separate from the reduction plan.  
The outdoor unit entered protective shutdown. Cause under investigation.』

“Protective shutdown?” Yuto said.

『HEATWAVE-3:  
A sudden rise in outside temperature pushed the heat-exchange side beyond its limit.  
Minimum twenty minutes to restore.』

“Twenty minutes...”

Could Point D keep taking people for twenty minutes?

The roofed area and wind-rest side.

Water.  
Towels.  
Weak mist.

It was working for now.

But if even more people came—

At that moment, Soma arrived carrying an insulated bag.

“More ice!”

A first-aid worker followed him with a cart.

“Ten more cooling towels!”

“Thank you!”

Yuto added a temporary table on the wind-rest side.

Cooling towels.  
Ice.  
Water.  
Paper cups.

Little by little, the temporary rest zone took shape.

One worker looked at the benches and said,

“This place started as just a little refuge spot.”

“Now it's almost like a port rest station.”

Yuto gave a small smile.

“Only temporarily.”

“Temporary still helps.”

Yuto's phone vibrated at those words.

『Point D / Response to rapid user increase』  
『On-site adaptation log confirmed』

『Additional evaluation started』

The screen changed.

『Pre-observation pass: Re-evaluation complete』

Yuto held his breath.

『Collaborative operation intervention confirmed』  
『Rule violation: None』

“...None?”

More text appeared.

『Observation pass converted to Collaborative Field Demonstration Pass』

『Formal participation qualification: Pending』  
『Discretionary participation qualification: Under review』

“Did it pass...?”

MIST CROW looked at his tablet.

“Not yet.  
The next evaluation is whether you can maintain this intake for twenty minutes.”

『Point D / Emergency Expanded Operation』  
『Until Rest Area No. 2 restoration: 00:19:46』

New conditions appeared on Yuto's phone.

『Conditions:  
・Maintain user safety  
・Keep impact on total port load within 5%  
・Emergency exit rate: 0%  
・Safe transition after Rest Area No. 2 restoration』

“A twenty-minute defense,” Haru said.

“This is getting interesting.”

“This is not the time to enjoy it.”

“Sometimes enjoying it a little helps you move.”

Yuto nearly laughed.

But at that exact moment—

The massive crane in the center of North Seventh Wharf sounded a low alarm.

WOOOOON.

The port-wide display boards turned red.

『Wide-area cooling load: Warning threshold reached』  
『Spare capacity: 11% → 7%』

MIST CROW's expression changed.

“...This isn't just Point D anymore.”

An emergency message came from FROST-9.

『Abnormality detected at North Seventh Wharf main cooling tower.  
Output restrictions may be imposed on all points.』

In front of Yuto, the weak local mist trembled once.

Hissss...

Then its sound weakened slightly.

『Point D / Local cooling  
External output restriction forecast detected』

A twenty-minute defense.

But before it could even begin, the whole of North Seventh Wharf might stop cooling.

## Afterword

Episode 66 follows the sudden inflow of workers into Point D after Rest Area No. 2 loses cooling, forcing Yuto's alternative-operation plan to face an unexpected surge in users.

Key points in this episode:

- Rest Area No. 2 did not stop because HEATWAVE-3's reduction plan failed; its outdoor unit entered protective shutdown after outside temperature rose too sharply.
- Point D was forecast to receive 14 additional people, far beyond the roofed area's capacity.
- Yuto improvised an expansion by prioritizing the roofed area for those who needed shade most while turning an unused bicycle area into a “wind rest side.”
- One mist nozzle was redirected and operated at 30 percent to provide local cooling to the bicycle area.
- Haru, Soma, first-aid staff, and the users themselves helped build a working flow of water, cooling towels, ice, and rest zones.
- FROST-9 ruled that collaborative operation was not a violation and converted Yuto's pass into a “Collaborative Field Demonstration Pass.”
- The new emergency expanded-operation conditions require user safety, less than 5 percent impact on total port load, a 0 percent emergency exit rate, and safe transition after Rest Area No. 2 is restored.
- Finally, a fault occurred at the main cooling tower, reducing wide-area spare cooling capacity from 11 percent to 7 percent.

Yuto chose not only to preserve the reduced Point D but to “expand the place itself” so it could absorb the sudden increase in users.  
However, using local mist and accommodating more people makes the overall port cooling situation even tighter.

Next episode begins the twenty-minute defense of Point D under the threat of a main cooling tower fault and possible wide-area output restrictions.  
Will Yuto wait for Rest Area No. 2 to recover, or reduce Point D further to protect the entire wharf? For the first time, he must weigh the people directly in front of him against the needs of North Seventh Wharf as a whole.
