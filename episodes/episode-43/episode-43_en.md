# Episode 43: Night Road Bonus and a Small Incident

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 43 is the episode where Yuto actually goes after the new earning opportunity introduced in Episode 42: the nighttime “route home” flow.  
In an atmosphere completely different from daytime, a small improvement unexpectedly calls forth a “small incident,” and the episode ends with a hook that makes the reader want to know what comes next.

---

## Main Text

Friday evening.  
Soma Yuto returned to the station-front plaza a little later than usual.

The daytime bustle had settled down.  
The mist had also become more modest,  
and people who seemed to be on their way home from work were sitting here and there on the benches.

The notification he had seen at the end of the previous day still would not leave his mind.

“Candidate target:  
Nighttime ‘route home’ flow”

“…What exactly is a night route?”

Just as Yuto muttered that, WindArc appeared.

“You came.”

“I came.  
So what exactly am I supposed to do with this nighttime ‘route home’ thing?”

“To put it simply,  
it means deciding where people can take one more breath before they get home.”

“How is that different from the daytime benches?”

“During the day, heat countermeasures are what stand out.  
At night, resetting fatigue becomes stronger.  
In other words, the meaning of a place to sit changes slightly.”

Yuto pictured the road leading from the station gates toward his apartment.

The station-front plaza.  
The edge of the shopping street.  
The corner where people waited at the traffic light.  
The space in front of the convenience store.

“Now that you mention it, on the way home, where you stop matters.”

“Exactly.  
What the operations side is seeing now is data suggesting that if there is a place where people can take one more breath on the way home, their usage is more likely to continue the next day too.”

“I see…”

After a brief silence, Yuto gave a small laugh.

“So, if I make a useful improvement here, there is a chance I can earn CC again.”

“Yes.  
And there still aren’t many players working on nighttime improvements.”

“So there are fewer rivals.”

“That is one way to put it, yes.”

Yuto traced the route home with his eyes from the plaza.

The flow of people was sparser than in the daytime,  
but the steps of each person passing by seemed a little heavier.  
People on their way home from work.  
Parents returning after picking up children from nursery school.  
People carrying shopping bags.

“…Where to let them take one breath, huh.”

At that moment, what entered Yuto’s view was a bench at the edge of the station-front plaza that felt slightly overlooked.  
It was just barely outside the range of the mist.  
During the day, almost no one used it.  
At night, someone sat there only occasionally.

“First, that one.”

Yuto headed toward it.

Beside the bench stood a small street tree.  
Under it hung a single guidance tag that was almost unnoticeable.

“Cooling Commons Use Area”

“The letters are tiny…”

Yuto gave a wry smile and called over a staff member.

“Could we adjust this bench a little for nighttime use?”

“For nighttime?”

“Like a ‘last breath before going home’ point.”

The staff member widened their eyes, then immediately nodded with interest.

“That sounds interesting.  
Specifically?”

“First, the direction of the mist.  
Then a small light.  
And after that… the wording.”

Yuto opened the notebook he had with him.

“Draft: one sentence for a night bench  
・This is a place to take a breath before going home.  
・It is free.  
・No application is required.  
・This is a ‘good work today’ area.”

“That last line is a little embarrassing.”

The staff member laughed.

“But at night, that might be just right.”

“Then let’s try it.”

Between dusk and night, the direction of the mist and the lighting were adjusted.  
A small glow was placed at the foot of the bench.  
The mist was weakened so it only drifted softly.  
The guidance tag was replaced with a new message.

“This is a place to take a breath before going home.  
It is free.  
No application is required.  
This is a ‘good work today’ area.”

The first person to react was a man in a suit on his way home from work.  
His steps, which had almost passed by, caught on the guidance text.  
He stopped, read the words, and smiled.

“…Good work today, huh.”

Then he sat down on the bench.

For a while he sat there silently,  
looked at his phone,  
looked up at the sky,  
and breathed out softly.

“This isn’t bad.”

Yuto watched that from a short distance away.

“…It’s reaching him properly.”

Next came a parent and child returning from nursery school.  
The parent was pushing a bicycle with the child seated on it when they saw the guidance text and stopped.

“Hey, shall we sit for a little bit?”

“Is it okay?”

“Apparently it’s a ‘good work today’ area.”

The parent and child sat side by side.  
The child reached out toward the mist.  
The parent let the strength leave their shoulders and looked up at the sky.

That sight created a quiet pause within the nighttime route home.

About twenty minutes later, Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“A change has been detected in the nighttime experience log.  
This may be recognized as an effective stay point on the route home.”

“…‘May be,’ huh.  
So it isn’t confirmed yet.”

Just as Yuto muttered that—  
a small voice rose from a little distance away.

“Excuse me!”

He turned around and saw a young woman trying to leave the bench in a hurry.  
At her feet were a convenience store bag and a spilled bottled drink.

“Oh no, I spilled it…!”

Some of the drink had splashed onto the light at the foot of the bench.

A staff member immediately ran over.

“It’s all right.  
We’ll wipe it up, so please feel free to stay seated.”

“But the electricity…”

“No need to worry.  
It’s waterproof.”

Yuto felt a chill for a moment, then quickly let out a breath of relief.

“…A small incident happened.”

“Yes.  
But this will also remain in the log.”

WindArc briefly showed him the terminal screen.

“Nighttime event record:  
・Small trouble occurred during a stay at the route bench  
・Staff response allowed the stay to continue  
・User sense of reassurance was maintained”

“So this is also part of the ‘experience.’”

“Yes.  
In fact, how a site responds when trouble happens is also evaluated.”

After a while, another notification arrived.

“Nighttime route home improvement recognized.  
Additional Cooling Credit granted.  
+0.007 CC”

“All right…!”

Yuto’s total jumped just slightly again.

“Earned today: 0.012 CC  
Total: 0.783 CC”

“That’s a little more than during the day.”

Just as Yuto grinned, WindArc added one more line.

“That may not be all.”

“What?”

“The operations side is considering a plan to show the next area candidate early to players who improve nighttime routes.”

“The next area?”

“Yes.  
It is not official yet, but…  
a temporary label has already appeared.”

WindArc showed Yuto the screen on the terminal.

“Next candidate:  
‘The town outskirts on the weekend’”

“…The outskirts?”

Yuto involuntarily caught his breath.

Not the station front, not the shopping street, not the city office.  
A place where support had hardly been discussed until now.  
An area like a boundary line.

“We’re going that far…?”

The night wind grew a little stronger, and the mist trembled faintly.  
Inside Yuto, only the words “the town outskirts on the weekend” remained heavily.

## Afterword

Episode 43 focused on Yuto opening up the nighttime route home, which feels completely different from daytime, through his own improvement.  
For a more entertainment-oriented structure, I was conscious of the following development.

- Setup: the new earning opportunity of the nighttime route home is presented  
- Development: the night bench’s wording, mist, and lighting are adjusted to change the experience  
- Turn: a small trouble occurs when someone spills a drink, and that incident is included in the “experience log”  
- Ending: the night bench succeeds, and the next candidate, “the town outskirts on the weekend,” becomes a strong hook

Yuto changes the nighttime atmosphere with one sentence:  
- “This is a ‘good work today’ area,”  
- steadily increases his Cooling Credit as a result,  
- and receives the name of a slightly unsettling and intriguing next-stage candidate: the outskirts of town.

In the next episode, we can depict what kind of place “the town outskirts on the weekend” is,  
why it becomes a candidate for support,  
and how Yuto becomes involved there, how much he can earn, or whether he ends up in a dangerous situation.
