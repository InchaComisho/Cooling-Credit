# Episode 51: The Too-Cold Waiting Station

[← Top / README](../../README.md)

Episode 51 follows the anonymous message that arrived at the end of Episode 50, as Yuto and the others head to the night delivery waiting station.  
The truth behind the ominous phrase “too cold,” and the conditions for the additional 0.100 CC, gradually become clear. This episode emphasizes the tension unique to a nighttime work site and sets up the next step.

---

## Main Text

Seven in the evening.

The sky at the edge of town was still slightly bright.

But once they entered the warehouse district, the air felt different from daytime.  
The low engine sounds of large trucks.  
The sound of carts carrying packages.  
A reversing buzzer sounding in the distance.

Beep, beep, beep.

Soma Yuto walked while looking at the map on his phone.

“Night Delivery Waiting Station”

Their destination was at the far end of the warehouse district.  
It was a one-story waiting facility beside an old delivery center.

The anonymous message that had arrived during the day still remained in his mind.

“That place is ‘too cold.’”  
“If you stop it the wrong way, tonight’s deliveries will stop.”

“Too cold, huh…”

The waiting station came into view.

Gray walls.  
A large automatic door.  
A small side entrance that seemed to be for delivery workers.  
The lights were on.

Above the entrance, there was an old signboard.

“Delivery Waiting Station  
Rest / Roll Call / Night Waiting”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Limited Challenge: Second Condition  
Confirm the load at the night delivery waiting station”

“Additional estimated reward: 0.100 CC”

“Caution: This facility is currently in use”

“Here it is.”

Haru said beside him.

Today, in addition to his black cap, he was wearing a reflective vest.

“Safety first?”

“There are many vehicles in a warehouse district.  
Not being visible is dangerous.”

“That is very reasonable.”

A little behind them were Nagi and Sōma.

Nagi had a thermometer and a small light.  
Sōma was quieter than before.

“I’ll say this first.”

Sōma said.

“I won’t enter a machine room on my own today.  
I won’t touch any place without permission.”

“You’ve grown.”

When Yuto said that, Sōma grimaced slightly.

“I’m here so one failure ends as one failure.”

“Good answer.”

Haru said briefly.

The four of them headed to the entrance of the waiting station.

Cold air was leaking from inside.

Outside was a summer night.  
Humid heat still remained.

And yet, only in front of the entrance, it felt a little cold.

“…It really is cold.”

Yuto rubbed his arms.

The automatic door opened.

Inside were people who seemed to be delivery workers.

A man in uniform.  
A woman checking shipping labels.  
Someone sitting in a chair and drinking canned coffee.  
Someone taking a nap by the wall.

And all of them were wearing long-sleeved jackets.

“It’s cold today.”

“Isn’t the air conditioning too strong again?”

“But if we turn it off, the warehouse side gets hot next.”

Small complaints could be heard here and there.

Yuto understood the meaning of the anonymous message.

“Too cold means… the cooling is running so much that the people in the waiting station are cold.”

Haru looked at the display on the wall.

“Room temperature: 18.2°C”  
“Humidity: 43%”  
“Shared cooling line: connected”

“Eighteen degrees…”

Nagi checked her thermometer.

“The difference from the outside air is too large.  
It is low for resting, and if people are going in and out often, it burdens the body too.”

“But why is it set like this?”

Yuto said.

A delivery worker nearby looked at them.

“Haven’t seen you before. Are you here for a tour?”

“Ah, sorry.  
We came for a Cooling Commons limited investigation… to check the shared cooling line.”

The delivery worker laughed with a tired face.

“Investigation?  
Then this is perfect. Do something about the air conditioning.”

“So it really is a problem?”

“It’s cold.  
But if we stop it, then the temporary package storage space gets hot.”

“The package storage?”

“This building uses the same line for the waiting station and the temporary package storage.  
If you lower one side, it affects the other.”

Yuto looked at the door in the back of the waiting station.

Beyond it seemed to be the temporary package storage space.

“So it’s being cooled for the packages.”

“Some goods are sensitive to temperature.  
They stay here until they are handed off for night deliveries.”

“So even if the waiting station is too cold, it can’t simply be turned down…”

At that moment, Haru’s terminal vibrated.

“Shared cooling line: load status detected”  
“Night delivery waiting station: high-output operation”

Then a notification appeared on Yuto’s screen as well.

“Begin on-site confirmation?”

“Begin”  
“Obtain user permission”

“We choose obtain permission.”

Yuto pressed the second option without hesitation.

Haru seemed to have made the same choice.

Nagi was already looking for someone who seemed to be responsible for the facility.

“There is someone we can talk to.  
That person is probably the night-shift supervisor.”

A woman was operating a tablet by the wall.

She looked to be in her forties.  
Blue work clothes.  
An armband for supervisors on her arm.

Nagi approached her.

“Excuse me.  
We came to confirm the shared cooling line.  
Could we speak with you for a moment?”

The woman looked at the four of them in order.

“Who gave you permission to enter?”

“From the Cooling Commons regional observation team, as a limited challenge—”

“A limited challenge?”

The woman’s expression changed.

“This place is not a game stage.”

The air grew heavy at once.

Yuto immediately bowed his head.

“I’m sorry.  
That isn’t how we meant to come here.”

“But you do get a reward, don’t you?”

The woman looked at Yuto’s phone screen.

There was a notification he had forgotten to close.

“Additional estimated reward: 0.100 CC”

Yuto found himself at a loss for words.

He could not deny it.

He had come here aiming for the reward.

But the delivery workers in front of him were resting so they could support the work of the night.

“…Yes.”

Yuto answered honestly.

“I want to earn CC.  
That is why I came here.”

Haru glanced sideways at him.

Nagi and Sōma said nothing.

“But I do not intend to change something on my own and take the reward while everyone here stays cold.  
If we are going to change the cooling, I want to first look for a way together that does not affect your work.”

The woman watched Yuto for a while.

Then she let out a short breath.

“Name?”

“Soma Yuto.”

“I’m Katase. Night-shift site supervisor.”

Katase closed her tablet.

“We are aware of the overcooling problem too.  
But whoever changes the settings will be held responsible.  
If packages are damaged, that is a problem. If deliveries are delayed, that is even worse.”

“I understand.”

“If you understand, then first look at the site.”

Katase opened the door in the back.

Behind the waiting station was the temporary storage space.

Small refrigerated containers.  
Boxes with barcodes.  
Packages brought in from loading platforms.  
And thick cooling pipes running along the wall.

Cold air was necessary here.

“What we keep here is not only food.”

Katase said.

“Medical cooling packs. Temperature-controlled specimen transport containers.  
Some things that move at night are precisely the kind of cargo that cannot be delayed.”

Yuto could no longer casually say, “Just weaken the air conditioning.”

“To make the waiting station warmer, we need to separate it from the cooling here.”

“Yes.  
But the equipment for separating them has been faulty for years.”

Haru looked at the piping.

“It’s similar to the old waiting room’s shared line.”

“Not just similar.”

Katase said in a low voice.

“This waiting station is a relay point for the shared cooling line.”

Yuto and the others’ expressions changed.

“A relay point…?”

“If this place is stopped carelessly, the old waiting room, the venue, and other bases will all be affected.  
That is why no one has wanted to touch it.”

Yuto’s phone vibrated.

“Hidden information confirmed”  
“Night delivery waiting station: shared cooling line relay base”

“Additional evaluation condition updated”  
“Present a plan that balances waiting-station comfort and logistics cooling”

“So it isn’t just checking it.”

Sōma murmured.

“It wants us to present a balancing plan.”

Nagi looked at the door between the waiting station and the temporary storage space.

“The air is mixing too much.  
If there were some kind of partition or route control here, we might be able to adjust only the waiting-station side.”

“Without stopping the equipment?”

“Probably.”

Just then, the sound of a truck braking echoed outside.

Screeech…

The air in the waiting station suddenly became busy.

Katase checked her terminal.

“It’s early.  
A large shipment has arrived.”

“What time was that shipment supposed to come?”

“It was supposed to be at nine.”

The wall clock still read seven twenty.

Katase’s face tightened.

“This is strange.  
And it is a shipment with a lot of cargo requiring cooling.”

A notification sound rang.

This time, it was not from Yuto’s terminal.

It was the warning sound of the whole facility.

“Shared cooling line: sudden demand increase detected”  
“Relay base surplus cooling capacity: 9% remaining”

Haru looked at the display.

“The load is rising again.”

Nagi said.

“The waiting station is cold.  
But the packages need even more cooling.  
If that large shipment enters like this, the line may not hold.”

Katase had the expression of someone being forced to make a decision.

At that moment, another anonymous message arrived on Yuto’s phone.

“Anonymous:  
Look above the entrance.  
The old ‘air separation damper’ is still there.”

Yuto looked up.

Above the door connecting the waiting station and the temporary storage space.

There was a dust-covered metal lever.

The label had almost faded away.

“Air Separation  
Manual Switch”

Yuto held his breath.

There was very little time before the large shipment arrived.

And the lever had a red seal tag attached to it.

“Operation prohibited except in emergencies”

## Afterword

Episode 51 revealed that the night delivery waiting station was not simply a “too-cold rest area,” but an important relay base supporting the shared cooling line.

The key points this time were as follows.

- The waiting station is too cold at 18.2°C, while the neighboring temporary storage space handles packages that require temperature control
- Yuto does not hide that he is aiming for the 0.100 CC reward, and on that basis shows a willingness to cooperate with the site
- Through the guidance of Katase, the night-shift supervisor, we learn why the cooling of the waiting station and the cooling for logistics cannot be easily separated
- Because a large shipment arrives earlier than planned, the shared cooling line’s surplus capacity drops to 9%
- An “air separation damper” that may be able to separate the waiting station from the temporary storage space is found, but it bears a sealed warning forbidding operation

For Yuto, this night’s contest cannot be won simply by making a place “comfortable” as before.  
He must solve the coldness of the waiting station without breaking any of the following: the health of the delivery workers, temperature-controlled cargo, delivery schedules, and the stability of the shared cooling line.

In the next episode, the focus will be who operates the sealed air separation damper, and under what responsibility.  
If they make the wrong choice before the large shipment arrives, not only the cooling line, but the night’s deliveries themselves may stop.
